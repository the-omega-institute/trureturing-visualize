ARGUED卷宗里的计算与论证（未形式化）：同一组速率下，多起点求根加 50 位牛顿精化找到第三个物理稳态 Z ≈ (0.43995, 0.50554, 0.0050850, 0.044208, …)；在原文数值检验的最大阶 C = 8，速率在 [10⁻³, 10³] 中对数均匀抽取时，800 个随机系统里有 4 个有两个或三个物理稳态，C = 6、7 各 1,500 个系统没有发现多解。机理是过程的循环（例如 4 + 4 → 6 → 3 + 5 → 6）：C ≥ 6 时稳态方程定不住通量，非细致平衡态带着循环通量，速率跨几个数量级时同一个多项式方程组可以有几个正解。Computations and arguments in the dossier (not formalized): for the same rates, multistart root finding with 50-digit Newton refinement finds a third physical steady state Z ≈ (0.43995, 0.50554, 0.0050850, 0.044208, …); at C = 8, the largest order of the paper's tests, rates drawn log-uniformly from [10⁻³, 10³] give two or three physical steady states in 4 of 800 random systems, and none of 1,500 systems each at C = 6 and 7 did. The mechanism is the cycles among the processes (for example 4 + 4 → 6 → 3 + 5 → 6): for C ≥ 6 the steady-state equations leave the fluxes undetermined, a non-detailed-balance state carries circulating fluxes, and with rates spread over several orders of magnitude one polynomial system can have several positive solutions.
ARGUED页面自己的论证（未形式化，未经评审）：Y 是 X 与 Z 两个吸引域之间的鞍点——沿它的正特征方向 ±v 出发的两条轨道分别落到 X 和 Z，网格起点也按 Q₃ 的大小分成两群。所以同一组速率下，动力学停在哪个稳态取决于从哪里出发。The page's own argument (not formalized, not reviewed): Y is the saddle between the basins of X and Z — the two trajectories leaving along its positive eigendirection ±v land at X and at Z, and the grid starts split into two groups by the size of Q₃. So with the same rates, which steady state the dynamics reaches depends on where it starts.

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

把二维颗粒堆积看成由多边形“胞”拼成的网，Q_k 是 k 边胞所占的比例（k = 3 … C）。堆积在剪切下重排时，i 阶与 j 阶的两个胞可以合并成一个 i + j − 2 阶胞，也可以反过来分开；速率分别是 p 与 q，净通量 η = p Q_i Q_j − q Q_{i+j−2}。Myhill 与 Blumenfeld 写出 Q 的演化方程，猜测：只要速率是正常数，物理稳态（每个 Q_k > 0，总和为 1）就只有一个。

  1. 答案。不成立。C = 10 时，对一组具体的正速率，X = (3,2,5,1,2,1,1,1)/16 与 Y = (12,6,5,4,4,1,1,1)/34 都是物理稳态，Lean 用精确有理数验证了全部 8 行方程。
  2. 为什么原文的定理不受影响。原文证明的是：细致平衡态（每个 η 都为 0）存在时它唯一。这组速率根本没有细致平衡态：θ = p/q 满足 θ₃₃θ₄₄ = 100/423，而 θ₃₄θ₃₅ = 2/9。X、Y 处的 16 个净通量都不为 0，只是每一阶的流入与流出恰好抵消。
  3. 动力学选哪一个。页面从 Y 沿它唯一的不稳定方向推一点点：一侧落到 X，另一侧落到第三个稳态 Z。X 与 Z 都吸引附近的轨道，Y 是它们之间的鞍点。
  4. 还没有回答的。原文测试的速率相差不超过 30 倍；这里的速率跨了四个多数量级。在那个范围内是否也会有多个稳态，卷宗列为未决。

原文：问题卷宗 · GranularCellOrderSteadyStateRefutation.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · J. Phys. A 论文 · arXiv:2306.10526

Think of a two-dimensional granular packing as a network of polygonal cells, and let Q_k be the fraction of cells with k sides (k = 3 … C). As the packing rearranges under shear, two cells of orders i and j can merge into one of order i + j − 2, or split back; the rates are p and q, and the net flux is η = p Q_i Q_j − q Q_{i+j−2}. Myhill and Blumenfeld write the evolution equations for Q and conjecture that for positive constant rates there is only one physical steady state (every Q_k > 0, summing to 1).

  1. The answer. It fails. For C = 10 and one explicit set of positive rates, X = (3,2,5,1,2,1,1,1)/16 and Y = (12,6,5,4,4,1,1,1)/34 are both physical steady states; Lean checks all eight equations in exact rationals.
  2. Why the paper's theorems stand. The paper proves that a detailed-balance steady state (every η zero) is unique when it exists. These rates have no detailed-balance state at all: with θ = p/q, θ₃₃θ₄₄ = 100/423 while θ₃₄θ₃₅ = 2/9. At X and Y all sixteen net fluxes are nonzero; only the inflow and outflow of each order cancel.
  3. Which one the dynamics picks. The page pushes Y slightly along its single unstable direction: one side lands at X, the other at a third steady state Z. X and Z both attract nearby trajectories, and Y is the saddle between them.
  4. What is still open. The paper tested rates within a factor of 30 of each other; the rates here span more than four orders of magnitude. Whether several steady states can occur within that range is open in the dossier.
