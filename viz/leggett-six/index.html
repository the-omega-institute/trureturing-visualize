ARGUED本页的论证（未形式化、未经评审）：把关联乘以 t。0 ≤ t ≤ 1 时成对表合法，6 边形、子集多边形与三时刻 LG 在 t = 0 与 t = 1 成立，而它们关于 t 是仿射的，所以对整段都成立。若有联合分布，Σμ W² = bᵀ(I + tC)b = 9 − 10t，而 W² ≥ 1、Σμ = 1，所以 t > 0.8 时不存在；I + tC 的特征值是 1 ± t√5/2 与 1，到 t = 2/√5 ≈ 0.894 都半正定。于是 0.8 < t ≤ 2/√5 时，这些条件加上半正定仍然不足以保证联合分布。The page’s argument (not formalized, not reviewed): scale the correlations by t. For 0 ≤ t ≤ 1 the pair tables are valid, and the 6-gon, the subset polygons and the three-time LG inequalities hold at t = 0 and t = 1; they are affine in t, so they hold on the whole interval. A joint law would give Σμ W² = bᵀ(I + tC)b = 9 − 10t, while W² ≥ 1 and Σμ = 1, so none exists for t > 0.8; I + tC has eigenvalues 1 ± t√5/2 and 1, so it stays positive semidefinite up to t = 2/√5 ≈ 0.894. Hence for 0.8 < t ≤ 2/√5 these conditions together with positive semidefiniteness still do not guarantee a joint law.
OPEN定理之外：n ≥ 6 时完整的充分条件；较小 n 的一般非对称情形；其他时间量子测量模型下的可实现性。“加上半正定是否足够”在卷宗里仍是未决，上面本页的论证没有经过形式化或评审。Outside the theorem: a complete sufficient condition for n ≥ 6; the general nonsymmetric cases for smaller n; realizability under other temporal quantum measurement models. “Does adding positive semidefiniteness suffice?” is still open in the dossier; the page’s argument above is neither formalized nor reviewed.

Leggett–Garg 检验在几个时刻测量同一个二值量 Q = ±1，但每次只测两个时刻，得到成对的概率表。经典（宏观实在论）的解释要求存在一个联合分布，把全部成对表作为边缘。Fine 定理在少数时刻给出这种存在性的不等式刻画；Halliwell 与 Mawby 猜想，对任意 n，完整的 n 边形不等式加上全部三时刻 LG 不等式就够了。本库在 Lean 里证明 n = 6 时不够。

  1. 例子。时刻 6 与时刻 1–4 正相关（1/2），与时刻 5 负相关（−1/2），其余时刻两两无关（右下图）。所有不等式都成立；最紧的三时刻 LG 不等式恰好取等号。
  2. 为什么没有联合分布。取 W = x₁ + x₂ + x₃ + x₄ − x₅ − 2x₆。系数和是奇数，所以 W 总是奇数，W² ≥ 1（三维视图的青柱全在青框之上）。若有联合分布，W² 的平均只取决于成对关联：bᵀ(I + C)b = 9 + 2·(−5) = −1。平均不可能比每一项都小——琥珀框掉到了地板以下。
  3. 把关联缩小（页面的推论）。把关联乘以 t：W² 的平均变成 9 − 10t，t ≤ 0.8 时不低于 1，而页面在 t ≤ 0.8 给出一个真正的联合分布（琥珀柱）。t 在 0.8 到 1 之间，猜想的条件仍然成立，却没有联合分布。
  4. 半正定也不够（本页的论证）。二阶矩矩阵 I + tC 直到 t = 2/√5 ≈ 0.894 都半正定。卷宗把“再加上半正定是否足够”列为未决；0.8 < t ≤ 0.894 这一段按同样的论证给出反例。这一点没有形式化，也未经评审。

原文：问题卷宗 · LeggettGargSixTimeSufficiencyRefutation.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · Halliwell、Mawby，doi:10.1103/PhysRevA.100.042103 · arXiv:1906.04865

A Leggett–Garg test measures one two-valued quantity Q = ±1 at several times, but only two times per run, which gives pairwise probability tables. A classical (macrorealist) account requires a joint law with all the pair tables as its marginals. Fine’s theorem characterizes this by inequalities for a few times; Halliwell and Mawby conjectured that for every n the full n-gon inequality together with every three-time LG inequality suffices. This library proves in Lean that it does not at n = 6.

  1. The example. Time 6 is positively correlated with times 1–4 (1/2) and negatively with time 5 (−1/2); all other pairs are uncorrelated (lower right chart). Every inequality holds; the tightest three-time LG inequalities hold with equality.
  2. Why there is no joint law. Take W = x₁ + x₂ + x₃ + x₄ − x₅ − 2x₆. Its coefficients sum to an odd number, so W is always odd and W² ≥ 1 (in the 3D view every cyan bar rises above the cyan frame). A joint law would make the average of W² depend only on the pair correlations: bᵀ(I + C)b = 9 + 2·(−5) = −1. An average cannot be smaller than every term — the amber frame has sunk below the floor.
  3. Scaling the correlations (the page’s corollary). Multiply the correlations by t: the average of W² becomes 9 − 10t, at least 1 for t ≤ 0.8, and the page gives a genuine joint law for t ≤ 0.8 (amber bars). For t between 0.8 and 1 the conjecture’s conditions still hold, yet there is no joint law.
  4. Positive semidefiniteness does not suffice either (the page’s argument). The second-moment matrix I + tC is positive semidefinite up to t = 2/√5 ≈ 0.894. The dossier lists “does adding positive semidefiniteness suffice?” as open; the stretch 0.8 < t ≤ 0.894 gives counterexamples by the same argument. This is neither formalized nor reviewed.
