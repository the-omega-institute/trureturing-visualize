理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

AURIC FIB-ATOM 理论卷从一条很小的局部规则出发：三个位置排成一行（低端 x、中间 z、高端 y），中间与两端互斥（xz = yz = 0），两端可以同时选。合法组合恰有五种，理论卷记作 0（全空）、2（只选低端）、3（只选中间）、5（只选高端）、25（两端都选），数字是它们的“数量”读数。这个页面画出这五种组合围成的几何，以及平均值能看见什么、看不见什么。

  1. 替换树（理论卷）。两个原子 α、β，替换 α → β、β → ⟨β, α⟩，有序括号是来源的一部分。第 n 层 Tn+2 = ⟨Tn+1, Tn⟩，叶子数、α 与 β 的个数都是斐波那契数；按“α 记 2、β 记 3”读出的数量恰为 F(n + 3)。另一方面，长 n、没有两个相邻 1 的位串恰有 F(n + 2) 个（已冻结），n = 3 时这五个位串 000、100、010、001、101 正是五种模式。
  2. 金字塔（已冻结）。对五种组合取任意概率分布，平均占用 (X, Y, Z) 所在的点恰好铺满方底金字塔 {X, Y, Z ≥ 0，X + Z ≤ 1，Y + Z ≤ 1}：底面是单位正方形（Z = 0 的四种组合），顶点是“只选中间”。高度 Z 处的截面是边长 1 − Z 的正方形，所以体积 ∫(1 − Z)² dZ = 1/3，体积重心 (3/8, 3/8, 1/4)。这个凸包描述就是 Stanley 的 chain polytope 在三点栅栏上的特例。页面撒在单位立方体里的点约有三分之一落在金字塔里（蒙特卡罗，只是演示）。
  3. 隐藏纤维（理论卷，Fréchet 区间已冻结）。五个概率只有四个自由度，三个平均值定住三个，剩下的是“两端同时被选”的概率 κ。给定平均点，可行的分布是 p = (r − X − Y + κ, X − κ, Z, Y − κ, κ)（次序 0, 2, 3, 5, 25，r = 1 − Z），κ 的范围是 [max(0, X + Y − r), min(X, Y)]——两事件的 Fréchet 区间（已冻结）按 r 缩放。区间宽度为 min{X, Y, r − X, r − Y}：在四个三角侧面上为零（平均值定死分布），在底面内部为正。两个同均值分布只能相差 t·(1, −1, 0, −1, 1)，也就是正方形关系 v0 + v25 = v2 + v5。底面四格的行列式 Δ = p0p25 − p2p5 = rκ − XY 是中间空着时两端的协方差乘 r²，且 |Δ| ≤ r²/4；Δ = 0 的那一点 κ* = XY/r 是乘积补全（中间条件下两端独立）。均匀分布 p = (1/5, …, 1/5) 正好落在 κ* 上，却仍有整体协方差 XYZ/r = 1/25。
  4. 拼接（路径已冻结，奇环来自理论卷）。把很多小窗口串成一排：每个位置平均占用 t，相邻约束是 t + t ≤ 1。路径上只要满足相邻约束就能实现（已冻结），理论卷还给出一个具体的 Markov 拼接：第一个位置以概率 t 选，之后上一个选了就不选，没选就以 t/(1 − t) 选。环上不一样：奇环 C2k+1 上每点 1/2 满足所有相邻约束，但任何真正的选法最多选 k 个点，平均和至多 k < k + 1/2，所以不能实现；均匀占用最多只能到 k/(2k + 1)。偶环没有这个障碍。这就是局部一致不保证整体一致的最小例子。
  5. 边界。理论卷明确写道：金字塔上的一个点是概率分布的平均，不是一次实际填充；页面上的类比与推导不能推出几何装箱的最优值，不能把奇环现象解释成物理曲率，也不能当作黎曼猜想或物理理论的证明。页面上的抽样（立方体撒点、路径与环上的配置）只是演示，读数带随机误差。

The AURIC FIB-ATOM theory volumes start from a very small local rule: three positions in a row (low end x, middle z, high end y), the middle excluding both ends (xz = yz = 0) while the two ends may be chosen together. There are exactly five legal combinations, written 0 (empty), 2 (low end only), 3 (middle only), 5 (high end only) and 25 (both ends) in the volumes, the numbers being their “quantity” readings. This page draws the geometry these five combinations span, and what averages can and cannot see.

  1. The substitution tree (theory volume). Two atoms α and β with the substitution α → β, β → ⟨β, α⟩; ordered brackets are part of the source. Level by level Tn+2 = ⟨Tn+1, Tn⟩, and the numbers of leaves, of α and of β are Fibonacci numbers; the quantity read as “α counts 2, β counts 3” is exactly F(n + 3). Separately, there are exactly F(n + 2) words of length n with no two adjacent 1s (frozen); for n = 3 these five words 000, 100, 010, 001, 101 are precisely the five modes.
  2. The pyramid (frozen). Over all probability laws on the five combinations, the average occupancy (X, Y, Z) fills exactly the square-based pyramid {X, Y, Z ≥ 0, X + Z ≤ 1, Y + Z ≤ 1}: the base is the unit square (the four combinations with Z = 0) and the apex is “middle only”. The slice at height Z is a square of side 1 − Z, so the volume is ∫(1 − Z)² dZ = 1/3 and the volume centroid is (3/8, 3/8, 1/4). This hull description is Stanley’s chain polytope of the three-point fence. About a third of the points scattered in the unit cube on the page land in the pyramid (Monte Carlo, a demonstration only).
  3. The hidden fibre (theory volume; the Fréchet interval is frozen). Five probabilities have four degrees of freedom; three averages fix three of them, and what remains is κ, the probability that both ends are chosen. Given the average point, the feasible laws are p = (r − X − Y + κ, X − κ, Z, Y − κ, κ) (order 0, 2, 3, 5, 25, r = 1 − Z), with κ in [max(0, X + Y − r), min(X, Y)]: the two-event Fréchet interval (frozen) rescaled by r. The width of the interval is min{X, Y, r − X, r − Y}: zero on the four triangular side faces (the averages fix the law) and positive inside the base. Two laws with the same averages can differ only by t·(1, −1, 0, −1, 1), the square relation v0 + v25 = v2 + v5. The determinant of the four base cells, Δ = p0p25 − p2p5 = rκ − XY, is r² times the covariance of the two ends when the middle is empty, and |Δ| ≤ r²/4; the point κ* = XY/r where Δ = 0 is the product completion (the ends independent given the middle). The uniform law p = (1/5, …, 1/5) sits exactly at κ*, and still has overall covariance XYZ/r = 1/25.
  4. Gluing (the path is frozen, the odd cycle comes from the theory volume). Chain many small windows in a row, every position with average occupancy t; the neighbour constraint is t + t ≤ 1. On a path, satisfying the neighbour constraints is enough (frozen), and the theory volume gives an explicit Markov gluing: choose the first position with probability t, then never choose a position after a chosen one, and choose it with probability t/(1 − t) after an unchosen one. A cycle is different: on an odd cycle C2k+1 the value 1/2 everywhere meets every neighbour constraint, but any actual choice has at most k positions, so the average sum is at most k < k + 1/2 and the value cannot be realized; uniform occupancy can reach only k/(2k + 1). Even cycles have no such obstruction. This is the smallest example of local consistency failing to guarantee global consistency.
  5. Limits. The theory volumes state it plainly: a point of the pyramid is the average of a probability law, not one actual filling; the analogies and derivations on the page do not give optimal values for geometric packing, do not turn the odd-cycle phenomenon into physical curvature, and are not a proof of the Riemann hypothesis or of a physical theory. The sampling on the page (points in the cube, configurations on the path and cycle) is a demonstration, and its readings carry random error.
