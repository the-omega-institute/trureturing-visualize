d 维空间的两组标准正交基互无偏，是说一组里的任何一个态 |u⟩ 拿去按另一组测量，每个结果的概率都是 |⟨u|v⟩|² = 1/d：知道一组的结果，对另一组的结果一无所知。三维视图有三块柱阵，对应三组已知的基；每根柱子是候选里一个向量与那组基里一个向量的重叠概率，半透明的平面高 1/d。三块柱阵全部齐平，候选就与三组都互无偏。下方曲线是同一个梯度下降从 8 个随机起点出发时缺陷 D = Σ(p − 1/d)² 的变化。

  1. 素数维与上界。任何维数最多有 d + 1 组互无偏基；素数维（及素数幂维）恰好有 d + 1 组（Ivanović，J. Phys. A 14, 3241 (1981)；Wootters、Fields，Ann. Phys. 191, 363 (1989)）。页面里奇素数维的三组已知基是标准基、傅里叶基 ωjk/√d 与二次相位基 ωjk + j²/√d；二维是泡利 Z、X、Y 三组。二维最多三组，所以二维“找第四组”注定失败，这是已证的。
  2. 六维：已知三组，第四组开放。六维的三组可由二维的三组与三维的三组逐个做张量积得到（理论卷 CONE_PROGRAM_FORMAL 定理 8.7 记录了这个构造，本库尚未形式化）。Zauner 猜想说六维最多三组；能否有四组、能否凑满七组，至今都没有证明（McNulty、Weigert 的综述，Quantum 10, 2051 (2026)）。
  3. 数值证据（不是证明）。Brierley 与 Weigert 广泛搜索后只找到 35 种“互无偏星座”中的 18 种，是“六维没有七组”的最强数值证据（Phys. Rev. A 78, 042312 (2008)）。Prat Colomer 等用三种数值方法都指向“六维没有四组”（arXiv:2203.09429）。Grassl 证明了六维里有几组三元组是极大的，不能再扩充（arXiv:quant-ph/0406175）。本页的搜索也是数值实验：在这组三元组上，8 个起点的缺陷都停在正值，连一个与三组都无偏的向量也没找到。这只是线索。
  4. 本库的形式化（已冻结的部分）。互无偏 ⟺ 中心化平面正交 ⟺ 两次不读的测量抹平迹零部分（mutually_unbiased_diagonal_planes）：右侧“最大 |p − 1/d|”就是这些平面内积的读数。六维若有四组，任意两组的对易子平方和恰为 10（fourMUBContexts_have_commutator_sum_ten）；由柯西–施瓦茨不等式，这个和最大就是 2(d − 1)，取等当且仅当互无偏——这一步是本页的推导。四组的存在可以归约成 Hadamard 图册上的“提升三元组”问题（no_fourMUBHadamardWitness_of_no_lifted_atlas）；两个标准 Zauner 补全永远不互无偏（zaunerCanonicalCompletion_crossGram_not_nonzero_flat）。
  5. 还没证出来的。理论卷（MUB_SIX_FOURTH_BASIS_THEORY）列出的关键开放命题包括：严格 X 补全在 context 层面的亲和度下界 α ≥ 1（卷内写明“仍未证明”）、四元组的立方定向一致性、完整 6 阶图册上特征核的零点集、非标准补全的排除，以及各分支的整体汇总。卷内明确规定：局部分支证书不得当作解决这个问题。
  6. 模型边界。搜索用斯蒂弗尔流形上的黎曼梯度下降，每个起点 400 步，QR 收回、回溯步长；缺陷降到 10−20 以下记为“找到”。没找到不能说明不存在：梯度下降会停在局部极小，五维、七维里也有起点停住；七维里第四组明明存在，换一批起点却可能整批 8 个都停住。

Two orthonormal bases of a d-dimensional space are mutually unbiased when any state |u⟩ of one, measured in the other, gives every outcome with probability |⟨u|v⟩|² = 1/d: knowing a result in one basis says nothing about results in the other. The 3D view has three bar fields, one for each of three known bases; each bar is the overlap probability between a vector of the candidate and a vector of that basis, and the translucent plane sits at 1/d. When all three fields are level, the candidate is unbiased to all three. The chart below follows the defect D = Σ(p − 1/d)² of one gradient descent run from 8 random starts.

  1. Prime dimensions and the bound. No dimension has more than d + 1 mutually unbiased bases; prime (and prime-power) dimensions have exactly d + 1 (Ivanović, J. Phys. A 14, 3241 (1981); Wootters and Fields, Ann. Phys. 191, 363 (1989)). In odd prime dimensions the page’s three known bases are the standard basis, the Fourier basis ωjk/√d and the quadratic-phase basis ωjk + j²/√d; in dimension two they are the Pauli Z, X and Y bases. Dimension two has at most three, so the search for a fourth must fail there, and that is proved.
  2. Dimension six: three known, the fourth open. The three bases in dimension six come from tensoring the three qubit bases with the three qutrit bases one by one (the CONE_PROGRAM_FORMAL volume records this construction as Theorem 8.7; this library has not formalized it). Zauner’s conjecture says six has at most three; whether four exist, or a full seven, is still unproved (the review by McNulty and Weigert, Quantum 10, 2051 (2026)).
  3. Numerical evidence (not proof). After extensive searches Brierley and Weigert found only 18 of the 35 “mutually unbiased constellations”, the strongest numerical evidence that six has no seven bases (Phys. Rev. A 78, 042312 (2008)). Prat Colomer et al. used three numerical methods, all suggesting that six has no four (arXiv:2203.09429). Grassl proved that several triples in dimension six are maximal and cannot be extended (arXiv:quant-ph/0406175). The page’s search is a numerical experiment too: on this triple, all 8 starts stall at a positive defect, and not even one vector unbiased to all three is found. That is only a lead.
  4. This library’s formal work (frozen parts). Unbiased ⟺ the centred planes are orthogonal ⟺ two unread measurements wipe out the traceless part (mutually_unbiased_diagonal_planes): the readout “largest |p − 1/d|” reads those plane inner products. If six had four bases, any two would have squared commutator sum exactly 10 (fourMUBContexts_have_commutator_sum_ten); by the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality that sum is at most 2(d − 1), with equality exactly when the bases are unbiased; that step is the page’s own derivation. The existence of four reduces to a lifted-triple problem on a Hadamard atlas (no_fourMUBHadamardWitness_of_no_lifted_atlas); two canonical Zauner completions are never mutually unbiased (zaunerCanonicalCompletion_crossGram_not_nonzero_flat).
  5. What is not proved yet. The key open propositions listed in the theory volume (MUB_SIX_FOURTH_BASIS_THEORY) include the context-level strict-X completion affinity lower bound α ≥ 1 (which the volume says is still unproved), the cubic orientation coherence of a quartet, the zero locus of the feature kernel over the full order-six atlas, the exclusion of noncanonical completions, and the global aggregation of branches. The volume states explicitly that local branch certificates must not be presented as solving the problem.
  6. Model limits. The search is Riemannian gradient descent on the Stiefel manifold, 400 steps per start, with a QR retraction and backtracking steps; a defect below 10−20 counts as found. Not finding is not proof of absence: gradient descent can stop in a local minimum, and some starts stall in dimensions five and seven as well; in dimension seven a fourth basis certainly exists, yet a whole batch of 8 starts can stall.
