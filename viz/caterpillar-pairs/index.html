For n ≤ 17 it finds only listed pairs (n = 12, 14, 16); at n = 18 it finds 6 pairs: one is the listed j = 3, the other 5 are unlisted, Lean’s witness among them.
ARGUED本页的论证（未形式化、未经评审）：可容许组合的毛毛虫去掉所有叶子后恰好剩下脊柱这条路径（首尾两部分 ≥ 2 保证脊柱端点不是叶子），所以两只这样的毛毛虫同构当且仅当组合相同或互为反转。于是上面的搜索覆盖了 n ≤ 18 的全部同构类，名单外的最小反例有 18 个顶点。卷宗把最小规模列为未决；这条论证没有形式化。The page’s argument (not formalized, not reviewed): removing all leaves from the caterpillar of an admissible composition leaves exactly the spine path (end parts ≥ 2 keep the spine’s ends from being leaves), so two such caterpillars are isomorphic exactly when their compositions are equal or reversed. The search above therefore covers every isomorphism class with n ≤ 18, and the smallest unlisted counterexample has 18 vertices. The dossier lists the smallest size as open; this argument is not formalized.
OPEN定理之外：所有这类成对的分类；其他无限族（n = 18 另外四对名单外的成对是否属于某个族）；原文的四色问题；t 精化版本；最小反例规模（见上面的论证，它未经评审）。Outside the theorems: a classification of all such pairs; other infinite families (whether the other four unlisted pairs at n = 18 belong to one); the source’s four-colour questions; t-refined analogues; the smallest counterexample size (see the argument above, which is not reviewed).

图的色对称函数把所有正常着色记下来；把第 i 种颜色换成 X^i 并只用 k 种颜色，就得到主特化 ps(G, k)：一个多项式，X^d 的系数是颜色编号之和为 d 的正常着色数。毛毛虫图由一列正整数决定：脊柱上第 i 个顶点挂 aᵢ − 1 片叶子。Bingham 等人发现 (5, 4, 3) 与 (4, 5, 3) 用两种颜色能分开、用三种颜色却分不开，并问这一现象在 (5, j, j, 4, 3)/(4, j, j, 5, 3) 上是否对所有 j 成立，以及是否只有这些成对。

  1. 整族成立。三色时，着色按脊柱分解成块权重的乘积；中间两块相同时，交换首块 5 与第四块 4 不改变总和（对角恒等式）。两色时一棵树只有两种着色，两种颜色之和分别是 X^{j+5} + X^{j+7} 与 2X^{j+6}，不同。
  2. 名单不完整。18 个顶点的 (2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 1, 4, 3) 与 (2, 2, 3, 2, 3, 4, 2) 也是两色不同、三色相同；前者的脊柱有 8 段，名单里的都不到 8 段，所以不同构。
  3. 三维视图。前面两排是两只毛毛虫（脊柱琥珀、叶子青/品红）。后面是三色主特化的系数天际线：每个次数 d 一对柱子，高度按 log₁₀(1 + 系数)，两只完全一样高。再后面一排小柱是二色主特化——它们落在不同的次数上。

原文：问题卷宗：整族 · 问题卷宗：唯一性 · BinghamCaterpillarPs3Family.lean · BinghamCaterpillarUniquenessRefutation.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.31785 · arXiv:2610.31785

The chromatic symmetric function of a graph records all its proper colourings; replacing colour i by X^i and allowing only k colours gives the principal specialization ps(G, k): a polynomial whose coefficient of X^d counts the proper colourings with colour labels summing to d. A caterpillar is determined by a list of positive integers: the i-th spine vertex carries aᵢ − 1 leaves. Bingham et al. noticed that (5, 4, 3) and (4, 5, 3) are told apart with two colours but not with three, and asked whether this holds for (5, j, j, 4, 3)/(4, j, j, 5, 3) for every j, and whether these are the only such pairs.

  1. The family holds. With three colours the colourings factor along the spine into products of block weights; when the two middle blocks agree, exchanging the first block 5 with the fourth block 4 does not change the sum (the diagonal identity). With two colours a tree has just two colourings, with colour sums giving X^{j+5} + X^{j+7} and 2X^{j+6}, which differ.
  2. The list is incomplete. The 18-vertex (2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 1, 4, 3) and (2, 2, 3, 2, 3, 4, 2) also differ with two colours and agree with three; the first has a spine of 8 parts, every listed caterpillar fewer than 8, so they are not isomorphic.
  3. 3D view. The two front rows are the two caterpillars (spine amber, leaves cyan / magenta). Behind them is the coefficient skyline of the three-colour specialization: one pair of bars per degree d, height log₁₀(1 + coefficient), and both are exactly as tall. The row of small bars further back is the two-colour specialization — it sits at different degrees.
