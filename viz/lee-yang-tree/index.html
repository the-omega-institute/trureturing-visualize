理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

Lee 与 Yang 证明：铁磁 Ising 模型的配分函数，作为外场逸度 x 的多项式，零点都在单位圆 |x| = 1 上。Fiol、Martínez-Montoya、Rios Fukelman 在超共形箭图理论的平面极限里遇到树上的一族这样的多项式，猜想它们也有这个性质。本库发现：把不同的分法按任意正权重混在一起，根会离开圆；但把物理展开里同一系数的所有放法加起来，根又全部回到圆上。

  1. 多项式（原文）。给树的每条边一个正整数总数 ni，再把它拆成 ki + (ni − ki)（1 ≤ ki ≤ ni − 1），两部分分给边的两个端点；顶点的“质量” mv 是它收到的整数之和。每个顶点取自旋上或下，一个状态贡献 τe± xΣ 向上的 mv，e± 是两端自旋相反的边数。对所有状态求和，再按正权重 ρ(k) 对所有分法求和，得到 P(τ, x)。翻转全部自旋把 xj 换成 xN−j，所以 P 是自反的。
  2. 猜想 1 与反例（已冻结）。猜想说根都在单位圆上。三点路径 0 → 1 ← 2、总数 (4, 4)、τ = −1，九种分法的联合权重取 W₃₁：对角线 (1, 10, 31)，其余 1。结果 P = 2(x − 1)²x³q(y)，y = x + 1/x，q(y) = 24y³ + 45y² − 2y − 26，判别式 −16404 < 0，所以 q 有非实根 w；x² − wx + 1 = 0 的根 x 若在圆上，w = x + x̄ 就是实数，矛盾。
  3. 为什么会破（卷宗论证）。τ = −1 时自旋和按顶点分解：∏v(1 + (−1)deg vxmv)。单个分法的根都是单位根，在圆上；不同分法的顶点质量不同，正系数相加不保持这个性质。把 (3, 3) 的权重换成 M，M ≥ 31 都会离开（散文证明），页面数值显示 M = 30 还在圆上。
  4. 物理权重（已冻结）。原文 (2.19) 的物理权重是每条边的二项式系数 C(2n, 2k) 乘上每个顶点的阶乘比与端点因子（2k)!/((k − 1)!k!)。这些权重也不能救回猜想：九点双中心树、每条边总数 3、τ = −1 时，Lean 把多项式约化成 (1 − x)⁶x⁹F(y)，证明 F 在 y ≥ 16/9 上为正、却不能完全分解成实一次因子，于是有一个根在圆内、它的倒数在圆外。离圆的距离只有约 0.002，页面要放大几百倍才看得见。
  5. 配对系数（已冻结）。原文真正需要的是自由能展开的系数：对一个固定的总数多重集，把它在边上的所有放法加起来。对“两条边总数 3、其余 2”这个多重集，本库对任何有限树证明了：系数是 2E + 2 次自反多项式，根全部在圆上，x = 1 的重数等于奇度顶点数。双中心树还有完整的根分类。页面显示单个放法可以离圆（m = 5 时两条中心边取 3 的那一种），再加入别的放法，根又回到圆上；全部加完正是冻结定理覆盖的系数。
  6. 读图。三维视图是复平面：单位圆在中央，根离圆的距离乘以放大倍数 Z 后画出（|x| = 1 ± d 画在 1 ± Zd），重根竖直叠起。右下的 y 平面把每个根 x 映到 y = x + 1/x：x 在单位圆上当且仅当 y 是 [−2, 2] 里的实数，所以“离圆”就是“y 离开区间”。
  7. 边界。乘积形式权重的猜想 1、物理权重下的大多数总数组合与树、对所有树求和的版本都还没有结论。页面上的根、离圆距离与各种扫描都是现算的数值。

原文：问题卷宗：树上的 Lee–Yang 猜想 · QuiverTreeIsingRefutation.lean · QuiverTreePhysicalE8.lean · TreePairTheorem.lean · FiolDoubleHubPhysical.lean · FiolDoubleHubCoefficient.lean · Fiol、Martínez-Montoya、Rios Fukelman，JHEP 08（2020）161 · Lee、Yang，Phys. Rev. 87, 410（1952） · Lalín、Smyth，Acta Math. Hungar. 138, 85（2013）

Lee and Yang proved that the partition function of a ferromagnetic Ising model, as a polynomial in the fugacity x of the external field, has all its zeros on the unit circle |x| = 1. Fiol, Martínez-Montoya and Rios Fukelman met a family of such polynomials on trees in the planar limit of superconformal quiver theories and conjectured the same property. This library finds that mixing different splits with arbitrary positive weights pushes roots off the circle, while summing all placements of one coefficient of the physical expansion brings every root back onto it.

  1. The polynomial (source). Give each edge of a tree a positive integer total ni and split it as ki + (ni − ki) with 1 ≤ ki ≤ ni − 1, the two parts going to the two endpoints; the “mass” mv of a vertex is the sum of the integers it receives. Each vertex has spin up or down, and a state contributes τe± xΣ mv over up vertices, where e± counts edges with opposite spins. Summing over states, then over splits with positive weights ρ(k), gives P(τ, x). Flipping every spin turns xj into xN−j, so P is self-reciprocal.
  2. Conjecture 1 and the counterexample (frozen). The conjecture says that all roots lie on the unit circle. Take the three-vertex path 0 → 1 ← 2 with totals (4, 4) and τ = −1, and give the nine splits the joint weights W₃₁: diagonal (1, 10, 31), all other entries 1. Then P = 2(x − 1)²x³q(y) with y = x + 1/x and q(y) = 24y³ + 45y² − 2y − 26, whose discriminant is −16404 < 0, so q has a nonreal root w. A root x of x² − wx + 1 = 0 on the circle would make w = x + x̄ real, a contradiction.
  3. Why it breaks (argued in the dossier). At τ = −1 the spin sum factors over vertices as ∏v(1 + (−1)deg vxmv). Each single split has only roots of unity, on the circle; different splits carry different vertex masses, and adding them with positive coefficients does not preserve the property. Replacing the (3, 3) weight by M, every M ≥ 31 leaves the circle (a prose proof), and the page finds M = 30 still on it.
  4. Physical weights (frozen). The physical weights of the source’s (2.19) multiply a binomial coefficient C(2n, 2k) per edge by a factorial ratio and endpoint factors (2k)!/((k − 1)!k!) at every vertex. They do not rescue the conjecture: on the nine-vertex double hub with every total 3 and τ = −1, Lean reduces the polynomial to (1 − x)⁶x⁹F(y), shows that F is positive for y ≥ 16/9 yet does not split into real linear factors, so one root lies inside the circle and its reciprocal outside. The distance from the circle is only about 0.002; the page must magnify it a few hundred times to show it.
  5. The pair coefficient (frozen). What the source actually needs are coefficients of the free-energy expansion: for one fixed multiset of totals, the sum over all its placements on the edges. For the multiset “two edges with total 3, the rest with total 2”, this library proves for every finite tree that the coefficient is a self-reciprocal polynomial of degree 2E + 2 with all its roots on the circle, and that x = 1 has multiplicity equal to the number of odd-degree vertices. The double hub also has a complete root classification. The page shows that a single placement can leave the circle (with m = 5, the one with both central edges at total 3); once other placements are added the roots are back on it, and the full sum is exactly the coefficient the frozen theorem covers.
  6. Reading the picture. The 3D view is the complex plane: the unit circle sits in the middle, the distance of each root from it is multiplied by the magnification Z (|x| = 1 ± d is drawn at 1 ± Zd), and repeated roots are stacked vertically. The y-plane at the lower right maps each root x to y = x + 1/x: x lies on the unit circle exactly when y is a real number in [−2, 2], so “off the circle” means “off the interval”.
  7. Limits. Conjecture 1 for product weights, most total multisets and trees under physical weights, and the version summed over all trees remain unsettled. The roots, distances and scans on the page are computed numbers.
