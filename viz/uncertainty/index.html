LEAN ✓Robertson–Schrödinger 恒等式，保留 Gram 余项：对复内积空间上的对称算子 A、B 与单位向量 ψ，令 u = Aψ − ⟨A⟩ψ，v = Bψ − ⟨B⟩ψ，则 ΔA²·ΔB² = cov² + c² + G，其中 cov = Re⟨u, v⟩ 是对称化协方差，c = Im⟨u, v⟩ = ⟨[A, B]⟩/2i 是对易子项，G ≥ 0。已冻结：The Robertson–Schrödinger identity with its Gram remainder kept: for symmetric operators A, B on a complex inner-product space and a unit vector ψ, with u = Aψ − ⟨A⟩ψ and v = Bψ − ⟨B⟩ψ, ΔA²·ΔB² = cov² + c² + G, where cov = Re⟨u, v⟩ is the symmetrized covariance, c = Im⟨u, v⟩ = ⟨[A, B]⟩/2i is the commutator term, and G ≥ 0. LEAN ✓熵不确定关系：d + 1 个 d 点分布的平方和之和若是 1 + P，则 Σb H(pb) ≥ (d + 1)·log((d + 1)/(1 + P))。证明是两次 Jensen：每组先用 −log Σp² ≤ H（碰撞熵不超过香农熵，等号当且仅当在支撑上均匀），再对 d + 1 组取平均。已冻结：The entropic uncertainty relation: if d + 1 distributions on d points have squared probabilities summing to 1 + P, then Σb H(pb) ≥ (d + 1)·log((d + 1)/(1 + P)). The proof is Jensen twice: for each basis −log Σp² ≤ H (collision entropy never exceeds Shannon entropy, with equality exactly when the distribution is uniform on its support), then an average over the d + 1 bases. Maassen and Uffink (1988) bounded the entropy sum of two bases by −2 log c. Ivanovic (1992) proved for pure states that the entropy sum over d + 1 mutually unbiased bases is at least (d + 1) log((d + 1)/2), the frozen bound above at P = 1; Sánchez-Ruiz (1995) strengthened it for even d. Wootters and Fields (1989) constructed complete sets of unbiased bases in prime-power dimensions. One-axis twisting is due to Kitagawa and Ueda (1993).

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

两个可观测量 A、B 不对易时，它们的涨落不能同时任意小。教科书里的 Robertson 不等式 ΔA·ΔB ≥ ½|⟨[A, B]⟩| 只记了一笔账。本库冻结的是一个等式：ΔA²·ΔB² 恰好等于三笔之和——协方差 cov²、对易子项 c²、非负的 Gram 余项 G。页面把这三笔画成一本账，看不同的态把账记在哪一栏；后半页换一种记账方式，用 d + 1 组互无偏基上的熵。

  1. 三笔账（已冻结）。u = Aψ − ⟨A⟩ψ 与 v = Bψ − ⟨B⟩ψ 是两个“偏差向量”，‖u‖² = ΔA²，‖v‖² = ΔB²。复数 ⟨u, v⟩ 的实部是协方差 cov = ½⟨AB + BA⟩ − ⟨A⟩⟨B⟩，虚部是 c = ⟨[A, B]⟩/2i。Cauchy–Schwarz 的缺口 G = ‖u‖²‖v‖² − |⟨u, v⟩|² ≥ 0，于是 ΔA²ΔB² = cov² + c² + G（RobertsonSchrodinger.robertson_schrodinger）。丢掉 cov² 和 G 就是 Robertson 不等式，只丢 G 就是 Schrödinger 不等式。
  2. 量子比特。纯态时 u 和 v 都垂直于 ψ，而二维空间里垂直于 ψ 的方向只有一个，所以 u、v 平行，G = 0：纯量子比特的 Robertson–Schrödinger 总是取等。混合态 ρ 可以写成 Hilbert–Schmidt 空间里的单位向量 √ρ，同一个冻结定理照样适用（这一步代入是页面的手工推导，不是 Lean 检查的）。对 A = a·σ、B = b·σ 和布洛赫向量 r，算出来 G = |a × b|²(1 − |r|²)：余项正好量出态有多混。冻结的混合态 Robertson 不等式（MixedStateRobertson.mixed_state_robertson_uncertainty）是这本账去掉 cov² 和 G 后的那一半。
  3. 自旋 j 与单轴扭转。从沿 x 的自旋相干态出发，取 A = Jy、B = Jz：这时 c = ⟨Jx⟩/2，cov = 0，G = 0，Robertson 取等。再加单轴扭转 e−iμJz²/2（Kitagawa、Ueda，Phys. Rev. A 47, 5138 (1993)）：μ 小时态被挤成香蕉形，账从 c² 挪到 cov²；μ 再大，态不再像高斯，账挪进 G；j = 5、μ = π 时是两个相干态的叠加（猫态），c 与 cov 都是 0，整个 ΔJy²ΔJz² 都记在 G 上。球面上画的是 Husimi 分布 |⟨θ, φ|ψ⟩|²。
  4. 互无偏基上的熵（已冻结）。两组基叫互无偏，是指一组里的任何向量在另一组里测，每个结果的概率都是 1/d。d 是素数时，计算基加上向量 ωbk² + mk/√d（b, m = 0, …, d − 1，ω = e2πi/d）就是完整的 d + 1 组（d = 2 时是三组泡利本征基；Wootters、Fields，Ann. Phys. 191, 363 (1989)）。冻结定理一：所有组的概率平方和恰好是 1 + tr ρ²（碰撞守恒）。冻结定理二：于是熵和 Σb H(pb) ≥ (d + 1) log((d + 1)/(1 + tr ρ²))。页面把一个随机纯态逐渐混成 I/d，三条曲线依次是下界、碰撞熵之和、香农熵之和；完全混合时三者相等，下界取等号。纯态时它就是 Ivanovic 的界（J. Phys. A 25, L363 (1992)）。
  5. 找最小熵和（数值，不是证明）。对纯态随机局部搜索熵和的最小值：多次随机起点，每步随机扰动，变小就接受，否则缩小步长。d = 2 搜到 2 比特（例如 σz 的本征态：0 + 1 + 1）；d = 3 搜到 4 比特，正好等于下界 4·log₂2——态 (|0⟩ − |1⟩)/√2 在四组基上都给出 (½, ½, 0)，两次 Jensen 都取等，这一点页面也直接核对；d = 5 搜到约 10.2524 比特，下界 9.5098；d = 7 约 17.3028，下界 16。
  6. 模型边界。量子比特与自旋的量都是精确的有限维计算；最小熵和是数值搜索的结果，不排除更小的值；文献结果（Robertson、Schrödinger、Maassen–Uffink、Ivanovic、Sánchez-Ruiz）本库只形式化了上面标 LEAN 的形式。

When two observables A and B do not commute, their fluctuations cannot both be made arbitrarily small. The textbook Robertson inequality ΔA·ΔB ≥ ½|⟨[A, B]⟩| books only one entry. What this library has frozen is an equality: ΔA²·ΔB² is exactly the sum of three entries, the covariance cov², the commutator term c² and a non-negative Gram remainder G. The page draws the three entries as a ledger and shows which column each state books its uncertainty in; the second half switches to another kind of bookkeeping, entropy over d + 1 mutually unbiased bases.

  1. Three entries (frozen). u = Aψ − ⟨A⟩ψ and v = Bψ − ⟨B⟩ψ are two “deviation vectors”, with ‖u‖² = ΔA² and ‖v‖² = ΔB². The real part of the complex number ⟨u, v⟩ is the covariance cov = ½⟨AB + BA⟩ − ⟨A⟩⟨B⟩, its imaginary part is c = ⟨[A, B]⟩/2i. The Cauchy–Schwarz gap G = ‖u‖²‖v‖² − |⟨u, v⟩|² is ≥ 0, so ΔA²ΔB² = cov² + c² + G (RobertsonSchrodinger.robertson_schrodinger). Dropping cov² and G gives the Robertson inequality; dropping only G gives Schrödinger’s.
  2. Qubits. For a pure state u and v are both orthogonal to ψ, and in two dimensions only one direction is orthogonal to ψ, so u and v are parallel and G = 0: for a pure qubit Robertson–Schrödinger is always an equality. A mixed state ρ can be written as the unit vector √ρ in Hilbert–Schmidt space, and the same frozen theorem applies there (this substitution is a hand step on the page, not a Lean check). For A = a·σ, B = b·σ and Bloch vector r it gives G = |a × b|²(1 − |r|²): the remainder measures exactly how mixed the state is. The frozen mixed-state Robertson inequality (MixedStateRobertson.mixed_state_robertson_uncertainty) is the half of this ledger left after dropping cov² and G.
  3. Spin j and one-axis twisting. Start from the spin coherent state along x with A = Jy and B = Jz: then c = ⟨Jx⟩/2, cov = 0, G = 0, and Robertson is an equality. Now apply one-axis twisting e−iμJz²/2 (Kitagawa and Ueda, Phys. Rev. A 47, 5138 (1993)): for small μ the state is squeezed into a banana and the balance moves from c² to cov²; for larger μ the state stops looking Gaussian and the balance moves into G; at j = 5 and μ = π it is a superposition of two coherent states (a cat state), c and cov are both 0, and all of ΔJy²ΔJz² is booked in G. The sphere shows the Husimi distribution |⟨θ, φ|ψ⟩|².
  4. Entropy over unbiased bases (frozen). Two bases are mutually unbiased when any vector of one, measured in the other, gives every outcome with probability 1/d. For prime d the computational basis together with the vectors ωbk² + mk/√d (b, m = 0, …, d − 1, ω = e2πi/d) is a complete set of d + 1 (for d = 2 the three Pauli eigenbases; Wootters and Fields, Ann. Phys. 191, 363 (1989)). Frozen theorem one: the squared probabilities of all bases add up to exactly 1 + tr ρ² (collision conservation). Frozen theorem two: hence the entropy sum Σb H(pb) ≥ (d + 1) log((d + 1)/(1 + tr ρ²)). The page mixes a random pure state gradually into I/d; the three curves are, from the bottom, the bound, the sum of collision entropies and the sum of Shannon entropies. At full mixing all three meet and the bound is attained. For pure states it is Ivanovic’s bound (J. Phys. A 25, L363 (1992)).
  5. The least entropy sum (numerical, not a proof). A random local search for the smallest entropy sum over pure states: several random starts, a random perturbation at each step, accepted if it lowers the sum, otherwise the step shrinks. In d = 2 it finds 2 bits (for example an eigenstate of σz: 0 + 1 + 1); in d = 3 it finds 4 bits, exactly the bound 4·log₂2: the state (|0⟩ − |1⟩)/√2 gives (½, ½, 0) in all four bases, so both Jensen steps are equalities, and the page checks this state directly; in d = 5 it finds about 10.2524 bits against the bound 9.5098; in d = 7 about 17.3028 against 16.
  6. Model limits. The qubit and spin quantities are exact finite-dimensional computations; the least entropy sums come from a numerical search and smaller values are not excluded; of the literature results (Robertson, Schrödinger, Maassen–Uffink, Ivanovic, Sánchez-Ruiz) this library has formalized only the forms marked LEAN above.
