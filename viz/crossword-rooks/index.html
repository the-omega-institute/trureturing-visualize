LEAN ✓定义：Cell N 是 N × N 网格的格子；SameAcross、SameDown 说两个格子同行（同列）且之间全是白格，即在同一个横词（竖词）里；IsRookPlacement W R：R 是白格的子集，其中任意两个车不共词，且每个白格的横词和竖词里都有车；rookCount W 数这样的 R。猜想 claim：每个自然数 r 都是某个方形网格的 rookCount。构造：gridSize r = max(5, 2r − 1)，白格是 rowLeft 到 rowRight 的行区间（white）；candidate r k 是第 2 列的车在第 2k 行时的放法。本库证明 claim。已冻结：Definitions: Cell N, a cell of the N × N grid; SameAcross, SameDown, two cells in the same row (column) with only white cells between them, i.e. in one across (down) word; IsRookPlacement W R, R is a set of white cells, no two of its rooks share a word, and every white cell's across and down words hold a rook; rookCount W counts such R. The statement claim: every natural number r is the rookCount of some square grid. The construction: gridSize r = max(5, 2r − 1), white cells the row intervals from rowLeft to rowRight (white); candidate r k, the placement whose column-2 rook is in row 2k. This library proves claim. Frozen: CrosswordRookCounts.result

在填字网格上放车：车沿着白格横走竖走，遇到黑格就停。要求每个横词、每个竖词里恰好一个车——既不互相攻击，又把每个词都占住。Lewis 与 Won 问：放法数可以是哪些数？

  1. 答案。每个 r ≥ 0 都可以。Lean 给出边长 max(5, 2r − 1) 的网格，恰有 r 种放法；r = 0 用一个 3 × 3 的小网格。
  2. 为什么恰好 r 种。第 2 列是一个长竖词，只能有一个车，落在某个偶数行 2k。每个区块的单格竖词必须有车，于是同一行的其他格子都被封住；三格的侧边竖词因此在第 2k 行之前只能取上端、之后只能取下端。k 有 r 种取法，每种恰好对应一种放法。
  3. r = 0。一个横词里的两个白格各自是单格竖词：两个竖词都要车，但同一个横词只能放一个。
  4. 置换网格。黑格恰好是一个置换矩阵时，论文猜测放法数永远不 ≡ 3 (mod 4)（也不是 4 或 12）。2 7 4 8 1 6 3 5 的网格有 155 种，155 ≡ 3 (mod 4)，猜想不成立。

原文：问题卷宗 · CrosswordRookCounts.lean · Blueprint · 反例卷宗 · CrosswordPermutationGridRefutation.lean · 文献笔记 · arXiv:2609.03081

Place rooks on a crossword grid: a rook moves along white cells across and down and stops at black cells. Every across word and every down word must hold exactly one rook — no two attack each other, and every word is covered. Lewis and Won ask which numbers of placements can occur.

  1. The answer. Every r ≥ 0. Lean gives a grid of side max(5, 2r − 1) with exactly r placements; r = 0 uses a small 3 × 3 grid.
  2. Why exactly r. Column 2 is one long down word and holds a single rook, in some even row 2k. Each block's one-cell down word must hold a rook, which blocks the rest of its row; so a three-cell side word can only use its upper cell before row 2k and its lower cell after it. There are r choices of k, each giving exactly one placement.
  3. r = 0. Two white cells of one across word are each a one-cell down word: both down words need a rook, but the across word can hold only one.
  4. Permutation grids. When the black cells form a permutation matrix, the paper conjectures that the count is never ≡ 3 (mod 4) (nor 4 or 12). The grid of 2 7 4 8 1 6 3 5 has 155 placements, and 155 ≡ 3 (mod 4), so the conjecture fails.
