LEAN ✓Hasheminejad 与 Ramazi（2023）问：在一般的有限无向图上，混合了模仿者与最佳回应者的协调博弈，是否在任何“每人无限次被激活”的排程下都会到达均衡？答案是否定的。反例：完全图 K₄ 去掉 0–2 这条边，智能体 1 做最佳回应，0、2、3 模仿；收益 (AA, AB, BA, BB) 依次为 (1, 0, 0, 4)、(3, 2, 2, 3)、(4, 1, 0, 4)、(4, 3, 3, 4)，每人都满足协调条件；从 BABB 出发、排程 2, 0, 3, 1 循环，状态每 8 步回到原处，永远不是均衡。已冻结：Hasheminejad and Ramazi (2023) ask whether, on a general finite undirected graph, a coordination game mixing imitators and best responders reaches an equilibrium under every schedule that activates each agent infinitely often. The answer is no. Counterexample: the complete graph K₄ without the edge 0–2, agent 1 a best responder and agents 0, 2, 3 imitators; the payoffs (AA, AB, BA, BB) are (1, 0, 0, 4), (3, 2, 2, 3), (4, 1, 0, 4) and (4, 3, 3, 4), each satisfying the coordination condition; from BABB under the schedule 2, 0, 3, 1 repeated, the state returns every 8 steps and is never an equilibrium. ARGUED卷宗论证、未形式化：图里有三角形，最佳回应者按自己的收益比较翻转，三个模仿者把由此产生的局部最高收益一站一站传下去；在这个排程下八个状态 BABB → BABA → BBBA → BBAA → ABAA → ABAB → AAAB → AABB 轮流出现。协调条件和“无限次激活”给不出一般图上共同下降的势函数。Argued in the dossier, not formalized: the graph contains triangles; the best responder switches on its own payoff comparison while the three imitators relay the resulting local maxima; under this schedule the eight states BABB → BABA → BBBA → BBAA → ABAA → ABAB → AAAB → AABB come round in turn. The coordination condition and infinite activation do not provide a common decreasing potential on general graphs.
OPEN未决：稠密树是否在每个排程下都到达均衡；混合群体是否存在势函数；这个网络在别的排程下会怎样（页面只算随机排程）。Open: whether dense trees equilibrate under every schedule; whether a potential function exists for mixed populations; what other schedules do on this network (the page only computes random ones).

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

网络上每个人在 A、B 两个策略里选一个，与每个邻居各玩一次协调博弈，收益加起来就是他的效用。被激活时，模仿者看邻居们谁的效用最高，抄他的策略；最佳回应者比较自己选 A 和选 B 的效用，选高的那个。只有模仿者、或只有最佳回应者时，群体总会停在均衡；混在一起时，原文在几类网络上证明了同样的结论，一般图留作问题。本库给出了一个四人反例。

  1. 规则（原文，形式化逐字保留）。效用 ui = Σj 是邻居 payi(xi, xj)。模仿者：若邻居中效用最高的人只用 A（或只用 B），就改用它；两种策略都有最高者、或没有邻居时保持不变。最佳回应者：比较把自己改成 A 与改成 B 后的效用，取严格更高的那个，相等时保持。协调条件：每个人都满足 max{pay(B, A), pay(A, B)} < min{pay(A, A), pay(B, B)}。均衡指在任何激活下都不再变化的状态。
  2. 反例（已冻结）。四个人，K₄ 去掉 0–2 边；智能体 1 最佳回应，其余模仿；收益见右侧。从 BABB 出发，排程 2, 0, 3, 1 不断重复：前两次激活不改变状态，此后每一步都有人改策略，8 步回到 BABB。排程是持续的（每人每 4 步被激活一次），而轨迹永远不经过均衡。
  3. 只有两个均衡（页面数值）。页面检查全部 16 个状态，只有 AAAA 与 BBBB 在每个人被激活时都不变；8 步环上的状态都至少有一个人会改。
  4. 混合角色缺一不可（页面数值）。同一个排程下，把规则换成全员模仿、全员最佳回应或其他 14 种组合，轨迹都会停在均衡；只有反例那一种组合循环。
  5. 随机激活（页面数值）。每一步随机激活一个人，2000 次模拟全部很快停在 AAAA 或 BBBB。反例依赖一个精心挑选的排程，这与原文引用的随机激活几乎必然均衡的结果并不矛盾。
  6. 边界。原文在线形图、环、星形网络与稀疏树上的正面结论不受影响；稠密树、势函数的存在、这个网络在其他排程下的行为都没有结论。页面上的穷举与模拟都是现算的数值。

原文：问题卷宗：混合模仿与最佳回应在一般图上不一定到达均衡 · MixedImitationBestResponseCycle.lean · Hasheminejad、Ramazi，IEEE Control Systems Letters 7, 3078（2023） · Sakhaei、Maleki、Ramazi，IEEE Trans. Automatic Control（2024）

Each agent on a network picks one of two strategies, A or B, and plays a coordination game once with each neighbour; the payoffs add up to its utility. When activated, an imitator looks for the best-earning neighbours and copies their strategy; a best responder compares its own utility with A and with B and takes the higher. Populations of only imitators or only best responders always settle in an equilibrium; for mixed populations the source proves the same on several network classes and leaves general graphs open. This library gives a four-agent counterexample.

  1. The rules (source, kept verbatim in the formalization). Utility ui = Σj a neighbour payi(xi, xj). An imitator switches to A (or B) if the best-earning neighbours use only that strategy, and keeps its strategy when both strategies are among the best or it has no neighbours. A best responder compares its utility after switching to A and to B, takes the strictly higher one, and keeps its strategy on a tie. The coordination condition: every agent has max{pay(B, A), pay(A, B)} < min{pay(A, A), pay(B, B)}. An equilibrium is a state that no activation ever changes.
  2. The counterexample (frozen). Four agents on K₄ without the edge 0–2; agent 1 best-responds, the others imitate; the payoffs are on the right. From BABB with the schedule 2, 0, 3, 1 repeated, the first two activations change nothing, after that every step changes someone, and after 8 steps the state is BABB again. The schedule is persistent (everyone is activated every 4 steps), yet the trajectory never passes an equilibrium.
  3. Only two equilibria (page numerics). The page checks all 16 states: only AAAA and BBBB stay unchanged whoever is activated; every state on the 8-cycle has someone who would change.
  4. The mixed roles matter (page numerics). Under the same schedule, all imitators, all best responders, or any of the other 14 assignments of rules settle in an equilibrium; only the counterexample’s assignment cycles.
  5. Random activation (page numerics). Activating a random agent at every step, all 2000 simulated runs quickly settle in AAAA or BBBB. The counterexample relies on a carefully chosen schedule, which is consistent with the almost-sure equilibration under random activation cited by the source.
  6. Limits. The source’s positive results on linear graphs, rings, starlike networks and sparse trees stand; dense trees, the existence of a potential function, and the behaviour of this network under other schedules are not settled. The enumerations and simulations on the page are computed numbers.
