一排格子，每格是 0 或 1。每一步每个格子看自己和左右邻居，按一条规则决定下一刻的值。规则 10 很简单：左边是 0、右边是 1 时变成 1，否则变成 0；规则 245 恰好相反。二倍体自动机让每个格子每一步独立地掷硬币，以概率 λ 用规则 245、否则用规则 10。问题是：长时间以后，1 占多大比例？

  1. 答案。Lean 证明从第一步起比例就恰好是 1/4 + λ/2，不需要等待，对每个 λ 都对。原文用三阶局部结构理论得到这个数，问它是否精确。
  2. 为什么。一个格子变成 1 的概率只取决于“左 0、右 1”这个模式是否出现。这个模式的概率 q 在下一步变成 λ(1 − λ) + (1 − 2λ)² q——因为相隔两格的两个模式不可能同时出现，递推自我封闭——而 q = 1/4 正是它的不动点，公平初态恰好从这里出发。
  3. 时空之塔。三维视图把环上的历史一层层叠起来，时间向上。青色的 1 由规则 10 产生，品红的 1 由规则 245 产生；顶层的琥珀点就是“左 0、右 1”的位置。左下图比较环上的密度、ℤ 上的精确值和平均场；平均场把 q 当成 d(1 − d)，所以算错了。
  4. 对照。规则 36-219 的平均场方程一模一样，但卷宗指出这种封闭是 10-245 独有的：36-219 的精确密度第二步就离开了直线。

原文：问题卷宗 · Diploid10245Density.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · doi:10.1140/epjs/s11734-026-02447-z · arXiv:2607.01917

A row of cells, each 0 or 1. At every step each cell looks at itself and its two neighbours and follows a rule to decide its next value. Rule 10 is simple: become 1 when the left neighbour is 0 and the right neighbour is 1, otherwise 0; rule 245 is exactly the opposite. The diploid automaton lets every cell toss its own coin at every step, using rule 245 with probability λ and rule 10 otherwise. The question: in the long run, what fraction of cells is 1?

  1. The answer. Lean proves the fraction is exactly 1/4 + λ/2 from the very first step, with no waiting, for every λ. The source obtained this number from third-order local structure theory and asked whether it is exact.
  2. Why. The chance that a cell becomes 1 depends only on whether the pattern “0 on the left, 1 on the right” is present. That pattern’s probability q becomes λ(1 − λ) + (1 − 2λ)² q at the next step — the recursion closes because two such patterns two cells apart can never occur together — and q = 1/4 is exactly its fixed point, where the fair start begins.
  3. The space-time tower. The 3D view stacks the ring’s history layer by layer, time going up. Cyan 1s come from rule 10, magenta 1s from rule 245; amber dots on the top row are the positions of “0 on the left, 1 on the right”. The lower-left chart compares the ring’s density, the exact value on ℤ and mean field; mean field takes q to be d(1 − d), and gets it wrong.
  4. The contrast. Rule 36-219 has exactly the same mean-field equation, but, as the dossier notes, the closure is special to 10-245: the exact density of 36-219 leaves the line at the second step.
