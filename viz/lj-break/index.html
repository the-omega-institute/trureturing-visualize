ARGUED卷宗里的散文论证（未形式化）：J 是力对自由位移的导数，所以在平衡附近 ü = Ju；每个正特征值 μ 给出一对实指数 ±√μ，即一对不稳定的特征值。证明的机制是 J = −Bᵀ diag(c) B，B 把位移映到键伸长，像是 Σwⱼ = 0 这个超平面：k 个负曲率在一个余维为 1 的子空间里至少留下 k − 1 维、至多 k 维。对任意的曲率系数，两个端点都能取到。Prose arguments in the dossier (not formalized): J is the derivative of the force with respect to the free displacements, so near an equilibrium ü = Ju; each positive eigenvalue μ gives a real pair ±√μ, an unstable eigenvalue pair. The mechanism of the proof is J = −Bᵀ diag(c) B, where B maps displacements to bond extensions with image the hyperplane Σwⱼ = 0: k negative curvatures leave at least k − 1 and at most k dimensions inside a subspace of codimension one. For arbitrary curvature coefficients both endpoints are attained.
NUM页面数值，不是定理：平衡由二分法求出（所有键张力相同，断键长度 r_b、未断键长度 r_u 满足 k·r_b + (m + 1 − k)·r_u = (m + 1)d）；谱由 Jacobi 旋转算出；运动由速度 Verlet 积分。页面观察到：U = k − 1 出现在平均键长随张力减小的那一支，U = k 出现在随张力增大的那一支，与 dL/dτ = Σ 1/cⱼ 的符号一致。Numbers computed on the page, not theorems: equilibria are solved by bisection (all bonds carry the same tension, and the broken length r_b and unbroken length r_u satisfy k·r_b + (m + 1 − k)·r_u = (m + 1)d); the spectrum comes from Jacobi rotations; the motion is integrated by velocity Verlet. The page observes U = k − 1 on the branch where the mean bond length decreases with tension and U = k on the branch where it increases, matching the sign of dL/dτ = Σ 1/cⱼ.
OPEN定理之外：给定断键数的平衡是否存在、哪一支平衡带多出的那一对不稳定特征值、断键之后的非线性演化，以及论文第五节提出的次近邻相互作用与高维推广。Outside the theorem: whether equilibria with a prescribed number of breaks exist, which branch carries the extra unstable pair, the nonlinear evolution after a break, and the next-nearest-neighbour and higher-dimensional extensions raised in Section V of the paper.

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

m 个粒子排成一列，两端各接一面固定的墙，相邻之间（共 m + 1 根键）用 Lennard-Jones 势 φ(r) = r⁻¹² − 2r⁻⁶ 相连。把整条链拉长到平均间距 d，有些键会被拉过拐点 rᵢ = (13/7)^(1/6) ≈ 1.1087：那里 φ″ 变成负数，键“断了”。Rodrigues、Kevrekidis 与 Dobson 猜想，k 根断键的平衡态恰有 k − 1 到 k 对不稳定特征值；本库在 Lean 里证明了它。

  1. 平衡。每个自由粒子受力为零，意味着所有键的张力 τ = φ′(rⱼ) 相同。对 0 < τ < φ′(rᵢ)，φ′(r) = τ 有两个解：拐点内侧的 r_u 与外侧的 r_b。k 根断键的平衡由 k·r_b + (m + 1 − k)·r_u = (m + 1)d 定出 τ。断键放在哪些位置不影响键长，只影响谱的数值。
  2. 谱与计数（已冻结）。cⱼ = φ″(rⱼ) 在断键处为负、其余为正。三对角矩阵 J 的正特征值个数 U 满足 k − 1 ≤ U ≤ k。页面直接算出谱并核对这个不等式。
  3. 两个端点都会出现。把平均键长 L(τ)/(m + 1) 画成张力的函数：张力很小时断键很长，L 随 τ 下降；靠近拐点时，若断键少于一半，L 又随 τ 上升。d 略小于 rᵢ 时，同一个 d 有两支平衡：下降那一支 U = k − 1，上升那一支 U = k。这是页面的数值观察，与 dL/dτ = Σ 1/cⱼ 的符号一致。
  4. 运动。从平衡出发，沿最不稳定的方向加 10⁻³ 的扰动，用速度 Verlet 积分 ü = F(u)。U = 0 时链只在平衡附近振动；U ≥ 1 时扰动按 e^(√μ t) 增长，接着断键会愈合、移动或合并。俯视时，三维视图就是粒子的时空图。
  5. 边界。定理不保证给定断键数的平衡存在，不说哪一支带多出的那对不稳定特征值，也不涉及非线性演化与论文提出的推广。

原文：问题卷宗 · LennardJonesChainBreakInstabilityCount.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · Rodrigues、Kevrekidis、Dobson，Phys. Rev. E 99, 022201（2019） · arXiv:1802.03477

m particles stand in a row with a fixed wall at each end, and neighbours (m + 1 bonds in all) are joined by the Lennard-Jones potential φ(r) = r⁻¹² − 2r⁻⁶. Stretch the chain to a mean spacing d and some bonds are pulled past the inflection point rᵢ = (13/7)^(1/6) ≈ 1.1087, where φ″ turns negative: the bond is “broken”. Rodrigues, Kevrekidis and Dobson conjectured that an equilibrium with k broken bonds has between k − 1 and k unstable eigenvalue pairs; this library proves it in Lean.

  1. Equilibria. Zero force on every free particle means every bond carries the same tension τ = φ′(rⱼ). For 0 < τ < φ′(rᵢ), φ′(r) = τ has two solutions: r_u inside the inflection point and r_b outside. An equilibrium with k breaks fixes τ through k·r_b + (m + 1 − k)·r_u = (m + 1)d. Where the breaks sit does not change the lengths, only the values of the spectrum.
  2. Spectrum and count (frozen). cⱼ = φ″(rⱼ) is negative on broken bonds and positive on the others. The number U of positive eigenvalues of the tridiagonal matrix J satisfies k − 1 ≤ U ≤ k. The page computes the spectrum directly and checks this inequality.
  3. Both endpoints occur. Plot the mean bond length L(τ)/(m + 1) against the tension: at small tension the broken bonds are very long and L decreases with τ; near the inflection point, if fewer than half the bonds are broken, L increases again. For d slightly below rᵢ the same d has two branches of equilibria: U = k − 1 on the decreasing one and U = k on the increasing one. This is a numerical observation of the page, matching the sign of dL/dτ = Σ 1/cⱼ.
  4. Motion. From an equilibrium the page adds a 10⁻³ perturbation along the most unstable direction and integrates ü = F(u) by velocity Verlet. With U = 0 the chain only vibrates near equilibrium; with U ≥ 1 the perturbation grows like e^(√μ t), and then breaks heal, move or merge. Seen from the top, the 3D view is the space-time diagram of the particles.
  5. Limits. The theorem does not guarantee that an equilibrium with a given number of breaks exists, does not say which branch carries the extra unstable pair, and does not address the nonlinear evolution or the extensions raised in the paper.
