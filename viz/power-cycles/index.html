Erdős 与 Gyárfás 问：最小度至少 3 的有限图是否总有长度为 4、8、16、32 … 的圈。要找反例，就得造出避开所有这些长度的三次图。Garcia 把 f(k) 定义为没有长度 2ᵐ（m ≤ k）的圈的三次图的最小阶，并提出一个构造：在仿射群的 Cayley 图上做顶点替换，只要能找到合适的“取向”，就得到 f(6) 的候选图。

  1. Cayley 图。V = AGL(1, p) = (ℤ/p)ˣ × ℤ/p，乘法 (x, y)(z, w) = (xz, xw + y)。取 t = (−1, 0)、g = (a, 1)、r = g⁻¹，把 x 连到 xt、xg、xr，得到 N = p(p − 1) 个顶点的三次图（p = 29 时 812，p = 31 时 930）。页面把它画在环面上：一圈方向是 x 的离散对数，另一圈方向是 y。
  2. 小部件与取向。每个顶点 x 换成 15 个顶点的 H₁₅，它有三个端口 u = 14、v = 7、w = 0。取向 σ(x) ∈ {t, g, r} 指定哪条边接到 u，另两条边按 τₓ 接到 v、w。替换图有 15N 个顶点。问题是：能否选 σ、τ 使替换图没有 64 圈？
  3. 两族 14 圈。每个乘子都有两个词，从单位元出发右乘后回到单位元、途中 14 个前缀互不相同：一个含四个 t，一个含六个 t（例如 a = 3 时 tgtggggtrrrtrr 与 tgtgtgtgtrrrtr）。在圈的每个顶点，有一种边类型没被圈用到；圈经过端口 u，恰好当 σ(x) 不等于这个类型。
  4. 平均。把每个圈的全部 N 个左平移加起来，经过 u 的总次数是 S₄ = 10N − 2T 与 S₆ = 8N + 4T，其中 T 是 σ = t 的顶点数。于是 2S₄ + S₆ = 28N。若每个平移都至多 9 次经过 u，则 28N ≤ 27N，矛盾。所以总有一个平移至少 10 次经过 u。
  5. 展开成 64 圈。小部件里，u 到 v、u 到 w 各有一条 3 边路与一条 5 边路，v 到 w 有一条 5 边路。在那 10 次经过 u 的地方走 3 边路，其余 4 次走 5 边路，再加上 14 条外部边：14 + 30 + 20 = 64。各次经过在不同的小部件里，所以这是简单圈。Lean 对任意 σ、τ 构造出这条圈。
  6. 边界。这否定了经过这八个实例构造 f(6) 的路线，但没有确定 f(6)，也没有解决 Erdős–Gyárfás 猜想。

Erdős and Gyárfás asked whether every finite graph of minimum degree at least 3 has a cycle of length 4, 8, 16, 32, … A counterexample would be a cubic graph avoiding all these lengths. Garcia defines f(k) as the least order of a cubic graph with no cycle of length 2ᵐ (m ≤ k), and proposed a construction: replace the vertices of a Cayley graph of an affine group; a suitable “orientation” would give a candidate graph for f(6).

  1. The Cayley graph. V = AGL(1, p) = (ℤ/p)ˣ × ℤ/p with (x, y)(z, w) = (xz, xw + y). With t = (−1, 0), g = (a, 1), r = g⁻¹, join x to xt, xg, xr, a cubic graph on N = p(p − 1) vertices (812 for p = 29, 930 for p = 31). The page draws it on a torus: one way round is the discrete logarithm of x, the other is y.
  2. The gadget and the orientation. Each vertex x becomes a copy of the 15-vertex H₁₅ with three ports u = 14, v = 7, w = 0. The orientation σ(x) ∈ {t, g, r} says which edge attaches to u, and τₓ gives the other two edges to v and w. The replacement graph has 15N vertices. The question: can σ, τ be chosen so that it has no 64-cycle?
  3. Two families of 14-cycles. Each multiplier has two words that, multiplied on the right from the identity, return to it with 14 distinct prefixes: one with four t’s and one with six (for a = 3, tgtggggtrrrtrr and tgtgtgtgtrrrtr). At each vertex of the cycle one edge type is unused; the cycle passes through port u exactly when σ(x) differs from that type.
  4. Averaging. Summing over all N left translates of each cycle, the visits through u total S₄ = 10N − 2T and S₆ = 8N + 4T, where T counts the vertices with σ = t. So 2S₄ + S₆ = 28N. If every translate had at most 9 visits through u, then 28N ≤ 27N, a contradiction. So some translate passes through u at least 10 times.
  5. Expansion to a 64-cycle. Inside the gadget, u–v and u–w each have a 3-edge path and a 5-edge path, and v–w has a 5-edge path. Use 3-edge paths for 10 visits through u, 5-edge paths for the other 4, plus 14 external edges: 14 + 30 + 20 = 64. The visits lie in different gadgets, so the cycle is simple. Lean builds this cycle for every σ, τ.
  6. Limits. This rules out the route to f(6) through these eight instances, but determines neither f(6) nor the Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture.
