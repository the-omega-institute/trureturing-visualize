LEAN ✓联盟 S 的价值 w(S) 是诱导子图 G[S] 的能量（邻接特征值绝对值之和）；核由满足“每个联盟拿到的总和不少于它的价值、全体拿到的总和等于全体的价值”的分配组成。Arizmendi 与 Arizmendi 的猜想 3.10 说，对每个有限简单图，Shapley 值都在核里。本库证明它不成立：在完全二部图 K₂,ₘ（m ≥ 50）里，去掉一个稀缺侧顶点 a 的联盟 V∖{a} 的价值 2√m，大于它在 Shapley 分配下拿到的总和。已冻结：A coalition S is worth the energy of its induced subgraph G[S] (the sum of the absolute adjacency eigenvalues); the core consists of the allocations under which every coalition receives at least its worth and everyone together receives exactly the worth of the whole graph. Conjecture 3.10 of Arizmendi and Arizmendi says that for every finite simple graph the Shapley value lies in the core. This library proves it false: in the complete bipartite graph K₂,ₘ with m ≥ 50, the coalition V∖{a} that leaves out one scarce-side vertex a is worth 2√m, more than it receives in total under the Shapley allocation. Frozen: ArizmendiEnergyGameShapleyRefutation.result

把图的顶点当作玩家。一组顶点（联盟）S 的价值是它诱导的子图的能量 𝓔(G[S])，即邻接矩阵特征值的绝对值之和。Shapley 值按“每个玩家在所有加入顺序下带来的平均增量”分配全体的价值。Arizmendi 与 Arizmendi 猜想它总在核里——没有哪个联盟能抱怨自己拿得比自己的价值少。本库在 Lean 里给出了反例。

  1. 完全二部图上的联盟只看两个数。K_{a,b} 里一个联盟含稀缺侧 r 个、另一侧 k 个顶点，诱导子图是 K_{r,k}，能量 2√(rk)（冻结引理）。同侧的顶点对称，Shapley 值只有两个：稀缺侧 φ_A、另一侧 φ_B。
  2. 赤字地形。联盟 (r, k) 的赤字是 2√(rk) − (rφ_A + kφ_B)。核要求所有赤字 ≤ 0。三维视图把每个 r 画成一条随 k 变化的曲线；品红是正赤字——这个联盟挡住了 Shapley 分配。纵轴用 asinh 刻度，小的正赤字也看得见。
  3. 冻结的反例。K₂,ₘ 里稀缺点 a 的平均增量很大：它和另一侧的每个子集都连满，加入时贡献 2√(2k) − 2√k 或 2√k。Lean 证明 m ≥ 50 时 φₐ > 2(√2 − 1)√m；由效率，V∖{a} 拿到 2√(2m) − φₐ < 2√m = w(V∖{a})。
  4. 更早的反例（页面数值）。时间轴把 b 从 1 推到上限。K₂,ₘ 在 m = 5 就被 (1, 3) 挡住，V∖{a} 从 m = 11 起挡住；K₃,₄ 也离开核，但赤字只有 0.002。小图模式穷举全部 2ⁿ 个联盟，直接核对这些结论；6 个顶点以内的所有图都在核里（数值穷举，容差 10⁻⁹，在测试里核对，不是定理）。
  5. 边界。定理只说 m ≥ 50 时 K₂,ₘ 离开核。最优门槛、赤字是否无界（数值上约 0.036√m）、哪些图类一定在核里，都不在定理之内。论文里另一种分配——顶点能量——已被证明总在核里，页面在小图上同时画出它作对照。

原文：问题卷宗 · ArizmendiEnergyGameShapleyRefutation.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · Arizmendi、Arizmendi，Discrete Appl. Math. 330（2023） · arXiv:2012.14114

Treat the vertices of a graph as players. A set of vertices (a coalition) S is worth the energy 𝓔(G[S]) of the subgraph it induces, the sum of the absolute values of its adjacency eigenvalues. The Shapley value shares out the worth of the whole graph as each player's average increment over all orders of joining. Arizmendi and Arizmendi conjectured that it always lies in the core — no coalition can complain that it receives less than its own worth. This library gives a counterexample in Lean.

  1. On complete bipartite graphs a coalition is two numbers. In K_{a,b}, a coalition with r vertices on the scarce side and k on the other induces K_{r,k}, of energy 2√(rk) (a frozen lemma). Vertices on one side are symmetric, so the Shapley value takes two values: φ_A on the scarce side and φ_B on the other.
  2. The deficit landscape. The deficit of coalition (r, k) is 2√(rk) − (rφ_A + kφ_B). The core requires every deficit to be ≤ 0. The 3D view draws each r as a curve in k; magenta marks a positive deficit — a coalition that blocks the Shapley allocation. The vertical axis uses an asinh scale so that small positive deficits stay visible.
  3. The frozen counterexample. In K₂,ₘ a scarce vertex a has a large average increment: it is joined to every vertex of the other side, so when it joins it adds 2√(2k) − 2√k or 2√k. Lean proves φₐ > 2(√2 − 1)√m for m ≥ 50; by efficiency V∖{a} receives 2√(2m) − φₐ < 2√m = w(V∖{a}).
  4. Earlier counterexamples (page numbers). The timeline pushes b from 1 to its limit. K₂,ₘ is blocked by (1, 3) already at m = 5, and V∖{a} blocks from m = 11; K₃,₄ also leaves the core, with a deficit of only 0.002. The small-graph mode enumerates all 2ⁿ coalitions to check these directly; every graph on at most 6 vertices stays in the core (a numerical enumeration with tolerance 10⁻⁹, run in the tests, not a theorem).
  5. Limits. The theorem only says that K₂,ₘ leaves the core for m ≥ 50. The optimal threshold, whether the deficit is unbounded (numerically about 0.036√m), and which graph classes always stay in the core are outside it. The paper’s other allocation — the vertex energies — is proved to lie in the core, and the page draws it beside the Shapley value on small graphs for comparison.
