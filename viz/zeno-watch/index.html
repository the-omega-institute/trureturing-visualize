一个量子比特被恒定的 Rabi 驱动从 |0⟩ 推向 |1⟩，不去管它，经过 T = π/Ω 就完整翻转。现在每隔 δ = T/N 刷新一次：让一个记录装置与它作用，看它是否还在 |0⟩。刷新越密，翻转越难发生——这就是量子芝诺效应。页面上的公式都在下面逐条列出出处。

  1. 来源。Misra 与 Sudarshan 在 1977 年给出“被观察的系统不衰变”的数学形式（J. Math. Phys. 18, 756）。Itano、Heinzen、Bollinger、Wineland 在 1990 年用囚禁离子做了“π 脉冲期间测 N 次”的实验，N 最多到 64（Phys. Rev. A 41, 2295）；ITANO-1990 预设复现这个协议的理论预言 P1(T) = (1 − cosN(π/N))/2，不包含实验数据点。
  2. 刷新是一次物理交互（理论卷 §222）。记录交互把 |0⟩、|1⟩ 分别写进记录态 |m0⟩、|m1⟩，重叠 ⟨m1|m0⟩ = η。不读记录，系统也经历相位阻尼：非对角元乘以 η。这正是冻结定理 trace_environment_controlled_record_eq_phase_damping 的内容；多次记录的累积重叠控制相干存活，见 record_action_controls_coherence_survival。同一个通道也可以由随机相位踢实现（以概率 (1 − η)/2 施加 Z），它不留下任何可读结果（定理 222.2）。所以“单次运行”在 η = 0 时画成投影测量的跳变，在 η > 0 时画成随机相位踢；两者的系综平均都与精确的系综演化一致。
  3. 冻结的定量界（§223）。每段时间 δ 内从 |0⟩ 翻到 |1⟩ 的概率是 q = sin²(Ωδ/2)。N 次全部仍在 |0⟩ 的概率为 (1 − q)N，而至少一次被测到离开的概率满足 P离开 ≤ κ²T²/N，其中 κ = ‖QHP‖/ħ = Ω/2。N 翻倍，界减半。系综在 T 时刻处于 |1⟩ 的概率是 (1 − zN)/2，理想投影时 zN = cosN(Ωδ)。
  4. 决定效果的是“间隔 × 强度”（§225）。每次固定 η 时，N → ∞ 等于把总监视强度推向无穷，系统冻结。若每次强度随间隔缩小，η = e−γδ，N → ∞ 收敛到连续弱测量：z̈ + γż + Ω²z = 0；γ ≫ Ω 时慢衰减率约为 Ω²/γ，跃迁被拖慢却没有冻结。“分成无限多小步”与“监视强度无限大”不是同一个极限。
  5. 稳定与学习的权衡（§226，CONE_PROGRAM_FORMAL §5.4）。理想投影时记录序列的熵率为 h2(q)/δ（自然对数），在 Ωδ ≈ 0.9518 处最大（约 0.5398 Ω），δ → 0 时趋于零；完整记录关于 Ω 的 Fisher 信息为 T²/N，随刷新次数下降。看得越勤，记录越单调，能学到的驱动信息越少。熵率趋零也不表示刷新本身免费（§229）。
  6. 拖动（§227）。没有哈密顿量，只让测量方向从 |0⟩ 缓慢转到 cos Θ|0⟩ + sin Θ|1⟩，每步检查是否跟上。全部跟上的概率是 cos2N(Θ/N)，且 1 − p ≤ Θ²/N：测量路径本身就能把态带走。失败分支并没有消失，它们在记录带上以红色出现。
  7. 反芝诺（§228）。对一个向环境衰变的不稳定态，周期测量把衰变率变成环境谱 J(ω) 与滤波函数 Fτ(ω) = (τ/2π) sinc²((ω − ω0)τ/2) 的重叠：Γ(τ) = 2π∫J Fτ dω（弱耦合领先阶）。本页取洛伦兹谱，峰在 ωc、宽 Λ，积分有闭式解。谱峰与能级对齐时只有芝诺抑制；谱峰失谐时，适中的测量频率把滤波展宽到谱峰上，衰变反而加快，即反芝诺（Kofman 与 Kurizki，Nature 405, 546 (2000)；冷原子实验 Fischer、Gutiérrez-Medina、Raizen，PRL 87, 040402 (2001)）。并非任何测量加速的衰变都该叫反芝诺：仪器直接驱动或额外耗散也会加速，需要对照实验分开。
  8. 模型边界与证据状态。本页以 Ω = 1（反芝诺模式以 Λ = 1）为单位，测量瞬时、理想；反芝诺模式取耦合 G = 0.02 Λ 的弱耦合领先阶与洛伦兹谱。§223–§228 的界和公式是理论卷的纸面证明，理论卷自己写明这批 Zeno 结论没有运行 Lean 编译；本页把它们标为模型计算，LEAN ✓ 只标在三个已冻结的定理上。§229 的资源界（形成正交记录需要非零作用时间）没有进入本页模型。

A qubit is pushed from |0⟩ towards |1⟩ by a constant Rabi drive; left alone it flips completely after T = π/Ω. Now refresh it every δ = T/N: let a recording device interact with it and check whether it is still in |0⟩. The denser the refreshes, the harder it is for the flip to happen: this is the quantum Zeno effect. Each formula on the page is traced to its source below.

  1. Origins. Misra and Sudarshan gave the mathematical form of “an observed system does not decay” in 1977 (J. Math. Phys. 18, 756). Itano, Heinzen, Bollinger and Wineland ran the “measure N times during a π pulse” experiment with trapped ions in 1990, with N up to 64 (Phys. Rev. A 41, 2295); the ITANO-1990 preset reproduces that protocol’s theoretical prediction P1(T) = (1 − cosN(π/N))/2 and contains no experimental data points.
  2. A refresh is a physical interaction (theory volume §222). The recording interaction writes |0⟩ and |1⟩ into record states |m0⟩ and |m1⟩ with overlap ⟨m1|m0⟩ = η. Even if the record is never read, the system is phase-damped: its off-diagonal entries are multiplied by η. This is the frozen theorem trace_environment_controlled_record_eq_phase_damping; the cumulative overlap of many records controls the surviving coherence, see record_action_controls_coherence_survival. The same channel can also be produced by random phase kicks (Z applied with probability (1 − η)/2), which leave no readable outcome at all (Theorem 222.2). So a “single run” is drawn as projective jumps when η = 0 and as random phase kicks when η > 0; both average to the exact ensemble evolution.
  3. A quantitative freezing bound (§223). Within each interval δ the probability of flipping from |0⟩ to |1⟩ is q = sin²(Ωδ/2). The chance of finding |0⟩ all N times is (1 − q)N, and the chance of catching at least one departure obeys Pleave ≤ κ²T²/N with κ = ‖QHP‖/ħ = Ω/2. Doubling N halves the bound. The ensemble’s probability of being in |1⟩ at time T is (1 − zN)/2, with zN = cosN(Ωδ) for ideal projections.
  4. What matters is “interval × strength” (§225). With a fixed η each time, N → ∞ drives the total monitoring strength to infinity and the system freezes. If each refresh weakens as the interval shrinks, η = e−γδ, then N → ∞ converges to continuous weak measurement: z̈ + γż + Ω²z = 0; for γ ≫ Ω the slow decay rate is about Ω²/γ, so the transition is slowed but not frozen. “Infinitely many small steps” and “infinitely strong monitoring” are not the same limit.
  5. A trade-off between stability and learning (§226, CONE_PROGRAM_FORMAL §5.4). With ideal projections the record sequence has entropy rate h2(q)/δ (natural log), largest near Ωδ ≈ 0.9518 (about 0.5398 Ω) and going to zero as δ → 0; the Fisher information of the full record about Ω is T²/N, which falls as refreshes are added. The more often you look, the more monotonous the record and the less it can teach about the drive. A vanishing entropy rate does not make refreshing free (§229).
  6. Dragging (§227). With no Hamiltonian at all, turn the measurement direction slowly from |0⟩ to cos Θ|0⟩ + sin Θ|1⟩ and check at each step whether the state followed. The chance that it followed every time is cos2N(Θ/N), with 1 − p ≤ Θ²/N: the measurement path alone can carry the state away. The failed branches do not disappear; they show up in red on the record tape.
  7. Anti-Zeno (§228). For an unstable state decaying into an environment, periodic measurement turns the decay rate into the overlap of the environment spectrum J(ω) with the filter Fτ(ω) = (τ/2π) sinc²((ω − ω0)τ/2): Γ(τ) = 2π∫J Fτ dω (leading order in weak coupling). This page uses a Lorentzian spectrum peaked at ωc with width Λ, for which the integral has a closed form. When the peak sits on the level there is only Zeno suppression; when it is detuned, a moderate measurement rate widens the filter onto the peak and the decay speeds up: the anti-Zeno effect (Kofman and Kurizki, Nature 405, 546 (2000); cold-atom experiment by Fischer, Gutiérrez-Medina and Raizen, PRL 87, 040402 (2001)). Not every measurement-induced speed-up deserves the name: an apparatus that drives the transition or adds dissipation also speeds decay, and control experiments are needed to tell them apart.
  8. Model limits and status of the evidence. The page uses Ω = 1 (Λ = 1 in anti-Zeno mode) as the unit, with instantaneous ideal measurements; the anti-Zeno mode uses the weak-coupling leading order with coupling G = 0.02 Λ and a Lorentzian spectrum. The bounds and formulas of §223–§228 are paper proofs in the theory volume, which states itself that this batch of Zeno results was not compiled in Lean; the page labels them as model calculations, and LEAN ✓ marks only the three frozen theorems. The resource bound of §229 (forming orthogonal records takes non-zero interaction time) is not part of this page’s model.
