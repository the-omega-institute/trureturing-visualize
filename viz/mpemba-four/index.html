The conjecture, claim: for N ≥ 3, strictly increasing energies, positive detailed-balance rates and every triplet violating (3), there is no Mpemba effect. This library proves ¬claim. ARGUED卷宗里的论证（未形式化）：b₂ 除常数 μ 外就是 g 的吉布斯平均。初始温度升高时，下面三个能级先拉平，g 的平均趋向 (0 + 1 + 2/3)/3 = 5/9；顶层要到高得多的温度才被填上，它的分量 8/15 小于 5/9，把平均拉向 11/20。所以 b₂ 先升后降，中间有最大值。三能级条件只看到本三元组的速率与能隙，而效应来自四个能级共同的慢模。The dossier’s argument (not formalized): up to the constant μ, b₂ is the Gibbs mean of g. As the initial temperature rises, the three lower levels equalize first and the mean of g approaches (0 + 1 + 2/3)/3 = 5/9; the top level fills only at a much higher temperature, and its component 8/15 lies below 5/9, pulling the mean toward 11/20. So b₂ rises and then falls, with an interior maximum. Condition (3) on a triplet sees only that triplet’s rates and gaps, while the effect comes from the joint slow mode of all four levels.
ARGUED本页的论证（未形式化、未经评审）：在 β = 0 处，db₂/dβ = −ln 16 · Cov(g, ℓ)，协方差取四个能级上的均匀分布，ℓ = (0, 1, 2, n) 是能级编号；算出来 Cov = (41 − n)/240。所以 n ≥ 42 时 b₂ 从无穷高温往下先上升，而 b₂(0) = 11/20 − μ > 0、b₂(β_b) = 0，中间必有最大值：比最大点更热的初态慢模系数更小，姆潘巴效应出现。n ≤ 41 时这个斜率不为正；页面扫描没有看到驼峰。The page’s argument (not formalized, not reviewed): at β = 0, db₂/dβ = −ln 16 · Cov(g, ℓ), the covariance taken over the uniform distribution on the four levels with level numbers ℓ = (0, 1, 2, n); it equals (41 − n)/240. So for n ≥ 42, b₂ first rises as the temperature drops from infinity, while b₂(0) = 11/20 − μ > 0 and b₂(β_b) = 0, so there is an interior maximum: states hotter than the maximum have the smaller slow-mode coefficient, and the Mpemba effect appears. For n ≤ 41 the slope is not positive, and the page’s scan sees no hump.
OPEN定理之外：重新计算等效速率之后，某种局部的三能级条件是否是效应的必要条件；在附加假设（例如能级间距有界）下猜想是否成立。Outside the theorem: whether some local triplet condition with recomputed effective rates is necessary for the effect; whether the conjecture holds under additional hypotheses, for example bounded level spacing.

姆潘巴效应：两杯水放进同一个冰箱，热的那杯反而先结冰。在马尔可夫模型里，一个 N 能级系统按速率矩阵向浴温 β_b 弛豫；从温度 β 的平衡态出发，长时间后离平衡的距离主要由慢模系数 b₂ = ℓ·Π(β) 决定。更热的初态 b₂ 更小，就会后来居上，先到平衡附近。Avitan 等人对三能级系统给出了必要条件 (3)，并猜想：如果 N 能级系统的每个三能级子系统都不满足 (3)，就不会有姆潘巴效应。

  1. 反例。四个能级，能量 0、ln 16、2 ln 16 与 64 ln 16，浴温 β_b = 1。四个三能级子系统都不满足 (3)，最慢的模是 −1 且是单的；可是 x = 16^(−β) 取 1023/1024（更热）时 b₂ ≈ 0.4890371，取 127/128 时 b₂ ≈ 0.4892979——更热的反而更小。
  2. 为什么。升温时下面三个能级先拉平，顶层到很高的温度才被填上，而顶层的分量把 b₂ 往下拉，于是 b₂ 随温度先升后降。三能级检验看不到四个能级共同的慢模。
  3. 三维视图。左边一列从热的初态出发，右边一列从温的初态出发，每个圆盘是一个能级，上面的粒子数正比于占据概率；顶层画在折断的能量轴上方。时间轴推进弛豫，下方左图是 b₂ 随初始温度的驼峰，右图是两个距离之比，过零就是两条曲线交叉。

The Mpemba effect: put two cups of water in the same freezer and the hotter one freezes first. In a Markov model, an N-level system relaxes to the bath β_b under a rate matrix; starting from equilibrium at temperature β, its distance from equilibrium at long times is governed by the slow-mode coefficient b₂ = ℓ·Π(β). If the hotter start has the smaller b₂, it overtakes and gets close to equilibrium first. Avitan et al. gave a necessary condition (3) for three-level systems and conjectured that an N-level system whose every triplet violates (3) shows no Mpemba effect.

  1. The counterexample. Four levels with energies 0, ln 16, 2 ln 16 and 64 ln 16, bath β_b = 1. Every triplet violates (3) and the slow mode −1 is simple; yet at x = 16^(−β) = 1023/1024 (hotter) b₂ ≈ 0.4890371, and at 127/128 b₂ ≈ 0.4892979 — the hotter start has the smaller coefficient.
  2. Why. As the temperature rises the lower three levels equalize first, the top level fills only at a much higher temperature, and the top level’s component pulls b₂ down, so b₂ first rises and then falls. A triplet test cannot see the slow mode shared by all four levels.
  3. 3D view. The left column starts hot and the right column warm; each disc is an energy level, with a number of particles proportional to its occupation, and the top level sits above a broken energy axis. The time rail advances the relaxation; the left chart below is the hump of b₂ against the initial temperature, and the right chart is the ratio of the two distances, whose sign change is the crossing of the curves.
