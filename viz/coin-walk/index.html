LEAN ✓证明的步骤（同一个冻结模块）：上确界可以取到（overlap_bounded、overlap_attained），并且只依赖各模概率（phase_invariance）；两个模的显式最大值（two_factor_max）给出：保持一对概率之和不变、把它们拉平，gMax 不会变大（pair_balancing）；行走关于硬币是线性的（walk_linearity），两个基硬币的演化 u、w 是实的并满足反射关系 u(1, −x) = −(−1)ⁿ w(0, x)、w(1, −x) = (−1)ⁿ u(0, x)（walk_reflection）；于是 c* 在每对 (0, x)、(1, −x) 上概率相等，且对和与任何单位硬币相同（walk_pair_probabilities）；逐对拉平得到结论（pair_average_le）。冻结：The steps of the proof (in the same frozen module): the supremum is attained (overlap_bounded, overlap_attained) and depends only on the mode probabilities (phase_invariance); the explicit two-mode maximum (two_factor_max) shows that evening out a pair of probabilities at a fixed sum cannot increase gMax (pair_balancing); the walk is linear in the coin (walk_linearity), and the evolutions u, w of the two basis coins are real with the reflection relations u(1, −x) = −(−1)ⁿ w(0, x) and w(1, −x) = (−1)ⁿ u(0, x) (walk_reflection); so c* has equal probabilities on every pair (0, x), (1, −x), with the same pair sums as any unit coin (walk_pair_probabilities); evening out pair by pair gives the result (pair_average_le). 一个光子在 P 个格点的圆环上做量子行走：它带着一个二维“硬币”，每一步先用 Hadamard 混合硬币，再按硬币的两个分量分别往左、往右走一格。走了 n 步之后，光子以叠加的方式分布在 2P 个光学模上。这种分布离“各模各自独立”有多远，就是几何纠缠 E_g。原文问：初始硬币怎样选，纠缠最大？

  1. 答案。Lean 证明：对每个圆环大小、每个时刻，(1, i)/√2 都给出最大的 E_g。原文在他们试过的所有大小和时刻都观察到这一点。
  2. 为什么。模 (0, x) 和 (1, −x) 组成一对。行走对硬币是线性的，而且两个基硬币的演化互为反射；结果是：无论初始硬币是什么，每一对的概率之和都一样，而 c* 恰好把每一对平分。把一对的概率拉平只会让态离乘积态更远——这一步由两个模的显式最大值证明。
  3. Bloch 球。左边的球上每一点是一个初始硬币，颜色是它在当前时刻的 E_g。最亮处在 c*（琥珀点），以及它的镜像 (1, −i)/√2——最大值并不唯一。
  4. 圆环。右边每个格点有两根柱子，青色是分量 0、品红是分量 1，白色短线是 c* 的值。地面上的弦连起反射对；所选硬币让某一对不平衡时，弦变成品红。

原文：问题卷宗 · SymmetricCoinMaximalWalkEntanglement.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.24679 · arXiv:2603.24679

One photon performs a quantum walk on a circle of P sites. It carries a two-dimensional “coin”; each step mixes the coin with the Hadamard matrix and then moves the two coin components one site left and one site right. After n steps the photon is spread in superposition over 2P optical modes. How far this spread is from “every mode on its own” is the geometric entanglement E_g. The source asks: which initial coin gives the most entanglement?

  1. The answer. Lean proves that (1, i)/√2 gives the largest E_g for every circle size at every time. The source observed this for every size and time it tried.
  2. Why. The modes (0, x) and (1, −x) form a pair. The walk is linear in the coin, and the evolutions of the two basis coins are reflections of each other. As a result every pair has the same total probability whatever the initial coin, and c* splits each pair evenly. Evening out a pair can only move the state further from product states — this step is proved from the explicit two-mode maximum.
  3. The Bloch sphere. Each point of the sphere on the left is an initial coin, coloured by its E_g at the current time. The brightest spots are c* (the amber dot) and its mirror image (1, −i)/√2 — the maximum is not unique.
  4. The circle. On the right each site has two bars, cyan for component 0 and magenta for component 1, with white ticks for the values from c*. Chords on the floor join the reflection pairs; when the chosen coin leaves a pair unbalanced its chord turns magenta.
