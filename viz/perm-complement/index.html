一个 n × n 的置换矩阵在每行每列各有一个 1。两个置换矩阵强互补，是说它们的 1 不在同一位置，并且满足一个四指标条件。Rajchel、Gąsiorowski 与 Życzkowski 用它们在 Birkhoff 多面体里构造单随机的三角形；支撑不相交说明族最多 n 个，他们问最大值究竟怎样依赖 n。

  1. 答案。Lean 证明最大值是 2^v₂(n)：n 里有多少个因子 2，就能有 2 的那么多次方个。n = 8 时是 8，n = 12 时只有 4，n 为奇数时只有 1。
  2. 为什么。把族平移到包含恒等，其余成员都是两两交换、没有不动点的对合，生成一个 2 群；族的大小不超过它的一个轨道，而最小的轨道大小 2^a 整除 n。平移族——只对前 a 位二进制做异或——正好达到这个数。
  3. 三维视图。每一层是族里的一个置换矩阵，从下往上加入；地面上是它们的投影，永不重叠。n 为 2 的幂时，最后一层加入后地面被铺满：这是一个拉丁方。下方右图把最新成员相对于恒等的置换画成配对弧——每个点恰好配一个对象，没有不动点。

原文：问题卷宗 · StronglyComplementaryPermutationMaximum.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · doi:10.1007/s11786-018-0384-y · arXiv:1804.10715

An n × n permutation matrix has one 1 in every row and every column. Two permutation matrices are strongly complementary when their 1s never share a position and a four-index condition holds. Rajchel, Gąsiorowski and Życzkowski use them to build unistochastic triangles in the Birkhoff polytope; disjoint supports allow at most n of them, and they ask how the maximum actually depends on n.

  1. The answer. Lean proves the maximum is 2^v₂(n): as many as the largest power of two dividing n. For n = 8 it is 8, for n = 12 only 4, and for odd n only 1.
  2. Why. Shift the family to contain the identity; the other members are then pairwise commuting fixed-point-free involutions and generate a 2-group. A family is no larger than one of its orbits, and the smallest orbit size 2^a divides n. The translation family — XOR on the first a binary digits — attains this number.
  3. 3D view. Each layer is one permutation matrix of the family, added from the bottom up; their shadows on the floor never overlap. When n is a power of two the floor is filled once the last layer arrives: a Latin square. The lower-right chart draws the newest member relative to the identity as pairing arcs — every point is paired with exactly one other, with no fixed point.
