LEAN ✓设 Z(m) 是正整数 m 的 Zeckendorf 展开里出现的 Fibonacci 下标集合（下标 ≥ 2、两两不相邻）。a(1) = 1；a(n+1) 是尚未出现、且 |Z(m) ∩ Z(a(n))| = 1 的最小正整数 m。OEIS A370630 的记录猜想它是正整数的一个排列。本库证明：每一步都存在合格的后继，a(n+1) 确实是其中最小的，各项互不相同，每个正整数都出现在某一步。已冻结：Let Z(m) be the set of Fibonacci indices (all ≥ 2, no two adjacent) in the Zeckendorf expansion of a positive integer m. a(1) = 1, and a(n+1) is the least positive integer m not yet used with |Z(m) ∩ Z(a(n))| = 1. The OEIS record A370630 conjectures that this is a permutation of the positive integers. This library proves that every step has an eligible successor, that a(n+1) is indeed the least of them, that the terms are distinct, and that every positive integer appears at some step. LEAN ✓证明的骨架是同一模块里一起冻结的私有引理：取一个足够远的新 Fibonacci 下标，与当前项的某个下标配对，就得到一个比已用值之和还大的合格值，所以链永不中断；单射来自“只取未用过的数”；若某个正整数永远缺席，每当它合格，最小性就把下一项压在它下面，它若无限次合格便与单射矛盾；大下标的“记录对”迫使某个固定下标成为“中枢”（含它的数都会被访问），中枢再沿新的三项展开传播到所有下标。冻结（模块内引理）：The skeleton of the proof is a set of private lemmas frozen with the module: pairing a sufficiently distant fresh Fibonacci index with an index of the current term gives an eligible value larger than the sum of all used values, so the chain never stops; injectivity comes from taking only unused numbers; if some positive integer were missing forever, minimality would put the next term below it whenever it is eligible, which contradicts injectivity if it is eligible infinitely often; record pairs of large indices force some fixed index to be a “hub” (every number containing it is visited), and hubs propagate to every index through fresh three-term expansions. Frozen (lemmas inside the module): exists_next · v_injective · record_pairs · pair_forces_two · root_hub · hub_propagates · every_positive

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

每个正整数都能唯一地写成若干个两两不相邻的 Fibonacci 数之和（Zeckendorf 展开），例如 11 = 8 + 3 = F(6) + F(4)。A370630 从 1 出发，每一步都贪心地取最小的、还没用过、并且与上一项恰好共享一个 Fibonacci 项的数。它会不会卡住？会不会漏掉某个数？本库在 Lean 里证明：都不会。

  1. 数字织锦。三维视图里横向是步数 n，纵深是 Fibonacci 下标 k，每个点表示 F(k) 出现在 a(n) 的展开里。相邻两步共享的那一项用琥珀线连起来——按定义每一步恰有一条。俯视时它像一块织物，经线是长时间被延续的高位项。
  2. 不会卡住（冻结）。取一个比所有已用下标都远的新下标 j，再取当前项的某个下标 i，F(i) + F(j) 与当前项恰共享 F(i)，且比所有已用值都大，所以合格的后继总是存在。
  3. 不会漏掉（冻结）。如果某个数 m 永远不出现，那么每当它在某一步合格，贪心选出的下一项就不超过 m；若 m 无限次合格，就有无限多个互不相同的项不超过 m，矛盾。证明的难点是说明 m 确实无限次合格：大下标的“记录对”迫使某个下标成为“中枢”，再传播到所有下标。
  4. 页面上的读数。左下图是 a(n) − n：链贴着对角线前进，偶尔向前跳、向后补。右下图是最小未出现数 m*(n)，冻结定理保证它趋于无穷；对每个已出现的 m，等待时间 τ(m) − m 是有限的。
  5. 边界。定理不给出任何定量的访问界。规则切换到“至少一项”（A370631）或“二进制恰一位”（A226077）只是数值对照，那些序列的排列性质不在本库定理之内。

原文：问题卷宗 · ZeckendorfGreedyPermutation.lean · Blueprint · OEIS A370630 · A370631 · A226077

Every positive integer is uniquely a sum of Fibonacci numbers, no two adjacent (its Zeckendorf expansion), for example 11 = 8 + 3 = F(6) + F(4). A370630 starts at 1 and at every step greedily takes the least number not yet used that shares exactly one Fibonacci term with the previous one. Can it get stuck? Can it skip a number for ever? This library proves in Lean that neither happens.

  1. The digit weave. In the 3D view, across is the step n and depth is the Fibonacci index k; each point means that F(k) occurs in the expansion of a(n). The term shared by consecutive steps is joined by an amber line — by definition there is exactly one per step. From above it looks like a woven cloth whose warp threads are high terms carried over many steps.
  2. It never gets stuck (frozen). Take a fresh index j farther than every index used so far and an index i of the current term: F(i) + F(j) shares exactly F(i) with the current term and is larger than every used value, so an eligible successor always exists.
  3. It skips nothing (frozen). If some number m never appeared, then whenever m is eligible the next term chosen by the greedy rule is at most m; if m were eligible infinitely often, infinitely many distinct terms would be at most m, a contradiction. The hard part is showing that m is indeed eligible infinitely often: record pairs of large indices force some index to become a “hub”, which then propagates to every index.
  4. The readings. The lower left chart is a(n) − n: the chain runs close to the diagonal, sometimes jumping ahead and later filling in behind. The lower right chart is the smallest number not yet seen, m*(n), which the frozen theorem sends to infinity; for every m already seen the waiting time τ(m) − m is finite.
  5. Limits. The theorem gives no quantitative visiting bound. Switching the rule to “at least one term” (A370631) or “exactly one binary bit” (A226077) is a numerical comparison only; the permutation property of those sequences is outside this library’s theorem.
