LEAN ✓一层 QAOA 态为 ψ = e^(−iβB) e^(−iγC)|+⟩⊗ⁿ，B = Σⱼ Xⱼ，C = diag(e)。Streif 与 Leib 断言：支撑（振幅不为零的基态）大小严格介于 2 与 2ⁿ 之间是不可能的。本库证明这个不加限制的断言不成立：n = 3，能量 e 在前两位不同的 4 个基态上为 π/2、其余为 2π，β = π/8，γ = 1 时，前两位相同的 4 个基态振幅恰为 0，另 4 个（010、011、100、101）振幅都等于 −(1 + i)e^(iπ/8)/√8，不为零；2 < 4 < 2³。已冻结：The level-one QAOA state is ψ = e^(−iβB) e^(−iγC)|+⟩⊗ⁿ with B = Σⱼ Xⱼ and C = diag(e). Streif and Leib asserted that a support (the basis states with nonzero amplitude) of size strictly between 2 and 2ⁿ is impossible. This library proves the unrestricted assertion false: for n = 3, the energy e equal to π/2 on the 4 basis states whose first two bits differ and 2π on the others, β = π/8 and γ = 1, the 4 basis states with equal first two bits have amplitude exactly 0 and the other 4 (010, 011, 100, 101) all have amplitude −(1 + i)e^(iπ/8)/√8 ≠ 0; 2 < 4 < 2³. Frozen: LevelOneQAOAGenuineSuperposition.result
LEAN ✓证明的骨架是同一模块里一起冻结的私有引理：混合算子把全 1 向量乘 3、把前两位的奇偶向量乘 −1，于是在 e^(−iβB) 下它们分别得到相位 e^(−3iβ) 与 e^(iβ)；相位层把 |+⟩⊗³ 写成这两个向量的组合；在 β = π/8 处 e^(−3iπ/8) = −i·e^(iπ/8)，前两位相同的分量恰好抵消。冻结（模块内引理）：The skeleton of the proof is a set of private lemmas frozen with the module: the mixer multiplies the all-ones vector by 3 and the parity vector of the first two bits by −1, so under e^(−iβB) they pick up the phases e^(−3iβ) and e^(iβ); the phase layer writes |+⟩⊗³ as a combination of these two vectors; at β = π/8, e^(−3iπ/8) = −i·e^(iπ/8), and the components with equal first two bits cancel exactly. Frozen (lemmas inside the module): mixer_uniform · mixer_parity · mixer_evolution · output_amplitudes · genuine_output

OPEN定理之外：Lean 的命题不要求目标态是能量的基态，论文的受限表述（目标为基态、配合其必要条件）是否同样被推翻不在定理之内；论文把混合角限制为 π/4 的奇数倍，这个限制下的排除是否成立仍未解决；更大 n、其他支撑大小的反例也未形式化。Outside the theorem: Lean’s claim does not require the target states to be ground states of the energy, so whether the paper’s restricted formulation (ground-state targets together with its necessary condition) also fails is not settled; whether the exclusion holds when the mixing angle is restricted to odd multiples of π/4, as in the paper, remains open; counterexamples for larger n or other support sizes are not formalized.

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

一层 QAOA 先给每个基态 |x⟩ 乘上相位 e^(−iγ e(x))，再让每个量子比特绕 X 轴转 2β。输出态在哪些基态上振幅不为零——它的“支撑”——取决于这些相位怎样干涉。Streif 与 Leib 断言支撑不可能恰好有 3 到 2ⁿ − 1 个基态；本库在 Lean 里给出 3 个量子比特、支撑为 4 的反例。

  1. 振幅。ψ(x) = 2^(−3/2) Σ_y (cos β)^(3 − d) (−i sin β)^d e^(−iγ e(y))，d 是 x 与 y 不同的比特数。每个 ψ(x) 是 (β, γ) 的复函数；它为零要两个实方程同时成立，所以零点一般是平面上的孤立点。
  2. 冻结的反例。Lean 的能量只看前两位是否相同，于是 |+⟩⊗³ 经相位层后是全 1 向量与奇偶向量的组合，它们是混合算子的特征向量（特征值 3 与 −1）。β = π/8、γ = 1 时，前两位相同的 4 个分量恰好抵消，剩下 4 个振幅相同，概率各 1/4。
  3. 地形。三维视图的地面是 (β, γ) 平面（β ∈ [0, π]，γ ∈ [0, 2π]），高度是 8 个振幅里最小的 |ψ(x)| 的对数；向下的尖刺就是零点，品红点是牛顿法收敛到的零点，标出那里的支撑大小。琥珀线是当前的 γ，琥珀点是当前的 (β, γ)。右上方的立方体是 8 个基态，柱高是概率。
  4. 别的能量。三角形 MaxCut（e = 切边数）在 β = π/4、γ = π/2 处只剩 |000⟩、|111⟩（支撑 2，论文允许的情形），在 (0.3077, 0.6155) 附近消去这两个、剩下 6 个；没有对称性的能量只在孤立点让单个振幅为零（支撑 7）。这些都是页面数值，不是定理。
  5. 边界。Lean 的命题去掉了“目标态是基态”的限制；论文在那种限制和 β 只取 π/4 奇数倍的假设下的结论，不在定理之内。

Level-one QAOA first multiplies each basis state |x⟩ by the phase e^(−iγ e(x)) and then rotates every qubit by 2β about the X axis. Which basis states end up with nonzero amplitude — the “support” of the output — depends on how these phases interfere. Streif and Leib asserted that the support cannot have between 3 and 2ⁿ − 1 basis states; this library gives a three-qubit counterexample with support 4 in Lean.

  1. Amplitudes. ψ(x) = 2^(−3/2) Σ_y (cos β)^(3 − d) (−i sin β)^d e^(−iγ e(y)), where d is the number of bits in which x and y differ. Each ψ(x) is a complex function of (β, γ); a zero needs two real equations at once, so zeros are generically isolated points of the plane.
  2. The frozen counterexample. Lean’s energy only looks at whether the first two bits agree, so after the phase layer |+⟩⊗³ is a combination of the all-ones vector and the parity vector, eigenvectors of the mixer with eigenvalues 3 and −1. At β = π/8 and γ = 1 the 4 components with equal first two bits cancel exactly; the remaining 4 amplitudes are equal, each with probability 1/4.
  3. The landscape. The floor of the 3D view is the (β, γ) plane (β ∈ [0, π], γ ∈ [0, 2π]) and the height is the logarithm of the smallest of the 8 amplitudes |ψ(x)|; downward spikes are zeros, and the magenta points are zeros reached by Newton's method, labelled with the support size there. The amber line is the current γ and the amber point the current (β, γ). The cube at the upper right holds the 8 basis states, with bar heights equal to the probabilities.
  4. Other energies. The triangle MaxCut energy (e = the number of cut edges) keeps only |000⟩ and |111⟩ at β = π/4, γ = π/2 (support 2, a case the paper allows), and removes exactly these two near (0.3077, 0.6155), leaving 6; an energy without symmetry makes single amplitudes vanish at isolated points (support 7). These are page numbers, not theorems.
  5. Limits. Lean’s claim drops the requirement that the targets are ground states; the paper’s conclusion under that restriction and under the assumption that β is an odd multiple of π/4 is outside the theorem.
