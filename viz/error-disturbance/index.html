LEAN ✓测量基由酉矩阵的列给出，Pᵢ 是第 i 列的投影（measurementProjector）。在态 ρ 上，A′ 测 A 的误差是 maxᵢ |tr ρ(P_i^A − P_i^{A′})|（errorAt），A′ 对 B 的扰动是 maxⱼ |tr ρ(R_j − Σ_k Q_k R_j Q_k)|（disturbanceAt）；Δ(A, A′, B) 是同一个 ρ 上两者之和的上确界（overallError），ε、η 分别取各自的上确界（error、disturbance），ε_rel 再对 B 的结果重新编号取下确界（relabel、relaxedError）。猜想 claim：对所有 d ≥ 2 与所有酉矩阵 A、A′、B，min{ε_rel(A, B), η(A, B)} ≤ Δ(A, A′, B)。本库证明 ¬claim。已冻结：A measurement basis is given by the columns of a unitary, and Pᵢ projects onto column i (measurementProjector). In a state ρ, the error of A′ measuring A is maxᵢ |tr ρ(P_i^A − P_i^{A′})| (errorAt) and the disturbance of B by A′ is maxⱼ |tr ρ(R_j − Σ_k Q_k R_j Q_k)| (disturbanceAt); Δ(A, A′, B) is the supremum of their sum over one common ρ (overallError), ε and η take their own suprema (error, disturbance), and ε_rel also minimizes over relabelings of B’s outcomes (relabel, relaxedError). The conjecture, claim: for every d ≥ 2 and all unitaries A, A′, B, min{ε_rel(A, B), η(A, B)} ≤ Δ(A, A′, B). This library proves ¬claim. Frozen: ErrorDisturbanceEndpointRefutation.result

想先测量 A、再测量 B，但只能用另一个测量 A′ 代替 A：A′ 的结果与 A 的差别是误差，A′ 对随后 B 的统计的改变是扰动。两个极端很简单——A′ = A 不出错，但扰动 B 的代价是 η(A, B)；A′ = B 不扰动，但测 A 的误差是 ε(A, B)。Zhou、Wu 与 Chau 猜想：在同一个态上同时计入误差与扰动，最坏情形 Δ(A, A′, B) 永远不比两个端点中便宜的那个更低。

  1. 反例。三维里取 A = I，A′ = U 是绕第三根轴转一个小角度的有理正交矩阵，B = V 是另一个有理正交矩阵。Lean 用半正定证书证明 Δ(I, U, V) ≤ 0.284，又用两个具体的纯态证明两个端点代价都超过 0.285。中间的测量严格更便宜。
  2. 三维视图。灰色三条箭头是 A 的基（坐标轴），青色是 A′，琥珀是 B；品红是让误差加扰动最大的那个态（一条射线）。时间轴让 A′ 沿 A → U → B 走，左下图画 Δ 与那条平线 f。
  3. 证书。按谱公式，Δ ≤ s 等价于 36 个矩阵 sI − (±Eᵢ ± Dⱼ) 都半正定。右下图画出它们在 s = 71/250 时的最小特征值——在 U 处全都 ≥ 0，这正是 Lean 用 LDL 分解核对的东西。

原文：问题卷宗 · ErrorDisturbanceEndpointRefutation.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · Zhou、Wu、Chau，doi:10.1016/j.physleta.2016.03.046 · arXiv:1509.03812

We want to measure A and then B, but can only use another measurement A′ in place of A: how far A′’s outcomes are from A’s is the error, and how much A′ changes the statistics of the later B is the disturbance. The two extremes are simple — A′ = A makes no error but disturbs B at cost η(A, B); A′ = B does not disturb but measures A with error ε(A, B). Zhou, Wu and Chau conjectured that counting error and disturbance on one common state, the worst case Δ(A, A′, B) is never lower than the cheaper of the two endpoints.

  1. The counterexample. In dimension three take A = I, A′ = U, a rational orthogonal matrix that turns by a small angle about the third axis, and B = V, another rational orthogonal matrix. Lean proves Δ(I, U, V) ≤ 0.284 with positive semidefinite certificates, and shows with two explicit pure states that both endpoint costs exceed 0.285. The intermediate measurement is strictly cheaper.
  2. 3D view. The three grey arrows are A’s basis (the coordinate axes), cyan is A′ and amber is B; magenta is the state that maximizes error plus disturbance (a ray). The rail moves A′ along A → U → B, and the lower left chart draws Δ against the flat line f.
  3. The certificate. By the spectral formula, Δ ≤ s is equivalent to all 36 matrices sI − (±Eᵢ ± Dⱼ) being positive semidefinite. The lower right chart shows their smallest eigenvalues at s = 71/250 — at U all are ≥ 0, which is what Lean checks with LDL factorizations.
