理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

400 只猫各关在一只盒子里，盒里有一个会衰变的原子和一台盖革计数器。三维体的每条竖线是一只猫的整段历史（下方是过去，上方是 60 分钟后），亮点是计数器第一次响、毒药放出的时刻。整块画的是同一份记录档案；播放只移动“现在”，不改写任何一条记录。

  1. 猫就是首次探测协议（§11–§12）。计数器每隔 Δt 检查一次：没响就继续，第一次响就记下轮次 n 并停止。未点击算子 Q 与点击算子 L 满足 Q†Q + L†L = I；第 n 轮首次点击的概率与到第 N 轮仍未点击的概率加起来恰好为一（命题 11.2）。整段历史可以写成一个带停止标签的等距编码：活着的分支加上“死于第 1、2、3……轮”的各个分支，每支一个正交标签（命题 12.2）。BLOCK 模式看的就是这块整体。
  2. 观测分两类。检查间隔、衰变方式、暗态权重参与形成记录，改变死亡时刻的概率律，所以档案指纹会变。开箱时刻、看哪本账、用哪个钟、怎样条件化，只是读取已有记录：顺序概率满足 p(w) = Tr(ρ0Fw)（§8.2；迹表示已冻结为 word_probability_trace_representation），开箱不作用于原子，所以死亡时刻律与开箱早晚无关。页面沿用同一组随机种子，这些控件一个字节也不改档案。
  3. 检查本身会改变猫何时死（命题 11.3）。一直被检查时，“没响”之后的状态不等于没人检查时的自由演化。相干耦合下，检查越频繁，每次看到衰变的机会越小，猫活得越久（量子芝诺效应）；检查稀疏时反而死得更快。马尔可夫衰变下，死亡时刻律与检查间隔无关。两个模型在 Δt = 1 分钟时重合，半衰期都取 20 分钟。
  4. 暗态与永生分支（§13）。若原子有一部分处在任何检查都探测不到的暗空间里，长期存活概率就收敛到这部分的权重 w。这一条已冻结：finite_detection_survival_limit（暗空间的刻画见 dark_space_eq_survival_defect_kernel）。账本里分不出一只活猫是暗态还是尚未衰变；“活着的时空”面板给出的是条件概率。
  5. 为什么看不到半死半活（§5 与退相干）。猫的死活被 M 份副本记下：计数器、毒药瓶、猫的身体、空气分子、光子……每份副本的“活”版与“死”版重叠为 c，全部副本的重叠就是 κ = cM，这一条已冻结：repeated_record_exponential_decay。两支之间的相干 V = |κ|，记录的可分辨度 D = √(1 − |κ|²)，二者满足 D² + V² = 1，也已冻结：pure_record_distinguishability_coherence_complementarity。页面把 D 叫作“事实强度”：记录越弱，块里的死活颜色越淡。
  6. 事实相对于账本（OBSERVER-QUANTUM 第 8 节，GICT 观察 6.30）。盒内账本在猫死的那一刻就写下了事实，盒外账本要到开箱才读到。两本账都自洽，不必合并；已观测事件的绝对性从未被授予。开箱之后，观察者自己的记忆也成了一份理想记录（重叠 0）。这一节是理论卷的推导与数值证书，不是 Lean 定理。
  7. Wigner 撤销。盒外观察者原则上可以把全部副本相干地反演回去，这是理论卷明文允许的“合法反测量”。撤销后死活不再是任何账本里的事实，两支之间的干涉重新可测。页面假设每份副本的反演保真度为 f，残余重叠为 fM：这是示意假设，不是定理。对真实的猫，副本数是天文数字（页面最大取 1023），f 只要比 1 小一点点，撤销就等于不可能——实际的不可逆就来自这里。撤销只在按下的那一刻成立，时间一动就回到未撤销的历史，之后的新历史不做模拟。
  8. 非线性时间。实验室钟均匀流逝；账本钟只在写入记录时前进（OBSERVER-QUANTUM §18.2：时间是账本长度，因果结构是记录前缀的偏序）。账本钟不是实验室时间的单射：没有记录的时段被压成一点。在盒外账本里，开箱前的整段时间都压在开箱那一刻；撤销成功时，被抹掉的那段历史在账本钟上长度为零。
  9. 这个页面不解释什么。某一次运行里猫为什么恰好死于那一刻，理论明确不解释（“无主格”）：概率律是被逼出来的，具体发生哪一个由世界给出。页面用固定种子抽样。两种衰变模型、参数和弱记录区（D 明显小于 1）都是示意；死亡时刻律按理想检测计算，不是真实猫或原子的数值模拟。

400 cats each sit in a box with a decaying atom and a Geiger counter. Every vertical line in the 3D block is one cat’s whole history (the past at the bottom, 60 minutes later at the top), and a bright point marks the moment the counter first clicks and the poison is released. The block is one and the same archive of records; playing only moves “now” and never rewrites a record.

  1. The cat is a first-detection protocol (§11–§12). The counter checks every Δt: no click, keep going; first click, record the round n and stop. The no-click operator Q and the click operator L satisfy Q†Q + L†L = I, and the probabilities of a first click at round n plus the probability of no click up to round N add up to exactly one (Proposition 11.2). The whole history can be written as one isometric encoding with stop labels: the alive branch plus the branches “died at round 1, 2, 3 …”, each with its own orthogonal label (Proposition 12.2). BLOCK mode shows that whole.
  2. Two kinds of observation. The check interval, the decay coupling and the dark weight take part in writing the records and change the law of death times, so the archive fingerprint changes. The opening time, which ledger you look at, which clock you use and how you condition only read existing records. Sequential probabilities satisfy p(w) = Tr(ρ0Fw) (§8.2; the trace form is frozen as word_probability_trace_representation), and opening the box does not act on the atom, so the death-time law does not depend on when you open it. The page reuses the same random seeds, so these controls leave the archive byte-for-byte unchanged.
  3. Checking changes when the cat dies (Proposition 11.3). Under continual checks, the state after “no click” is not the free evolution without checks. With coherent coupling, frequent checks leave less chance to catch a decay each time, so the cat lives longer (the quantum Zeno effect); sparse checks kill it faster. With Markov decay the death-time law does not depend on the check interval. The two models coincide at Δt = 1 minute, with a 20-minute half-life.
  4. Dark state and the immortal branch (§13). If part of the atom lies in a dark space that no check can detect, the long-run survival probability converges to the weight w of that part. This is frozen: finite_detection_survival_limit (the dark space is characterised in dark_space_eq_survival_defect_kernel). The ledger cannot tell whether a living cat is dark or simply not yet decayed; the “alive spacetime” panel gives the conditional probabilities.
  5. Why nobody sees a half-dead cat (§5 and decoherence). The cat’s fate is recorded in M copies: the counter, the vial, the cat’s body, air molecules, photons … If each copy’s alive and dead versions overlap by c, all copies together overlap by κ = cM; this is frozen as repeated_record_exponential_decay. The coherence between the two branches is V = |κ| and the distinguishability of the records is D = √(1 − |κ|²), with D² + V² = 1, also frozen: pure_record_distinguishability_coherence_complementarity. The page calls D the “fact strength”: the weaker the record, the paler the alive/dead colours in the block.
  6. Facts are relative to a ledger (OBSERVER-QUANTUM section 8, GICT Observation 6.30, both in Chinese). The inside ledger writes the fact the moment the cat dies; the outside ledger reads it only when the box is opened. Both ledgers are consistent and need not be merged: the absoluteness of observed events is never granted. After opening, the observer’s own memory is one more ideal record (overlap 0). This part is a derivation plus numerical certificates in the theory volumes, not a Lean theorem.
  7. Wigner’s undo. In principle the outside observer can coherently reverse every copy, which the theory volume explicitly allows as a legitimate reverse measurement. After the undo the cat’s fate is no longer a fact in any ledger, and interference between the branches becomes measurable again. The page assumes each copy is reversed with fidelity f, leaving an overlap fM; this is a schematic assumption, not a theorem. For a real cat the number of copies is astronomical (the page goes up to 1023), and with f even slightly below 1 the undo is effectively impossible, which is where practical irreversibility comes from. The undo holds only at the moment you press it: once time moves, the page returns to the history without it and does not simulate the new history.
  8. Nonlinear time. The lab clock runs evenly; the ledger clock moves only when a record is written (OBSERVER-QUANTUM §18.2: time is ledger length, and causal structure is the prefix order of records). The ledger clock is not injective in lab time: stretches without records collapse to a point. In the outside ledger the whole time before opening collapses onto the moment of opening; after a successful undo, the erased stretch of history has zero length on the ledger clock.
  9. What this page does not explain. Why a cat in one particular run died at that moment is explicitly left unexplained by the theory (the “unowned slot”): the probability law is forced, but which outcome happens is supplied by the world. The page samples with fixed seeds. Both decay models, the parameters and the weak-record regime (D well below 1) are schematic; the death-time law assumes ideal detection. This is not a numerical simulation of a real cat or atom.
