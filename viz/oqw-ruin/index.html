LEAN ✓游走在 {0, …, M} 上，内部态是 2 × 2 密度矩阵；一步向右作用 R、向左作用 L，L*L + R*R = I。一条步序 w 的分支算子是按时间顺序的乘积（wordOperator），位置从 k 出发（position），firstAbsorbs 要求之前一直在 0 与 M 之间、最后一步到达 0 或 M；absorbedMass 是长度 n 的首次吸收步序上 tr(WρW*) 的和，meanHittingTime = Σ n·质量（取值于 [0, ∞]）。猜想 claim：对每个满足归一的复矩阵对与每个 M ≥ 3，存在 h、j 使 j(M, k) = −j(M, M − k)，且对每个 1 ≤ k ≤ M − 1 与每个密度矩阵 ρ，平均时间有限并等于 1 + h(M, M − k)ρ₀₀ + h(M, k)ρ₁₁ + j(M, k)·Re ρ₀₁。本库证明 ¬claim。已冻结：The walk lives on {0, …, M} with a 2 × 2 density matrix as internal state; a step right applies R, a step left applies L, and L*L + R*R = I. The branch operator of a step sequence w is the time-ordered product (wordOperator), the position starts at k (position), firstAbsorbs asks that the walk stays strictly between 0 and M and reaches 0 or M on the last step; absorbedMass sums tr(WρW*) over first-absorbing sequences of length n, and meanHittingTime = Σ n·mass (valued in [0, ∞]). The conjecture, claim: for every normalized complex pair and every M ≥ 3 there are h, j with j(M, k) = −j(M, M − k) such that for every 1 ≤ k ≤ M − 1 and every density matrix ρ the mean time is finite and equals 1 + h(M, M − k)ρ₀₀ + h(M, k)ρ₁₁ + j(M, k)·Re ρ₀₁. This library proves ¬claim. Frozen: OpenQuantumWalkGamblerHittingTimeRefutation.result

For Lean’s pair, ρ from k ≥ 2 steps left once and then right all the way, mean time M − k + 2; σ goes right all the way, mean time M − k.

开放量子游走里，一个粒子在 0 到 M 的格点上左右走，同时带着一个两能级的内部态。向右一步对内部态作用 R，向左一步作用 L，两者满足 L*L + R*R = I，所以每一步的总概率守恒。赌徒破产问题问：从 k 出发、内部态为 ρ，平均多久走到 0 或 M？Jacq 与 Lardizabal 根据数值实验猜想，答案只依赖 ρ 的两个对角元和 Re ρ₀₁，并且关于 k ↔ M − k 有一种反射对称。本库在 Lean 里证明这个猜想对一般的复矩阵对不成立。

  1. 反例。取 u = (1, i)、v = (1, −i)。L 把 u 方向送到 v 方向并消灭 v，R 保持 v 并消灭 u。从 k = 1 出发，ρ（指向 u）第一步就向左被吸收，平均时间 1；σ（指向 v）向右走两步才被吸收，平均时间 2。但 ρ 与 σ 的 ρ₀₀、ρ₁₁、Re ρ₀₁ 完全相同，只有 Im ρ₀₁ 符号相反——猜想的公式分不开它们。
  2. 三维视图。左边是游走在空间（横向）与时间（纵深）里的展开：每个格点上的点大小是那里的概率，短箭头是归一化内部态的 Bloch 向量，琥珀色表示 Im ρ₀₁ < 0（指向 u），品红表示 Im ρ₀₁ > 0；两端的柱是每一步被吸收的概率。右边是初始态的 Bloch 球，球面颜色是从那里出发的平均时间，品红轴是 Im ρ₀₁ 的方向——猜想只允许平均时间沿另外两个方向变化。
  3. 命中时间算子（本页的论证）。平均时间对 ρ 是线性的：E_k(ρ) = tr(A_k ρ)，A_k 满足第一步方程。猜想的形式等价于每个 k 上三个等式；右下图画出它们的差，全为 0 才可能成立。经典公平硬币三个都为 0；Lean 的矩阵对与随机复矩阵对在 Im 项上就不为 0；随机实矩阵对 Im 项为 0，但反射等式不成立（页面数值）。

原文：问题卷宗 · OpenQuantumWalkGamblerHittingTimeRefutation.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · Jacq、Lardizabal，doi:10.1007/s10955-017-1876-x · arXiv:1701.08400

In an open quantum walk a particle steps left or right on the sites 0 to M while carrying a two-level internal state. A step right applies R to the internal state and a step left applies L, with L*L + R*R = I, so total probability is conserved. The gambler’s ruin question asks: starting from k with internal state ρ, how long does it take on average to reach 0 or M? From numerical experiments Jacq and Lardizabal conjectured that the answer depends only on the two diagonal entries of ρ and on Re ρ₀₁, with a reflection symmetry under k ↔ M − k. This library proves in Lean that the conjecture fails for general complex pairs.

  1. The counterexample. Take u = (1, i) and v = (1, −i). L sends the u direction to the v direction and kills v; R keeps v and kills u. From k = 1, ρ (pointing along u) steps left at once and is absorbed, mean time 1; σ (pointing along v) needs two steps right, mean time 2. Yet ρ and σ have the same ρ₀₀, ρ₁₁ and Re ρ₀₁; only the sign of Im ρ₀₁ differs — the conjectured formula cannot tell them apart.
  2. 3D view. On the left the walk unfolds over the sites (across) and time (into the depth): the size of a dot is the probability at that site, the short arrow is the Bloch vector of the normalized internal state, amber for Im ρ₀₁ < 0 (towards u) and magenta for Im ρ₀₁ > 0; the bars at both ends are the probability absorbed at each step. On the right is the Bloch ball of initial states, coloured by the mean time from each of them; the magenta axis is the direction of Im ρ₀₁ — the conjecture lets the mean time vary only along the other two directions.
  3. The hitting-time operator (the page’s argument). The mean time is linear in ρ: E_k(ρ) = tr(A_k ρ), where A_k solves the first-step equations. The conjectured form is equivalent to three equations for every k; the lower right chart draws their gaps, which must all vanish. For the classical fair coin all three vanish; for Lean’s pair and the random complex pair the Im term already does not; for the random real pair the Im term vanishes but the reflection equations fail (page numbers).
