LEAN ✓骰子是 n 个取值在 1 … n 的非降列表（IsDie，允许重复，标准骰子也算）。d 胜 e 的计数是 n² 对面里 d 的面严格大于 e 的面的对数（winCount），概率是它除以 n²，平局留在分母里（winProbability）。边 d → e 表示这个概率恰好等于所有骰子对里大于 1/2 的最小概率（IsMinimum、Edge）。S(n)：存在长度 n 的简单循环，且没有更长的（HasCycle、S）。猜想 ConjectureOne：S(6)，且对每个 n ≥ 7 都有 ¬S(n)。本库证明 ConjectureOne。已冻结：A die is a nondecreasing list of n labels in 1 … n (IsDie; repeats allowed, the standard die included). The win count of d over e is the number of the n² face pairs in which d’s face is strictly larger (winCount); the probability divides it by n², so ties stay in the denominator (winProbability). An edge d → e means that this probability equals the smallest probability above 1/2 over all pairs of dice (IsMinimum, Edge). S(n): a simple cycle of length n exists and no longer one does (HasCycle, S). The conjecture, ConjectureOne: S(6), and ¬S(n) for every n ≥ 7. This library proves ConjectureOne. Frozen: EaslickSixBound.conjecture_one
LEAN ✓证明的步骤（四个模块里一起冻结）：最小概率在 n 偶时是 1/2 + 1/n²、n 奇时是 1/2 + 1/(2n²)，即胜的计数等于 ⌊n²/2⌋ + 1（threshold_attained、minimum_probability、edge_iff）；n = 6 … 11 的六个种子循环由通用检查器核对（checkCycle_sound、sourceSeed_check），n ≥ 12 由嵌套填充得到长度 3n − 12（偶）或 7(n − 5)（奇）的循环（padded_cycle、longer_cycle、not_S_above_six）；n = 6 的 462 颗骰子按秩分层，每个秩纤维至多 7 颗，两个 7 颗的纤维里各有两颗骰子只能走向同一颗，所以循环至多 6（checkRank_sound、sixRank_check、six_cycle_bound、S_six）。冻结：The steps of the proof (frozen across four modules): the smallest probability is 1/2 + 1/n² for even n and 1/2 + 1/(2n²) for odd n, i.e. a win count of ⌊n²/2⌋ + 1 (threshold_attained, minimum_probability, edge_iff); six seed cycles for n = 6 … 11 pass a general checker (checkCycle_sound, sourceSeed_check), and for n ≥ 12 nested padding gives cycles of length 3n − 12 (even) or 7(n − 5) (odd) (padded_cycle, longer_cycle, not_S_above_six); the 462 six-sided dice are layered by rank, every rank fiber has at most 7 dice, and in each of the two 7-dice fibers two dice can only move to the same die, so a cycle has at most 6 dice (checkRank_sound, sixRank_check, six_cycle_bound, S_six). NUM页面数值，不是定理：n ≤ 7 时页面枚举全部 C(2n − 1, n) 颗骰子与全部有序对，按 winCount = ⌊n²/2⌋ + 1 连边，用 Tarjan 算法求强连通分支，n ≤ 6 时在每个分支里穷举最长循环。n = 3、4、5 的图没有循环；n = 6 只有两个 7 颗骰子的分支；n = 7 的 1716 颗骰子里有 1081 颗落在同一个强连通分支里。Numbers computed on the page, not theorems: for n ≤ 7 the page enumerates all C(2n − 1, n) dice and all ordered pairs, joins d → e when winCount = ⌊n²/2⌋ + 1, finds strongly connected components with Tarjan’s algorithm, and for n ≤ 6 searches every component for its longest cycle. The graphs for n = 3, 4 and 5 have no cycle; n = 6 has just two components of 7 dice; for n = 7, 1081 of the 1716 dice lie in a single strongly connected component.
ARGUED本页的论证（未形式化、未经评审）：把每颗骰子的每个面 x 换成 n + 1 − x 再排序，记作 φ(d)。d 的面大于 e 的面当且仅当 φ(e) 的对应面大于 φ(d) 的，所以 winCount(φ(d), φ(e)) = winCount(e, d)：φ 把每条边 d → e 变成 φ(e) → φ(d)，把强连通分支映成强连通分支。n = 6 时它把一个 7 颗骰子的结映成另一个，两个结的最长循环因此一样长。The page’s argument (not formalized, not reviewed): replace every face x of a die by n + 1 − x and sort, calling the result φ(d). A face of d beats a face of e exactly when the corresponding face of φ(e) beats that of φ(d), so winCount(φ(d), φ(e)) = winCount(e, d): φ turns every edge d → e into φ(e) → φ(d) and maps strongly connected components to strongly connected components. For n = 6 it maps one 7-dice knot onto the other, so the two knots have equally long longest cycles.
OPEN定理之外：n ≥ 7 时这种循环的最大长度到底是多少。定理只造出比 n 长的循环，没有定出最大值；图里的条形是已知下界。Outside the theorem: the actual maximum length of such a cycle for each n ≥ 7. The theorem only builds a cycle longer than n and does not determine the maximum; the bars in the chart are known lower bounds.

两颗骰子各掷一次，点大的赢，平局不算谁赢。非传递骰子是一串骰子，每颗都比下一颗更可能赢，最后一颗又更可能赢第一颗，像石头剪刀布。Easlick 只看每面取 1 到 n 的 n 面骰子，并要求每一步的优势都是最小可能的那个：胜的计数恰好是 ⌊n²/2⌋ + 1（n = 6 时是 36 种掷法里赢 19 种）。他猜想，这种循环的最大长度恰好等于 n，只有 n = 6 这一种情况。

  1. n = 6。462 颗骰子之间有 5491 条最小优势的边，约 95% 从总点数大的骰子指向小的（页面数值），整张图只有两个打结的地方，每个结有 7 颗骰子。每个结里都有两颗骰子只能走向同一颗下一颗，所以一个循环最多用到 6 颗——六循环存在，七循环不存在。
  2. n ≥ 7。原文附录给出 n = 7 … 10 的长循环（14、29、10、110），本库再用“嵌套填充”把种子循环推广到所有更大的 n，于是 n ≥ 7 时最长循环总比 n 长。
  3. 三维视图。每个点是一颗骰子，高度是它的总点数；细线是最小优势的边，琥珀色的点在某个强连通分支里，青色是当前的循环。时间轴沿着循环一步一步走，下方左图是当前这一步两颗骰子对掷的 n² 种结果。

原文：问题卷宗 · EaslickSixBound.lean · EaslickCycleSeeds.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · doi:10.48550/arXiv.2610.40321 · arXiv:2610.40321 · CC BY 4.0

Roll two dice once each; the higher face wins and a tie counts for neither. Non-transitive dice form a chain in which every die is more likely to beat the next one and the last is more likely to beat the first, like rock–paper–scissors. Easlick looks only at n-sided dice with faces in 1 … n and asks every step to have the smallest possible advantage: a win count of exactly ⌊n²/2⌋ + 1 (for n = 6, 19 of the 36 outcomes). He conjectured that the maximum length of such a cycle equals n only in the single case n = 6.

  1. n = 6. The 462 dice carry 5491 minimal-advantage edges, about 95% of which point from a die with a larger face sum to one with a smaller sum (page numbers); the whole graph has only two knots, each of 7 dice. In each knot two dice can only move to the same next die, so a cycle uses at most 6 dice — a six-cycle exists and a seven-cycle does not.
  2. n ≥ 7. The paper’s appendices give long cycles for n = 7 … 10 (lengths 14, 29, 10 and 110); this library extends the seed cycles to every larger n by nested padding, so for n ≥ 7 the longest cycle is always longer than n.
  3. 3D view. Every point is a die, raised to the height of its face sum; thin lines are minimal-advantage edges, amber points lie in a strongly connected component, and cyan marks the current cycle. The time rail walks the cycle step by step, and the left chart below shows the n² outcomes of the current duel.
