理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

从原点出发，在方格纸上走 L 步，每步往右、左、上、下之一。往右走一步时加上当时的高度，往左走一步时减去它，这就是“代数面积”——路径与横轴之间的有向面积。A385672 把所有游走按面积分组计数。L = 2n 时面积最多是 n²（先上 n 步再右 n 步）。条目猜测：面积比最大值少 k（k < n）的游走恰有 2·A029552(k) 条。

  1. 答案。Lean 证明了偶数和奇数两条公式。
  2. 为什么只剩单调游走。右步时高度最多是总的向上次数 u，左步时最多是总的向下次数 d，所以面积不超过 r·u + l·d。如果一条游走既有向右或向上、又有向左或向下，这个界就不超过 n² − n，而 k < n 时目标面积 n² − k 比它大。于是面积这么大的游走只能一直往右上、或一直往左下；后者翻转后就是前者，所以乘 2。
  3. 为什么是划分。u 次向上、r 次向右的游走被包在 u × r 的盒子里，面积等于盒子面积减去路径上方缺的格子数 m。缺的格子从左到右越来越少，组成一个划分的杨图——三维视图里的品红方框。只要 m 足够小，这样的路径就和 m 的划分一一对应。
  4. 边界。把 k 推到 n，混合方向的游走就能挤进来，计数比公式多（3 ≤ L ≤ 24 时偶数步多 8 条，奇数步多 4 条，这是页面数出来的）。

原文：问题卷宗 · LatticeWalkNearMaximalArea.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · OEIS A385672 · A029552

Start at the origin and take L steps on squared paper, each right, left, up or down. A right step adds the current height and a left step subtracts it: this is the algebraic area, the signed area between the path and the horizontal axis. A385672 counts all walks by their area. For L = 2n the area is at most n² (n ups, then n rights). The entry conjectures that the walks whose area falls short of the maximum by k (k < n) number exactly 2·A029552(k).

  1. The answer. Lean proves both formulas, for even and for odd lengths.
  2. Why only monotone walks remain. At a right step the height is at most the total number u of up steps, and at a left step at least minus the number d of down steps, so the area is at most r·u + l·d. If a walk has a right or up step and also a left or down step, this bound is at most n² − n, while for k < n the target area n² − k is larger. So walks of such large area go only right and up, or only left and down; flipping the latter gives the former, hence the factor 2.
  3. Why partitions. A walk with u ups and r rights fits in a u × r box, and its area is the box’s area minus the number m of missing cells above the path. The missing cells shrink from left to right and form the Young diagram of a partition — the magenta squares in the 3D view. When m is small enough, such paths correspond one-to-one to the partitions of m.
  4. The boundary. Push k up to n and walks with mixed directions squeeze in; the count exceeds the formula (by 8 walks for even lengths and 4 for odd ones, as the page counts for 3 ≤ L ≤ 24).
