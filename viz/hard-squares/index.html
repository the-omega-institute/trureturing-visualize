硬核模型：在一个图上放粒子，相邻的两个顶点不能同时有粒子（配置就是独立集 S），每个配置的权重是 λ|S|，λ 叫活度。在正方形网格上它叫硬方块格气。λ 小时粒子稀疏、无序；λ 大时粒子挤满一个子格，形成棋盘。三维视图里粒子是方块，青色和品红区分棋盘的两个子格。

  1. 涨落等于响应（已冻结）。对任意有限图，平均粒子数 ⟨N⟩ 对 log λ 的导数等于 N 的方差：λ·d⟨N⟩/dλ = Var(N)（GibbsOccupation.mean_occupation_fluctuation_response）。这是统计力学里“响应 = 涨落”的一个精确有限版本。页面对 8 × 8 环面逐个数出各粒子数的配置个数（一共 213 256 442 503 个配置），算出 ⟨N⟩、Var(N)，再用数值微分核对这条等式。
  2. 相变（文献，数值）。无限网格上，λ 越过约 3.7962 时，两个子格的对称性自发破缺（Baxter、Enting、Tsang，J. Stat. Phys. 22, 465 (1980)）。页面在 48 × 48 环面上做热浴蒙特卡罗：随机挑一个格点，邻居都空时以 λ/(1 + λ) 的概率放一个粒子，否则清空。有限格子上只能看到相变的痕迹：子格占据差 |ρA − ρB| 在 λ 大时变大。
  3. 项链（已冻结的猜想证明）。Adamaszek 研究圆柱上硬方块的独立复形，引入了一个组合模型：周长 n（偶数）的圆上有 2k 颗石子，每颗带向量 −2、−1、1 或 2，相邻石子方向相反，并满足若干间距条件。一步 T：所有石子同时按向量跳，然后转向（−2→1，−1→2，1→−2，2→−1），最后把相距 3、面对面的石子的长度 2 向量缩成 1。他猜想 Tn−3kN 回到 N（在圆的保距变换意义下），并在 n ≤ 36 上验证过（arXiv:1202.1655，猜想 7.4）。本库证明了它（HardSquareNecklacePeriod.result）：把石子“压缩”成速度 ±1 的粒子，T 变成自由运动加交换标签，L = n − 3k 步后每个粒子绕回整圈。三维视图把每一步画成一圈，往上叠，最上面那圈与初始配置的旋转像重合。
  4. 占据下界被推翻（已冻结）。Davies、Sandhu、Seo、Tan 证明了 λ 有上界时的多变量占据下界 𝔼|I| ≥ Σv λv/(1 + (dv + 1)λv)，并猜测上界可以去掉（arXiv:2605.05149）。本库给出反例：星形图 K1,2，中心活度 15、两片叶子活度 2。五个独立集 ∅, {0}, {1}, {2}, {1, 2} 的权重是 1, 15, 2, 2, 4，所以 𝔼|I| = 27/24 = 9/8，而右边是 15/46 + 2/5 + 2/5 = 259/230（MultivariateOccupancyRefutation.result）。页面画出整个 (λ中心, λ叶) 平面上下界失败的区域。
  5. 模型边界。蒙特卡罗是随机模拟，读数带统计误差；8 × 8 的精确结果是有限格子的，不是无限网格的极限；相变点 3.7962 是文献的数值估计。

The hard-core model: put particles on a graph so that no two neighbouring vertices are both occupied (a configuration is an independent set S), and give each configuration weight λ|S|, where λ is the activity. On the square grid it is called the hard-square lattice gas. For small λ the particles are sparse and disordered; for large λ they crowd onto one sublattice and form a checkerboard. In the 3D view particles are tiles, cyan and magenta for the two sublattices of the checkerboard.

  1. Fluctuation equals response (frozen). On any finite graph the derivative of the mean particle number ⟨N⟩ with respect to log λ equals the variance of N: λ·d⟨N⟩/dλ = Var(N) (GibbsOccupation.mean_occupation_fluctuation_response). It is an exact finite version of the statistical-mechanics rule “response = fluctuation”. The page counts the configurations of the 8 × 8 torus by particle number (213 256 442 503 configurations in all), computes ⟨N⟩ and Var(N), and checks the identity by numerical differentiation.
  2. The phase transition (literature, numerical). On the infinite grid the symmetry between the two sublattices breaks spontaneously once λ passes about 3.7962 (Baxter, Enting and Tsang, J. Stat. Phys. 22, 465 (1980)). The page runs heat-bath Monte Carlo on a 48 × 48 torus: pick a site at random; if its neighbours are all empty, occupy it with probability λ/(1 + λ), otherwise empty it. A finite lattice shows only traces of the transition: the sublattice imbalance |ρA − ρB| grows for large λ.
  3. Necklaces (a frozen proof of a conjecture). Studying independence complexes of hard squares on cylinders, Adamaszek introduced a combinatorial model: 2k stones on a circle of even circumference n, each carrying a vector −2, −1, 1 or 2, neighbouring stones pointing in opposite directions and obeying some spacing rules. One step T: all stones jump by their vectors at once, then turn (−2→1, −1→2, 1→−2, 2→−1), and finally facing stones at distance 3 have their length-2 vectors shortened to 1. He conjectured that Tn−3kN returns to N up to an isometry of the circle, and checked it for n ≤ 36 (arXiv:1202.1655, Conjecture 7.4). This library proves it (HardSquareNecklacePeriod.result): compressed into particles of velocity ±1, T becomes free motion plus a relabelling, and after L = n − 3k steps every particle has wound once round. The 3D view stacks one ring per step; the top ring coincides with a rotated copy of the start.
  4. An occupancy bound refuted (frozen). Davies, Sandhu, Seo and Tan proved the multivariate occupancy bound 𝔼|I| ≥ Σv λv/(1 + (dv + 1)λv) for bounded activities and conjectured that the bound on λ can be removed (arXiv:2605.05149). This library gives a counterexample: the star K1,2 with activity 15 at the centre and 2 at both leaves. The five independent sets ∅, {0}, {1}, {2}, {1, 2} have weights 1, 15, 2, 2, 4, so 𝔼|I| = 27/24 = 9/8, while the right-hand side is 15/46 + 2/5 + 2/5 = 259/230 (MultivariateOccupancyRefutation.result). The page maps the region of the (λcentre, λleaf) plane where the bound fails.
  5. Model limits. Monte Carlo is a random simulation and its readings carry statistical error; the exact 8 × 8 results belong to a finite lattice, not to the infinite-grid limit; the transition point 3.7962 is a numerical estimate from the literature.
