LEAN ✓Ghosh 与 Ramachandran（2026）问：K 连通时，Fekete 多项式（根取在使 Π|aᵢ − aⱼ| 最大的位置）是否面积最小？不是。K = [−20, 20]、n = 4：Fekete 多项式 F = (z² − 400)(z² − 80) 的单位水平集面积 ≥ 7.231744 × 10⁻⁸π，竞争者 G = (z² − 400)(z² − 100) 的面积 ≤ 7.204616 × 10⁻⁸π。两边都用有理半径的圆盘证书：F 的水平集包含四个不相交的小圆盘，G 的水平集被四个小圆盘覆盖。已冻结：Ghosh and Ramachandran (2026) ask whether, for connected K, Fekete polynomials (roots placed to maximize Π|aᵢ − aⱼ|) minimize the area. They do not. K = [−20, 20], n = 4: the Fekete polynomial F = (z² − 400)(z² − 80) has unit sublevel area ≥ 7.231744 × 10⁻⁸π, the competitor G = (z² − 400)(z² − 100) has area ≤ 7.204616 × 10⁻⁸π. Both sides use certificate discs with rational radii: F’s sublevel set contains four disjoint small discs, G’s is covered by four small discs. Frozen: FeketeLemniscateAreaRefutation.result
LEAN ✓他们又问：容量为一的紧集上，最小面积 Aₙ(K) 是否只能按 1/n 或 1/log n 衰减？也不是。K 取闭单位圆盘加孤立点 4，它的超限直径为 1；竞争者 (zᴺ − 1)(z − 4)ᵏ 的单位水平集落在环带 1 − 2⁻ᵏ < |z| < 1 + 2⁻ᵏ 与 4 附近半径 2⁻⁽ᵏ⁻²⁾ 的圆盘里，取 N = k² + 1 得 Ak²+k+1(K) ≤ 20π·2⁻ᵏ，于是 n·Aₙ 沿这串 n 趋于 0，两种衰减率都不成立。已冻结：They also ask whether on a compact set of capacity one the minimal area Aₙ(K) can only decay like 1/n or 1/log n. Neither. Take K the closed unit disc plus the isolated point 4, whose transfinite diameter is 1; the competitor (zᴺ − 1)(z − 4)ᵏ has its unit sublevel set inside the annulus 1 − 2⁻ᵏ < |z| < 1 + 2⁻ᵏ and a disc of radius 2⁻⁽ᵏ⁻²⁾ about 4; with N = k² + 1 this gives Ak²+k+1(K) ≤ 20π·2⁻ᵏ, so n·Aₙ tends to 0 along these n and neither rate holds. Frozen: CapacityOneAreaDecayRate.result
NUM页面数值，不是定理：面积用数值积分算出（实根时沿竖直截线积分，否则沿每个根的极坐标积分）；R = 20 时 F、G 的面积分别约 7.32 × 10⁻⁸π 与 6.94 × 10⁻⁸π，落在 Lean 的两个界之外侧；页面扫描的各个 R 上，对称竞争者 (z² − R²)(z² − c²) 中都有比 Fekete 更小的；(zᴺ − 1)(z − 4)ᵏ 的实际面积远小于 Lean 的上界。Numbers computed on the page, not theorems: areas come from numerical integration (along vertical slices for real roots, otherwise in polar coordinates about each root); at R = 20 the areas of F and G are about 7.32 × 10⁻⁸π and 6.94 × 10⁻⁸π, on the far sides of the two Lean bounds; for every R the page scans, some symmetric competitor (z² − R²)(z² − c²) beats the Fekete polynomial; the actual area of (zᴺ − 1)(z − 4)ᵏ is far below the Lean bound.
OPEN未决：哪些连通集上 Fekete 多项式恰好面积最小；容量为一的集合上还能出现哪些衰减率，最小面积的精确阶。Open: on which connected sets Fekete polynomials do minimize the area; which other decay rates occur on capacity-one sets, and the exact order of the minimal area.

LIT问题源于 Erdős、Herzog 与 Piranian（1958）。Ghosh 与 Ramachandran（2026）证明 cap(K) > 1 时 Fekete 多项式在对数尺度上渐近最优，并用不连通的集合说明它一般不是最小；区间 [−2, 2] 上 Aₙ ≤ c/n。Pendyala（2026）给出圆盘与圆周上 1/log n 的精确阶。The problem goes back to Erdős, Herzog and Piranian (1958). Ghosh and Ramachandran (2026) prove that Fekete polynomials are asymptotically optimal on a logarithmic scale when cap(K) > 1, show with a disconnected set that they are not minimal in general, and prove Aₙ ≤ c/n on [−2, 2]. Pendyala (2026) gives the sharp order 1/log n for the disc and the circle.

给定复平面上的紧集 K，考虑所有根都在 K 里的 n 次首一多项式 p。它的单位水平集 Λₚ = {z : |p(z)| < 1} 是一些围绕根的区域，Erdős 问这块区域的面积最小能有多小，记为 Aₙ(K)。Ghosh 与 Ramachandran（2026）留下两个具体问题，本库把两者都否定了。

  1. Fekete 多项式（原文）。在 Kⁿ 里使 Πi<j|aᵢ − aⱼ| 最大的点组叫 Fekete 点组，对应的多项式 F = Π(z − aᵢ)。原文证明 cap(K) > 1 时 Fekete 多项式在对数尺度上渐近最优，又用不连通的集合说明它一般不是面积最小，留下 K 连通时的问题。
  2. 连通集上的反例（已冻结）。K = [−20, 20]、n = 4：Fekete 点组是 ±20、±√80（一般地 ±R、±R/√5）。竞争者把内根放在 ±10。根之间相距很远，每个根附近的水平集几乎是半径约 1/|p′(a)| 的小圆盘；Lean 用有理半径的圆盘夹住两边的面积：F 至少 7.231744 × 10⁻⁸π，G 至多 7.204616 × 10⁻⁸π。
  3. 为什么 Fekete 会输（页面数值的解释）。水平集面积近似为 π Σ 1/|p′(aₖ)|²，Fekete 点组却最大化 Π|p′(aₖ)|。乘积最大与倒数平方和最小不是同一个目标：把内根从 ±√80 外移到 ±10，内根处的 |p′| 变大、外根处变小，倒数平方和反而下降。
  4. 容量为一的衰减率（已冻结）。K = 闭单位圆盘 ∪ {4}，超限直径为 1。(zᴺ − 1)(z − 4)ᵏ 在单位圆附近只剩 N 个小花瓣，在 4 附近只剩一个极小的圆盘：Lean 证明它们落在环带 1 ± 2⁻ᵏ 与半径 2⁻⁽ᵏ⁻²⁾ 的圆盘里，取 N = k² + 1 得到 Ak²+k+1(K) ≤ 20π·2⁻ᵏ，比 1/n 快得多。
  5. 页面怎么算面积。根都是实数时，|p(x + iy)|² 对 |y| 单调，每条竖直截线上的水平集是一个区间，沿 x 积分即可；否则在每个根周围按极坐标求边界半径。页面的面积是数值，不承担证明。
  6. 边界。哪些连通集上 Fekete 恰好最小、容量为一时还能有哪些衰减率，都还是开放问题。

原文：问题卷宗：连通集上的 Fekete 多项式 · 问题卷宗：容量为一时的衰减率 · FeketeLemniscateAreaRefutation.lean · CapacityOneAreaDecayRate.lean · Ghosh、Ramachandran，Proc. AMS（2026）（arXiv:2604.03036） · Erdős、Herzog、Piranian，J. Analyse Math. 6（1958） · Pendyala，arXiv:2606.17097（2026）

Given a compact set K in the complex plane, consider the monic polynomials p of degree n with all roots in K. The unit sublevel set Λₚ = {z : |p(z)| < 1} consists of regions around the roots, and Erdős asked how small its area can be; call the infimum Aₙ(K). Ghosh and Ramachandran (2026) left two concrete questions, and this library answers both in the negative.

  1. Fekete polynomials (source). A Fekete tuple maximizes Πi<j|aᵢ − aⱼ| over Kⁿ, giving F = Π(z − aᵢ). The source proves that Fekete polynomials are asymptotically optimal on a logarithmic scale when cap(K) > 1, shows with a disconnected set that they are not area minimizers in general, and leaves the connected case open.
  2. A counterexample on a connected set (frozen). K = [−20, 20], n = 4: the Fekete tuple is ±20, ±√80 (in general ±R, ±R/√5). The competitor puts its inner roots at ±10. The roots are far apart, so near each root the sublevel set is almost a disc of radius about 1/|p′(a)|; Lean squeezes both areas between discs of rational radius: F has at least 7.231744 × 10⁻⁸π, G at most 7.204616 × 10⁻⁸π.
  3. Why Fekete loses (an explanation of the page numerics). The area is close to π Σ 1/|p′(aₖ)|², while a Fekete tuple maximizes Π|p′(aₖ)|. Maximizing the product and minimizing the sum of inverse squares are different goals: moving the inner roots from ±√80 out to ±10 raises |p′| at the inner roots and lowers it at the outer ones, and the sum of inverse squares goes down.
  4. Decay on capacity one (frozen). K = closed unit disc ∪ {4} has transfinite diameter 1. The polynomial (zᴺ − 1)(z − 4)ᵏ leaves only N small petals near the unit circle and one tiny disc near 4: Lean proves they lie in the annulus 1 ± 2⁻ᵏ and a disc of radius 2⁻⁽ᵏ⁻²⁾, and with N = k² + 1 this gives Ak²+k+1(K) ≤ 20π·2⁻ᵏ, much faster than 1/n.
  5. How the page computes area. With real roots, |p(x + iy)|² is monotone in |y|, so each vertical slice of the sublevel set is an interval and the area is an integral over x; otherwise the boundary radius is found in polar coordinates about each root. The page’s areas are numbers, not proofs.
  6. Limits. On which connected sets Fekete polynomials do minimize, and which decay rates occur at capacity one, remain open.
