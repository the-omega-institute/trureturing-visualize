  1. 1 → 2。恒等态被区分成两条可读分支；分叉门是规范化的二元关系。

The page turns relational closure into a minimal runnable automaton. It exposes the structure; it is not an independent physical theorem.

  1. 1 → 2.Identity becomes two distinguishable readable branches; the split gate is a normalized binary relation.
  2. 2 → 3.A relative phase appears between the branches; the triangle records two edges and their relation.
  3. 3 → ∞.Repeated gates generate a relation network; hidden fibres can change trajectories, while only observable readouts carry physical content.
