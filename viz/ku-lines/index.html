LEAN ✓棋盘 [k]^d = {1, …, k}^d；一条完整直线是 p + t·v（0 ≤ t < k，v ∈ {−1, 0, 1}^d 非零）整个落在棋盘里的 k 个点。配置不得含任何直线的 k − 1 个点；饱和指不能再加点；D(k, [k]^d) 是饱和配置的最大点数除以 k^d。Ku 的猜想 5.6 说 D(k, [k]^d) = 1 − 2/k。本库证明在 k = 3、d = 3 时不成立：每个配置最多 8 个点，所以 D(3, [3]³) ≤ 8/27 < 1/3。已冻结：The board is [k]^d = {1, …, k}^d; a full line is the k points p + t·v (0 ≤ t < k, v ∈ {−1, 0, 1}^d nonzero) lying entirely in the board. A configuration may contain no k − 1 points of any line; it is saturated if no point can be added; D(k, [k]^d) is the largest size of a saturated configuration divided by k^d. Ku’s Conjecture 5.6 says D(k, [k]^d) = 1 − 2/k. This library proves it false at k = 3, d = 3: every configuration has at most 8 points, so D(3, [3]³) ≤ 8/27 < 1/3. LEAN ✓证明的机制（同一模块里冻结的公开引理）：同一条轴线上的两点必共线，所以向前两个坐标的投影是单射；若有 9 个点，投影就是双射，第三个坐标构成一个 3 阶 Latin 方阵；任何 3 阶 Latin 方阵的主对角线或副对角线上必有重复的值；重复的两个点恰好落在同一条面对角线上，与配置条件矛盾。冻结：The mechanism of the proof (public lemmas frozen in the same module): two points on an axis line are collinear, so projecting to the first two coordinates is injective; with 9 points the projection is a bijection and the third coordinate forms a Latin square of order 3; every Latin square of order 3 repeats a value on its main or anti-diagonal; the two repeated points lie on one face diagonal, contradicting the configuration condition. ARGUED上界的来源（论文里的论证）：沿某个坐标方向的 k^(d−1) 条轴线把棋盘分成不相交的组，每条至多 k − 2 个点，所以任何配置至多 (k − 2)k^(d−1) 个点，密度至多 1 − 2/k。猜想断言这个上界总能达到；3×3×3 说明不能。Where the upper bound comes from (the paper’s argument): the k^(d−1) axis lines in one coordinate direction partition the board, each holds at most k − 2 points, so every configuration has at most (k − 2)k^(d−1) points, a density of at most 1 − 2/k. The conjecture asserts that this bound is always attained; 3×3×3 shows that it is not.
OPEN定理之外：D(3, [3]³) 的精确值在 Lean 里未定（页面穷举给 6/27）；一般 (k, d) 的 D 值，以及哪些参数能达到上界 1 − 2/k；论文问题 5.7（最小密度）。Outside the theorem: the exact value of D(3, [3]³) is not settled in Lean (the page’s enumeration gives 6/27); D for general (k, d), and which parameters attain the bound 1 − 2/k; the paper’s Question 5.7 (minimum density).

k 连棋（k in a row）推广到 d 维：在 [k]^d 上，一条“线”是横、竖、斜方向上连续的 k 个格子。Ku 研究这样的配置：任何一条线上选中的点都少于 k − 1 个，而且再加任何一点都会破坏这一点。他猜想这种配置最多能占 1 − 2/k 的格子。本库在 Lean 里证明 3×3×3 上做不到：最多 8 个点，而 1 − 2/3 要求 9 个。

  1. 直线。3×3×3 有 49 条完整直线：27 条轴线、18 条面对角线、4 条体对角线。k = 3 时条件是“每条线至多 1 个点”，即任意两个选中点都不共线。三维视图里灰色是全部直线，青色点是配置，品红线是违规的直线。
  2. 上界从哪来。沿一个坐标方向的 9 条轴线互不相交、覆盖全部 27 格，每条至多 1 个点，所以至多 9 个点——这正是 1 − 2/3。猜想说 9 能达到。
  3. 为什么 9 不行（冻结）。9 个点时三个轴向都恰好每条一个点，于是第三个坐标是前两个坐标的 3 阶 Latin 方阵。“Latin 方阵障碍”模式把 12 个 3 阶 Latin 方阵逐个摆上去：每一个的主对角线或副对角线都有重复，重复的两个点落在同一条面对角线上（品红）。
  4. 真正的最大值（页面数值）。“饱和配置”模式逐个展示回溯枚举出的全部饱和配置，按大小排序；3×3×3 一共 305 个，最大的只有 6 个点。右下图是大小分布，琥珀线是猜想的值，绿线是 Lean 证明的上界 8。
  5. 别的棋盘。3×3、4×4、5×5 与 4×4×4 都能达到 1 − 2/k；3×3×3×3 的最大值只有 11，而猜想要 27。这些是页面与测试的计算，不是定理。

原文：问题卷宗 · KuNearLineRefutation.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · Ku，arXiv:2606.12880

k in a row generalised to d dimensions: on [k]^d a “line” is k consecutive cells in an axis, diagonal or space-diagonal direction. Ku studies configurations in which every line has fewer than k − 1 chosen points and adding any point would break this. He conjectured that such a configuration can fill a fraction 1 − 2/k of the board. This library proves in Lean that 3×3×3 cannot: at most 8 points, whereas 1 − 2/3 asks for 9.

  1. Lines. 3×3×3 has 49 full lines: 27 axis lines, 18 face diagonals and 4 space diagonals. For k = 3 the condition is “at most 1 point per line”, i.e. no two chosen points are collinear. In the 3D view grey shows every line, cyan points form the configuration, and magenta lines are violated.
  2. Where the bound comes from. The 9 axis lines in one coordinate direction are disjoint and cover all 27 cells, each holds at most 1 point, so there are at most 9 points — exactly 1 − 2/3. The conjecture says 9 is attained.
  3. Why 9 fails (frozen). With 9 points every axis line in all three directions holds exactly one point, so the third coordinate is a Latin square of order 3 in the first two. The “Latin-square obstruction” mode places the 12 Latin squares of order 3 one by one: each repeats a value on its main or anti-diagonal, and the two repeated points lie on one face diagonal (magenta).
  4. The true maximum (page numbers). The “saturated configurations” mode shows every saturated configuration found by backtracking, sorted by size; 3×3×3 has 305 of them and the largest has only 6 points. The lower right chart is the size distribution, the amber line the conjectured value and the green line the bound 8 proved in Lean.
  5. Other boards. 3×3, 4×4, 5×5 and 4×4×4 all reach 1 − 2/k; the maximum on 3×3×3×3 is only 11, while the conjecture asks for 27. These are computations of the page and the tests, not theorems.
