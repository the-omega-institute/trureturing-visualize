LEAN ✓Gronwall 包络：对每个 ε > 0，从某处起 R(n) ≤ 1 + ε，并且有任意大的 n 使 R(n) ≥ 1 − ε，这些 n 取素数阶乘的幂；所以 R(n) 的上极限恰好是 1。Robin 对数余量 γ + log log log n − log(σ(n)/n) 的下极限是 0。这些结论不依赖黎曼猜想，也不断言 Robin 判据。已冻结：The Gronwall envelopes: for every ε > 0, eventually R(n) ≤ 1 + ε, and there are arbitrarily large n with R(n) ≥ 1 − ε, taken among powers of primorials; so the upper limit of R(n) is exactly 1. The logarithmic Robin margin γ + log log log n − log(σ(n)/n) has lower limit 0. These are unconditional and assert no Robin criterion. The theorem gives no numerical value for “eventually”. Frozen: PrimeValuationGap.bounded_prime_valuation_robin_ratio · bounded_prime_valuation_robin_margin_gap

Robin (1984) proved that the Riemann hypothesis holds exactly when σ(n) < e^γ n log log n for all n > 5040. Choie, Lichiardopol, Moree and Solé (2007) proved that an n ≥ 37 violating Robin’s inequality must be even, neither squarefree nor squarefull, and divisible by a fifth power greater than 1. 理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

σ(n) 是 n 的全部因子之和。它可以比 n 大很多，但大不过 e^γ n log log n 太多：Robin 比值 R(n) = σ(n)/(e^γ n log log n) 的上极限恰好是 1。Robin 证明，黎曼猜想等价于 5040 之后 R(n) 永远小于 1。本库在 Lean 里冻结了这条边界附近的三类结论；黎曼猜想本身仍然开放。

  1. Gronwall 包络（已冻结）。两个 ε 条件：从某处起 R(n) ≤ 1 + ε；又有任意大的 n 使 R(n) ≥ 1 − ε。后一条的证人是素数阶乘的幂 n = (2·3·…·p_k)^a：σ(n)/n = ∏ (1 − p^(−a−1))/(1 − 1/p)，由 Mertens 第三定理，固定 a、k → ∞ 时 R(n) → 1/ζ(a + 1)，再让 a 增大即趋于 1。由此 Robin 对数余量的下极限是 0。
  2. 按素数指数分层（已冻结）。把 p 的指数固定在不超过 A，σ(n)/n 里 p 那一项最多是 (1 − p^(−A−1))/(1 − 1/p)，比无限制时少一个因子 1 − p^(−A−1)，所以这一层的 R(n) 最终不超过 1 − p^(−A−1) + ε。页面的绿线就是这些上限。收敛按 log log n 的速度，极慢：10⁶ 以内有些层的最大值仍高于自己的上限，这不违反定理，因为定理只说“从某处起”。
  3. 离线零点的回声（已冻结）。显式公式里每个零点 ρ 给 θ(x) − x 贡献约 −x^ρ/ρ。若 β = Re ρ > 1/2，这一项比临界线上零点的 √x 大。按 Lean 的定义取加权尾项 f(u)，冻结定理说 u·e^((1−β)u)·f(u) 会在任意晚的地方超过任何小于 m/(|ρ||ρ − 1|) 的数。页面只保留一对假想零点来画这个机制：归一化的尾项以约 2m/(|ρ||ρ − 1|) 的振幅振荡，超过定理的门槛；不归一化时它按 e^((β−1)u)/u 衰减，比 e^(−u/2) 慢，迟早越过 7e^(−u/2)。
  4. 页面数值。σ(n) 筛到 10⁶；n ≤ 5040 有 26 个数 R(n) ≥ 1，最大的是 5040；页面在 (5040, 10⁶] 没有找到 R(n) ≥ 1 的数，这只是数值。
  5. 边界。定理都是“最终”或“任意晚”的形式，不给出具体阈值；把尾项越过包络变成真实 Robin 反例的桥仍是开放的；离线模式移动的零点在现实中已验证在临界线上。

σ(n) is the sum of all divisors of n. It can be much larger than n, but not much larger than e^γ n log log n: the Robin ratio R(n) = σ(n)/(e^γ n log log n) has upper limit exactly 1. Robin proved that the Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to R(n) staying below 1 for every n after 5040. This library has frozen three kinds of results near this edge in Lean; the Riemann hypothesis itself remains open.

  1. The Gronwall envelopes (frozen). Two ε conditions: eventually R(n) ≤ 1 + ε, and there are arbitrarily large n with R(n) ≥ 1 − ε. The witnesses for the second are primorial powers n = (2·3·…·p_k)^a: σ(n)/n = ∏ (1 − p^(−a−1))/(1 − 1/p), and by Mertens’ third theorem R(n) → 1/ζ(a + 1) as k → ∞ with a fixed, which tends to 1 as a grows. Hence the logarithmic Robin margin has lower limit 0.
  2. Layers by the exponent of a prime (frozen). With the exponent of p held to at most A, the factor of p in σ(n)/n is at most (1 − p^(−A−1))/(1 − 1/p), one factor 1 − p^(−A−1) short of the unrestricted bound, so on this layer R(n) eventually stays below 1 − p^(−A−1) + ε. The green lines on the page are these caps. Convergence runs at the speed of log log n, very slowly: up to 10⁶ some layers still exceed their caps, which does not contradict the theorem, since it only says “eventually”.
  3. The echo of an off-line zero (frozen). In the explicit formula each zero ρ contributes about −x^ρ/ρ to θ(x) − x. If β = Re ρ > 1/2, this term outgrows the √x of zeros on the critical line. With the weighted tail f(u) defined as in Lean, the frozen theorem says that u·e^((1−β)u)·f(u) exceeds any number below m/(|ρ||ρ − 1|) arbitrarily late. The page keeps one hypothetical zero pair to draw the mechanism: the normalized tail oscillates with amplitude about 2m/(|ρ||ρ − 1|), above the theorem’s threshold; unnormalized, it decays like e^((β−1)u)/u, slower than e^(−u/2), and sooner or later crosses 7e^(−u/2).
  4. Page numerics. σ(n) is sieved to 10⁶; for n ≤ 5040, 26 numbers have R(n) ≥ 1, the largest being 5040; the page finds no n in (5040, 10⁶] with R(n) ≥ 1, which is numerics only.
  5. Limits. The theorems are of the form “eventually” or “arbitrarily late” and give no thresholds; the bridge from a tail crossing an envelope to an actual Robin violation is open; the zeros moved in the off-line mode are verified to lie on the critical line in reality.
