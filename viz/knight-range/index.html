LEAN ✓定义：move 是马的八种走法，move 0 = (1, 2)；position w j 是走了前 j 步后的位置；visited w 是访问过的格子（含起点）；expectedRange n = Σ_w |visited w| / 8ⁿ，对全部 8ⁿ 条步序求和。猜想 claim：对每个 n ≥ 1，存在整数 m 使 expectedRange n · 2^(3n−3) = m。本库证明 claim。已冻结：Definitions: move, the eight knight moves, move 0 = (1, 2); position w j, the position after the first j steps; visited w, the squares visited, the start included; expectedRange n = Σ_w |visited w| / 8ⁿ over all 8ⁿ step sequences. The statement claim: for every n ≥ 1 there is an integer m with expectedRange n · 2^(3n−3) = m. This library proves claim. Frozen: KnightWalkRangeIntegrality.result
LEAN ✓证明的步骤（同一个冻结模块）：sym m 是把走法 0 送到走法 m 的走法置换（m 为偶数时 i ↦ m + i，奇数时 i ↦ m − i），symInv 是它的逆；latFun / lat 是与之配套的带符号坐标置换，它是加法映射，把 move i 送到 move (sym m i)。把一条游走的每一步 i 换成 sym m i，所有位置都被 lat m 映射，访问格数不变；这把第一步为走法 0 的游走一一映到第一步为走法 m 的游走，所以 S(k + 1) = 8T，E(k + 1)·2^(3k) = T。冻结：The steps of the proof (in the same frozen module): sym m is the permutation of moves sending move 0 to move m (i ↦ m + i for even m, i ↦ m − i for odd m), symInv its inverse; latFun / lat is the matching signed permutation of coordinates, an additive map sending move i to move (sym m i). Replacing every step i of a walk by sym m i maps all its positions by lat m and keeps the number of visited squares; this maps the walks starting with move 0 one-to-one onto those starting with move m, so S(k + 1) = 8T and E(k + 1)·2^(3k) = T. 一匹马站在无限大的棋盘上，每一步从它的八种走法里随便挑一种。走 n 步后它到过多少个不同的格子？对全部 8ⁿ 种走法取平均，就是 E(n)。E(n) 是分母为 2 的幂的分数，Sloane 发现乘上 2^(3n−3)——比 8ⁿ 少一个 8——就总是整数，但只是猜想。

  1. 答案。Lean 证明对每个 n ≥ 1 都是整数：所有游走访问格数的总和 S(n) 能被 8 整除。
  2. 为什么。棋盘有八个对称（四个旋转、四个翻转），它们恰好把马的八种走法互相置换，而且对每两种走法都恰有一个对称把一种变成另一种。把一条游走的每一步都换成对称后的那一步，整条路径就被旋转或翻转，访问的格子数不变。按第一步分成八类，这个替换把一类一一变成另一类，所以八类的总和相同，S(n) 是某个数的 8 倍。
  3. 三维视图。棋盘上同时画着一条游走和它的七个像，八种颜色各从一个方向出发。每条路径访问的格子数都一样。
  4. 下面的图。左图是八类各自的总和，八根柱子一样高，都等于 a(n)。右图是“访问 r 个格子的游走有多少条”，品红短线是第一类的条数乘 8——两者完全重合。

原文：问题卷宗 · KnightWalkRangeIntegrality.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · OEIS A309221 · A326954

A knight stands on an infinite chessboard and at every step picks one of its eight moves at random. How many different squares has it been on after n steps? The average over all 8ⁿ choices is E(n). E(n) is a fraction whose denominator is a power of two, and Sloane noticed that multiplying by 2^(3n−3) — one factor of 8 short of 8ⁿ — always gives an integer, but only as a conjecture.

  1. The answer. Lean proves it is an integer for every n ≥ 1: the total S(n) of visited squares over all walks is divisible by 8.
  2. Why. The board has eight symmetries (four rotations and four reflections), and they permute the eight knight moves, with exactly one symmetry taking any move to any other. Replacing every step of a walk by its symmetric counterpart rotates or reflects the whole path and keeps the number of visited squares. Sorted by their first step into eight classes, this replacement maps one class one-to-one onto another, so the eight classes have equal totals and S(n) is eight times a whole number.
  3. The 3D view. One walk and its seven images are drawn on the board at once, each colour starting in a different direction. Every path visits the same number of squares.
  4. The charts below. The left chart shows the eight class totals, eight bars of equal height a(n). The right chart counts the walks that visit r squares; the magenta ticks are eight times the count in the first class — they coincide exactly.
