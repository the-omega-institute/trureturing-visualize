理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

二维晶格上的电子穿过磁场时，每个格子围住的磁通让跳跃带上相位。磁通为 π/2（每个格子四分之一个磁通量子）时，一个元胞里有四个格点，能谱分成四条 Bloch 能带。Fabbri、Marcelli、Giuliani（2026）研究这个模型在拓扑相变处的电导，需要一条假设：质量 m 不为零时，第三、四条能带被费米能 μ 隔开。他们只给了数值证据，本库给出了证明。

  1. 模型（原文）。Bloch 哈密顿量是 4 × 4 厄米矩阵 H(k; m)，矩阵元含 e2ik₁、e2ik₂，所以动量的周期是 π；对角线上是 d = 2 + m 与 −d。四个特征值从小到大记作 λ₁ ≤ λ₂ ≤ λ₃ ≤ λ₄。在 kF = (π/4, π/4)、m = 0 处，特征多项式是 X(X + 4)(X − 2)²，所以第三、四条能带在这里相碰，费米能 μ = λ₃(kF; 0) = 2。
  2. 结论（已冻结）。对 −2 ≤ m ≤ 2、m ≠ 0，存在 δ > 0，使 λ₃(k; m) ≤ 2 − δ、λ₄(k; m) ≥ 2 + δ 对所有实动量成立；证明取 δ = m²/500。原文写的是布里渊区上的严格最小、最大值，结算卷宗说明两种写法在紧致环面上等价（这一步是论证，未形式化）。
  3. 证明的骨架（已冻结）。设 K = H − 2，yj = sin 2kj。重新排列坐标后，K 合同于 diag(S, −2I₂)，S 是一个 2 × 2 的 Schur 块，由此得到 det K = −4q，q = (y₁ − d)² + (y₂ − d)² + 2 − d²。由于 yj ≤ 1、m ≥ −2，有 q ≥ m²/2，m ≠ 0 时 q > 0。行列式为负只说明负特征值的个数是奇数；合同关系进一步给出惯性：K 恰有一个正、三个负特征值，所以 λ₃ < 2 < λ₄。最后用 Gershgorin 圆盘把每个 |λ − 2| 限制在 10 以内：|det K| ≥ 2m²，其他三个因子的乘积至多 1000，于是 |λ − 2| ≥ m²/500。
  4. 界与实际间隙（页面数值）。m²/500 是证明里的“预算”，远小于实际间隙：m = 1 时实际间隙约 0.30，界只有 0.002。卷宗另外论证了上界：m > 0 时 λ₄(kF) = 2 + m，所以间隙不超过 m。最优间隙和它在 m → 0 时的精确阶都没有确定。
  5. 蝴蝶（文献 + 页面数值）。Harper 方程在有理磁通 α = p/q 时的能谱是 q 条带，按 α 画出来就是 Hofstadter 蝴蝶；Chambers 关系把能带写成 |Pq(E)| ≤ 4 的能量集合。页面现算 q ≤ 20 的切片。m = −2（d = 0）时，本模型四条带的带边与 α = 1/4 切片一致：±2√2、±√(4 + 2√2)、±√(4 − 2√2)、0——这是页面上的数值对比，不是本库的定理。
  6. 边界。本库证明的是间隙这一条假设；原文的电导定理还需要无质量时费米点的分类、其他能带的分离、锥形渐近等条件，这些都没有在这里形式化。页面上的能带、实际间隙和蝴蝶都是现算的数值。

原文：问题卷宗：π/2 磁通 Hofstadter 模型的一致质量间隙 · HalfFluxHofstadterMassGap.lean · Fabbri、Marcelli、Giuliani，arXiv:2609.03832（2026） · Hofstadter，Phys. Rev. B 14, 2239（1976） · Harper，Proc. Phys. Soc. A 68, 874（1955） · Chambers，Phys. Rev. 140, A135（1965）

When electrons on a two-dimensional lattice move through a magnetic field, the flux through each plaquette puts a phase on every hop. At flux π/2 (a quarter of a flux quantum per plaquette) a unit cell holds four sites and the spectrum splits into four Bloch bands. Fabbri, Marcelli and Giuliani (2026) study the conductivity of this model at the topological phase transition and need one assumption: for nonzero mass m, the third and fourth bands are separated by the Fermi energy μ. They gave numerical evidence only; this library gives a proof.

  1. The model (source). The Bloch Hamiltonian is a 4 × 4 Hermitian matrix H(k; m) whose entries involve e2ik₁ and e2ik₂, so momenta have period π; its diagonal carries d = 2 + m and −d. Its four eigenvalues in increasing order are λ₁ ≤ λ₂ ≤ λ₃ ≤ λ₄. At kF = (π/4, π/4) and m = 0 the characteristic polynomial is X(X + 4)(X − 2)², so the third and fourth bands touch there and the Fermi energy is μ = λ₃(kF; 0) = 2.
  2. The result (frozen). For −2 ≤ m ≤ 2 with m ≠ 0 there is δ > 0 with λ₃(k; m) ≤ 2 − δ and λ₄(k; m) ≥ 2 + δ for every real momentum; the proof takes δ = m²/500. The source speaks of strict minima and maxima over the Brillouin zone; the settlement dossier explains that the two wordings agree on the compact torus (that step is argued, not formalized).
  3. The skeleton of the proof (frozen). Let K = H − 2 and yj = sin 2kj. After reordering coordinates, K is congruent to diag(S, −2I₂) with a 2 × 2 Schur block S, which gives det K = −4q with q = (y₁ − d)² + (y₂ − d)² + 2 − d². Since yj ≤ 1 and m ≥ −2, q ≥ m²/2, and q > 0 when m ≠ 0. A negative determinant only says that the number of negative eigenvalues is odd; the congruence supplies the inertia: K has exactly one positive and three negative eigenvalues, so λ₃ < 2 < λ₄. Finally Gershgorin discs bound every |λ − 2| by 10: |det K| ≥ 2m² and the product of the other three factors is at most 1000, so |λ − 2| ≥ m²/500.
  4. The bound and the actual gap (page numerics). m²/500 is the proof’s budget and is far below the actual gap: at m = 1 the actual gap is about 0.30 while the bound is 0.002. The dossier also argues an upper bound: for m > 0, λ₄(kF) = 2 + m, so the gap is at most m. The optimal gap and its exact order as m → 0 are not determined.
  5. The butterfly (literature + page numerics). At rational flux α = p/q, Harper’s equation has a spectrum of q bands; drawn against α it is Hofstadter’s butterfly. Chambers’ relation writes the bands as the energies with |Pq(E)| ≤ 4. The page computes the slices with q ≤ 20. At m = −2 (d = 0) the band edges of the four bands of this model coincide with those of the α = 1/4 slice: ±2√2, ±√(4 + 2√2), ±√(4 − 2√2), 0. This is a numerical comparison on the page, not a theorem of this library.
  6. Limits. This library proves the gap assumption. The source’s conductivity theorem also needs the classification of the massless Fermi points, the separation of the other bands and the conical asymptotics, none of which is formalized here. The bands, the actual gap and the butterfly on the page are computed numbers.
