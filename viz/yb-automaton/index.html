LEAN ✓R 矩阵 R(x, y) = (y + f(x + y), x − f(x + y))（rmat）；辅助值从边界 b 出发，第 i 个格子输出的第二个分量传给下一个格子（carry）；一步 step 把每个格子换成 R 的第一个分量，并把最后的辅助值作为新的边界值（螺旋边界）。猜想 claim：对每个特征为 2 的有限域 F、每个满足 f(x) + f(x + f(y)) = f(x + f(y + f(x)))（约化的杨–巴克斯特方程）的双射 f 与每个 N，step 迭代 |F| 次是恒等映射。本库证明 claim。已冻结：The R-matrix R(x, y) = (y + f(x + y), x − f(x + y)) (rmat); the auxiliary value starts at the boundary b and the second output of cell i is passed to the next cell (carry); one step replaces every cell by the first output of R and makes the last auxiliary value the new boundary (helical boundary). The conjecture, claim: for every finite field F of characteristic 2, every bijective f with f(x) + f(x + f(y)) = f(x + f(y + f(x))) (the reduced Yang–Baxter equation) and every N, iterating step |F| times is the identity. This library proves claim. Frozen: YangBaxterAutomatonPeriod.result

一排 N 个格子，每个格子里放一个 q 元域的元素（q = 2ⁿ，这里只用到加法：n 位二进制数按位异或）。一步更新时，一个辅助值从边界出发，依次穿过每个格子：在格子 i，R(x_i, y) = (y + f(x_i + y), x_i + f(x_i + y)) 把格子换成第一个分量，把第二个分量传下去；穿过最后一个格子后，它成为下一步的边界值——所以时空图是一条螺旋。f 满足约化杨–巴克斯特方程时，这是一个可积的自动机。Araoka 与 Tokihiro 猜想它的周期总整除 q，并证明了 q = 4、8。

  1. 证明。本库证明了所有 q：满足方程的双射 f 总有一个“平移对称” u ≠ 0，f(x + u) = f(x) + u。于是一步与给某些格子同时加上 u 的操作交换，自动机降到 q/2 元的商上，归纳就得到 step^q = id。
  2. 三维视图。每一圈是一个时间步，圈上依次是 N 个格子与边界值，颜色是元素的值；白色圆环标出与初态相同的时刻。下方左图是同一张时空图摊平，右图是全部初态的周期分布：只出现 q 的因子。
  3. 对照。“不满足方程的 f”一档里，R 仍是双射，自动机仍有周期，但周期是 6 与 10——都不整除 8。

A row of N cells holds elements of a field with q elements (q = 2ⁿ; only addition is used, so the elements are n-bit numbers added by XOR). In one update an auxiliary value starts at the boundary and passes through the cells in turn: at cell i, R(x_i, y) = (y + f(x_i + y), x_i + f(x_i + y)) replaces the cell by the first output and passes the second one on; after the last cell it becomes the next boundary value — so the space-time diagram is a helix. When f solves the reduced Yang–Baxter equation the automaton is integrable. Araoka and Tokihiro conjectured that its period always divides q, and proved it for q = 4 and 8.

  1. The proof. This library proves every q: a bijective solution f always has a “translation symmetry” u ≠ 0 with f(x + u) = f(x) + u. Then one step commutes with adding u to some of the cells at once, the automaton descends to a quotient with q/2 elements, and induction gives step^q = id.
  2. 3D view. Each turn is one time step, carrying the N cells and the boundary value in order, coloured by their values; white rings mark the times at which the state equals the initial one. The left chart below is the same space-time diagram laid flat, and the right chart is the distribution of periods over all initial states: only divisors of q appear.
  3. A contrast. In the “f that fails the equation” preset, R is still a bijection and the automaton is still periodic, but its periods are 6 and 10 — neither divides 8.
