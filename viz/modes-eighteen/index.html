18 矢量的 Kochen–Specker 集合有 18 个模式和 9 个语境（四个互相正交的模式组成一组完备测量），每个模式恰好属于两个语境。把 N 个全同玻色子放进去：非语境模型要给每个模式一个固定的占据数，并且每个语境里的占据数加起来都是 N。Kurzyński 指出 N = 1 不行、N = 2 可以，并问较大的 N 会怎样。

  1. 奇偶。把九个语境的和加起来，每个模式被数了两次，总和是粒子数的两倍——偶数；而它又应等于 9N。所以 N 必为偶数。Lean 证明这一点，并证明每个偶数都可以：在环 1 → 2 → 8 → 4 → 7 → 3 → 9 → 5 → 6 → 1 的九个模式上各放 N/2 个。
  2. 三维视图。九个语境恰好构成 3 × 3 的车图：同一行或同一列的两个语境共享一个模式。所以把它们画在环面上，模式是沿管或沿环的弧，粒子是弧上的点；和等于 N 的语境变绿。
  3. 缺额。右下图是 D = Σ|sᵢ − N|。每放或拿一个粒子，D 变化 0 或 ±2，奇偶不变：N 为奇数时 D 从 9N 开始，永远是奇数，降不到 0。

原文：问题卷宗 · EighteenModeBosonicAssignmentParity.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · doi:10.1103/PhysRevA.95.012133 · arXiv:1609.01250

The 18-vector Kochen–Specker set has 18 modes and 9 contexts (four mutually orthogonal modes form a complete measurement), and every mode lies in exactly two contexts. Put N identical bosons into it: a non-contextual model must give every mode a fixed occupation such that the occupations in every context add up to N. Kurzyński pointed out that N = 1 fails and N = 2 works, and asked what happens for larger N.

  1. Parity. Adding the nine context sums counts every mode twice, so the total is twice the number of particles — even; yet it should be 9N. So N must be even. Lean proves this, and proves that every even N works: put N/2 particles on each of the nine modes of the cycle 1 → 2 → 8 → 4 → 7 → 3 → 9 → 5 → 6 → 1.
  2. 3D view. The nine contexts form the 3 × 3 rook’s graph: two contexts in the same row or column share a mode. So they are drawn on a torus, with the modes as arcs along the tube or around the ring and the particles as dots on the arcs; a context whose sum equals N turns green.
  3. The defect. The lower-right chart shows D = Σ|sᵢ − N|. Adding or removing a particle changes D by 0 or ±2, so its parity never changes: for odd N, D starts at 9N, stays odd, and cannot reach 0.
