理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

n × 1 的环面就是一圈 n 个格子。起初除 c[0,0] 外每格 4 粒沙；只要有格子不少于 4 粒，就推倒它：拿走 4 粒，给四个邻居各 1 粒。在 n × 1 的环面上，上下两个邻居就是它自己，所以推倒一次留下 2 粒、左右各送 1 粒；送进 c[0,0] 的沙粒从此丢失。OEIS A293452 记录了推倒的总次数，并猜想第一列等于 A023855(n)。

  1. 下标。印出的等式在每个 n 都不成立（n = 1 时 0 ≠ 1）：数据项 0, 1, 2, 7, 10, 22, … 是 A023855 右移一位。Lean 证明的是 T(n,1) = A023855(n − 1)，n ≥ 2。
  2. 与顺序无关。证明不假设“顺序无关”，而是证出来：任何合法序列里格子 x 推倒的次数不超过 u(x)（最小作用）；以终态结束时恰好等于 u(x)（离散极大值原理）；Σ u(x) = A023855(n − 1)。
  3. 三维视图。外圈每一粒沙是一个点，琥珀色的格子不稳定，品红的弧线是下一次推倒送出的两粒沙。里圈的柱子是已推倒的次数 v(x)，琥珀短线是终点 u(x)：无论换哪种顺序，柱子最后都正好碰到短线。

原文：问题卷宗 · TorusColumnToppling.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · OEIS A293452 · OEIS A023855

The n × 1 torus is a ring of n cells. At the start every cell except c[0,0] holds 4 grains; while some cell holds at least 4, topple it: take 4 grains away and give 1 to each of its four neighbours. On the n × 1 torus the upper and lower neighbours are the cell itself, so a toppling keeps 2 grains and sends 1 to each side; grains sent to c[0,0] are lost. OEIS A293452 records the number of topplings and conjectures that the first column equals A023855(n).

  1. The index. The printed identity fails at every n (at n = 1, 0 ≠ 1): the data terms 0, 1, 2, 7, 10, 22, … are A023855 shifted by one place. Lean proves T(n,1) = A023855(n − 1) for n ≥ 2.
  2. Order independence. The proof does not assume it but derives it: in every legal sequence cell x topples at most u(x) times (least action); when the sequence ends in a final state it topples exactly u(x) times (a discrete maximum principle); and Σ u(x) = A023855(n − 1).
  3. 3D view. Each grain on the outer ring is a dot, amber cells are unstable, and the magenta arcs carry the two grains of the next toppling. The inner bars count the topplings v(x) so far and the amber ticks mark the end values u(x): whatever the order, the bars end exactly at the ticks.
