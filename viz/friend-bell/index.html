理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

一个源发出一对纠缠的量子比特，一个飞向左边的实验室，一个飞向右边的 Bob。左边实验室里有一位朋友，他先测 Z 并写下结果；实验室外的 Wigner 随后要么去问朋友（设置 0），要么先把朋友的测量相干地撤销、再测 XS⊗XF（设置 1）。Bob 在类空间隔之外选两种角度之一。两边的结果以光速送到中间汇合。三维体横轴是空间、竖轴是时间，纵深排着 400 次运行。

  1. 朋友的记录是一臂，撤销后的相干测量是另一臂（OBSERVER-QUANTUM 第 8 节，GICT 观察 6.30）。设置 0 读朋友写下的 Z；设置 1 的观测量 A1 = XS⊗XF 必然相干地触碰朋友的记忆。对共享的贝尔态，关联为 E(0, y) = cos θy，E(1, y) = cos φ cos θy + v sin φ sin θy，CHSH 值 S = E₀₀ + E₀₁ + E₁₀ − E₁₁ 在默认角度下为 2√2。
  2. 三者不可兼得。若朋友的结果在每一次运行里都是绝对事实（包括被撤销的那些），再加上设置自由与无超距，每次运行就对应一张写满 A0, A1, B0, B1 的答案表，而任何答案表及其混合都满足 |S| ≤ 2——这一条已在 trureturing 中冻结：classical_chsh_bound_is_exact。量子账本给出 2√2 > 2，所以三者不能同时成立。理论卷的结论是放弃第一条：已观测事件的绝对性从未被授予，事实只相对于记录它的账本；跨越未共享记录合并事实，不是允许的推理规则。理论卷同时写明，这是拒绝 no-go 的一个前提，其他解释可以拒绝别的前提。这一节是理论卷的推导与数值证书，不是 Lean 定理。
  3. Tsirelson 上界。对页面默认的那组观测量（A0 = Z，A1 = X，B0,1 = (Z ± X)/√2），任何两比特态的 CHSH 期望都不超过 2√2，贝尔态恰好取到：bell_chsh_state_expectation_is_greatest。Landau 恒等式 C² = 4I − [A₀,A₁]⊗[B₀,B₁] 也已冻结：landau_identity。对任意角度的一般 Tsirelson 上界是已知结果（Cirel’son 1980），页面把禁区画在 2√2 以外，但不把它说成已冻结。
  4. 撤销的代价。朋友的结果被写进 M 份副本（记忆、笔记、实验室环境……）。页面假设每份副本以保真度 f 被反演，撤销后剩下的相干可见度 v = fM：这是示意假设，不是定理。可见度把 X 部分的关联乘上 v，最优角度下的最大值为 Smax = 2√(1 + v²)。量子比特朋友 v = 1，达到 2√2；真人朋友的副本数是天文数字，v → 0，Smax 只剩 2，违背消失——朋友的结果在实际上表现得像绝对事实。不撤销直接测，等同于 v = 0。
  5. 谁先测取决于参考系。Wigner 与 Bob 的测量是类空间隔的：在实验室系里 Bob 先测，换到 β < −0.117 的参考系里 Wigner 先测，而每组设置的关联完全不变。“此刻”切片是 t′ = γ(t − βx) 等于常数的面，在实验室坐标里是倾斜的。两份结果以光速送到中间汇合，汇合事件在两次测量的未来光锥里，在任何参考系中都排在两次测量之后：关联只在那里才成为某一本账上的事实。
  6. LINEAR 与 BLOCK。LINEAR 只显示当前参考系中切片以前的事件，账本读数按切片统计：朋友账本里被撤销的记录划掉，档案估计的 S 要等汇合事件进入切片才有。BLOCK 把整块时空一起显示。换参考系、换坐标、换条件化方式都不改档案指纹；改角度、撤销或答案表才会改变结果。
  7. 模型边界。纠缠态、测量和撤销都是理想化的两比特模型，撤销可见度 fM 是示意假设，时空几何只用到 1+1 维洛伦兹变换。页面用固定种子抽样，每组设置约 100 次运行，档案估计带有统计涨落。它不是某个真实实验的数值模拟。

A source emits an entangled pair of qubits, one flying to the lab on the left and one to Bob on the right. Inside the left lab a friend measures Z first and writes the result down; Wigner, outside the lab, then either asks the friend (setting 0) or first coherently undoes the friend’s measurement and then measures XS⊗XF (setting 1). Bob, spacelike separated, picks one of two angles. Both results travel to the middle at the speed of light. The 3D block shows space across and time up, with 400 runs lined up in depth.

  1. The friend’s record is one arm; the coherent measurement after the undo is the other (OBSERVER-QUANTUM section 8, GICT Observation 6.30, both in Chinese). Setting 0 reads the Z the friend wrote down; the setting-1 observable A1 = XS⊗XF necessarily touches the friend’s memory coherently. For the shared Bell state the correlations are E(0, y) = cos θy and E(1, y) = cos φ cos θy + v sin φ sin θy, and the CHSH value S = E₀₀ + E₀₁ + E₁₀ − E₁₁ is 2√2 at the default angles.
  2. The three cannot all hold. If the friend’s result were an absolute fact in every run (including the undone ones), then together with free settings and no superluminal influence each run would carry one answer table filled with A0, A1, B0, B1, and every answer table and every mixture of them satisfies |S| ≤ 2. That bound is frozen in trureturing: classical_chsh_bound_is_exact. The quantum ledgers give 2√2 > 2, so the three cannot hold together. The theory volume gives up the first: the absoluteness of observed events is never granted, facts are relative to the ledger that records them, and merging facts across unshared records is not an allowed inference. The volume also says this rejects one premise of the no-go theorem and that other interpretations may reject another. This part is a derivation plus numerical certificates in the theory volumes, not a Lean theorem.
  3. Tsirelson’s bound. For the page’s default observables (A0 = Z, A1 = X, B0,1 = (Z ± X)/√2), no two-qubit state has a CHSH expectation above 2√2, and the Bell state attains it: bell_chsh_state_expectation_is_greatest. Landau’s identity C² = 4I − [A₀,A₁]⊗[B₀,B₁] is frozen too: landau_identity. The general Tsirelson bound for arbitrary angles is a known result (Cirel’son 1980); the page draws the forbidden zone beyond 2√2 but does not call that general bound frozen.
  4. The price of the undo. The friend’s result is written into M copies (memory, notes, the lab’s surroundings …). The page assumes each copy is reversed with fidelity f, leaving a coherence visibility v = fM after the undo; this is a schematic assumption, not a theorem. The visibility multiplies the X part of the correlations, and at the best angles the maximum is Smax = 2√(1 + v²). A qubit friend has v = 1 and reaches 2√2; a human friend has an astronomical number of copies, v → 0, Smax drops to 2 and the violation disappears, so in practice the friend’s result behaves like an absolute fact. Measuring without the undo is the same as v = 0.
  5. Who measures first depends on the frame. Wigner’s and Bob’s measurements are spacelike separated: in the lab frame Bob measures first, in a frame with β < −0.117 Wigner does, and the correlation for every setting pair stays exactly the same. The “now” slice is the surface t′ = γ(t − βx) = constant, tilted in lab coordinates. Both results travel to the middle at light speed; the meeting event lies in the future light cone of both measurements and comes after both in every frame, and only there does the correlation become a fact in anyone’s ledger.
  6. LINEAR and BLOCK. LINEAR shows only the events before the slice in the chosen frame, and the ledger readouts count by that slice: undone records are struck out of the friend’s ledger, and the archive estimate of S appears only once the meeting event is inside the slice. BLOCK shows the whole block at once. Changing the frame, the coordinates or the conditioning leaves the archive fingerprint unchanged; only the angles, the undo or the answer table change the results.
  7. Model limits. The entangled state, the measurements and the undo form an idealised two-qubit model, the undo visibility fM is a schematic assumption, and the geometry uses only 1+1-dimensional Lorentz transformations. The page samples with fixed seeds, about 100 runs per setting pair, so the archive estimate fluctuates. It is not a numerical simulation of a real experiment.
