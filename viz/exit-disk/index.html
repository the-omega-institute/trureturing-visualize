LEAN ✓定义：四个方向的单位步 step；方向词给出的位置 position；exits S p w：词的每个中间位置都在 S 里、最后一个位置不在；exitProb S p n：长度为 n 的 4ⁿ 个方向词里恰在第 n 步第一次离开的比例；expectedExit S p = Σ n · exitProb S p n；meanExit S 是它在 S 上的平均；connected S：S 里任意两点能用 S 内的步连起来；square m = {0, …, m − 1}²。猜想 claim：对每个 m ≥ 1，每个连通、恰有 m² 个格子的 S 都满足 meanExit S ≤ meanExit (square m)。本库反驳 claim。已冻结：Definitions: the four unit steps step; the positions along a direction word, position; exits S p w: every intermediate position of the word lies in S and the last one does not; exitProb S p n: the fraction of the 4ⁿ direction words of length n that leave for the first time at step n; expectedExit S p = Σ n · exitProb S p n; meanExit S, its average over S; connected S: any two points of S are joined by steps inside S; square m = {0, …, m − 1}². The statement claim: for every m ≥ 1, every connected S with exactly m² cells satisfies meanExit S ≤ meanExit (square m). This library refutes claim. Frozen: SquareClusterExitTimeRefutation.result

理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

在方格纸上涂出一块连通的格子。在其中随便选一格放一个行人，让它每一步往上下左右之一随机走一格，数它第一次走出这块格子用了几步；对所有起点取平均，就是平均出逃时间 τ。Rognone 与 Nicosia 把 τ 当作团簇“紧不紧凑”的指标，并猜想格子数相同时正方形最难走出。

  1. 反例。Lean 给出了精确值：121 格的圆盘 x² + y² ≤ 37 要走 23.7466… 步，11 × 11 正方形只要 23.5568… 步。证明用整数证书：每个格子的期望步数 t 满足 t = 1 + 四个邻居 t 的平均（外面算 0）；证书满足这个方程，又夹在 1 和 43 之间，Lean 由此证明它就是期望步数。
  2. 穹顶。三维视图里每根柱子的高度就是 t：中心最高，边上最低。正方形的四个角很低——从角上出发，两个方向一步就能出去。圆盘没有角，同样多的格子堆得更高，平均高度 τ 更大。
  3. 生存曲线。下方右图是 k 步后还在里面的行人比例 S(k)。τ 恰好等于这条曲线下的面积，所以哪条曲线衰减得慢，哪个形状的 τ 就大。白点行人的走出过程是对这条曲线的一次随机抽样。
  4. 自己试。在下方左图里拿起一格、放到别处，或按「爬一步」让页面找最好的单格移动。从正方形出发，任何一次单格移动都让 τ 变小；但连走两步——把两个上角都搬到底边中间——就已经超过正方形（页面数值）。

原文：问题卷宗 · SquareClusterExitTimeRefutation.lean · Blueprint · 文献笔记 · doi:10.1088/2632-072X/aeb36a · arXiv:2308.07880

Shade a connected patch of cells on squared paper. Put a walker on a random cell, let it step up, down, left or right at random, and count the steps until it first stands outside the patch; the average over all starting cells is the mean exit time τ. Rognone and Nicosia use τ as a measure of how compact a cluster is, and conjecture that for a given number of cells the square is the hardest to leave.

  1. The counterexample. Lean gives exact values: the 121-cell disk x² + y² ≤ 37 needs 23.7466… steps, the 11 × 11 square only 23.5568…. The proof uses integer certificates: the expected number of steps t from each cell satisfies t = 1 + the average of t over the four neighbours (0 outside); the certificates satisfy this equation and lie between 1 and 43, and from this Lean proves that they are the expected numbers of steps.
  2. The domes. In the 3D view each column rises to t: highest in the middle, lowest at the edge. The four corners of the square sit low — from a corner, two directions lead out in one step. The disk has no corners, so the same number of cells piles up higher and the mean height τ is larger.
  3. The survival curve. The lower-right chart shows the fraction S(k) of walkers still inside after k steps. τ is exactly the area under this curve, so the shape whose curve decays more slowly has the larger τ. The white walkers leaving the domes are one random sample of this curve.
  4. Try it. In the lower-left map, pick up a cell and put it somewhere else, or press “Climb one step” to let the page find the best single-cell move. From the square every single move lowers τ; but two moves in a row — both top corners to the middle of the bottom edge — already beat it (page numbers).
