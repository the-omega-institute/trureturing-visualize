理论接口 · 这个页面在演示什么Theory interface · what this page shows

Miura-ori 是一种由平行四边形组成的折纸。Gupta（2026）把 m × n Miura-ori 的每种合法折法写成网格上的一个高度函数：相邻格的高度恰好相差 1。一个格子若比所有邻居都低（严格局部极小）或都高（严格局部极大），就可以把它的高度改变 2，对应翻转图的一条边。本库证明了他提出的两个问题：一个关于翻转图的直径，一个关于度数的计数多项式。

  1. 高度函数与翻转（原文）。高度函数 h 在每条网格边上满足 |h(u) − h(v)| = 1，h mod 3 是网格的一个真 3-着色。严格局部极小的所有邻居都比它高 1，翻转把它升高 2；严格局部极大反之。一个状态在翻转图里的度数就是它的严格局部极值个数 d = a + b。
  2. 离散度不等式（已冻结）。对网格上任何整数 1-Lipschitz 函数 φ，disp(φ) = minK Σ|φ − K| ≤ D(m, n)，D(m, n) 是斜坡 i + j 的离散度。这里不要求 φ 来自折纸，相邻差可以是 0。斜坡本身取到等号（卷宗论证）。
  3. 证明的重排（冻结证明的私有引理）。把每一行排序、再把每一列排序：值的多重集不变，所以离散度不变；相邻行逐位比较的关系在排序后仍然成立，单位台阶也不会被拉开，得到行列都递增的 F。F 与 A − F（A = i + j）都是递增函数，FKG 不等式给出 Cov(sgn(F − EF), A − F) ≥ 0，由此 F 的平均绝对偏差不超过 A 的；最后说明整数中心不比有理中心差，A 的最优中心在 (m + n + 2)/2。
  4. 直径（卷宗论证 + 页面数值）。原文的定理 4.1 给出直径 ≥ D(m, n)，命题 4.2 给出直径 ≤ 最大离散度；加上不等式 (4)，直径恰为 D(m, n)。页面在状态数不超过 3000 的网格上用广度优先搜索核对：直径等于 D(m, n)，任意两个状态的翻转距离等于 disp((h₁ − h₂)/2)。最长的一条路从 −A 走到 A，页面一步一步画出来，每一步都翻转一个让距离减 1 的极值。
  5. 度数的计数多项式（已冻结）。固定 a 个严格极小、b 个严格极大（d = a + b ≥ 5，a ≤ b），只数“极小值不全在同一条边上”的高度函数，计数在 m, n ≥ 2d − 1 时是一个总次数 ≤ d − 3 的多项式。证明的关键：把函数沿“冻结”的整列、整行收缩到唯一的核，计数分解成按核求和，每个核贡献正整数组合数的乘积；两个方向冻结段数的联合上界 r + s ≤ d − 1 把次数压到 d − 3。
  6. 边界。原文的折纸对应、定理 4.1 与命题 4.2、翻转图本身都没有在本库形式化；页面的翻转图、直径、翻转距离与穷举都是现算的数值。原文的猜想 4.6 与非矩形折纸的问题仍然开放。

原文：问题卷宗：Miura 网格的离散度不等式 (4) · 问题卷宗：剩余项次数猜想 8.3 · GuptaMiuraGridDispersion.lean · MiuraResolution.lean · Gupta，arXiv:2606.22614（2026）、arXiv:2607.05567（2026） · Christensen 等，arXiv:2506.19700（2025） · Fortuin、Kasteleyn、Ginibre，Comm. Math. Phys. 22, 89（1971）

The Miura-ori is a fold pattern made of parallelograms. Gupta (2026) writes each valid folding of the m × n Miura-ori as a height function on the grid: neighbouring cells differ in height by exactly 1. A cell lower than all its neighbours (a strict local minimum) or higher than all of them (a strict local maximum) can change its height by 2, which is one edge of the flip graph. This library proves two of his questions, one on the diameter of the flip graph and one on the counting polynomials of its degrees.

  1. Height functions and flips (source). A height function h satisfies |h(u) − h(v)| = 1 on every grid edge, and h mod 3 is a proper 3-colouring of the grid. All neighbours of a strict local minimum are higher by 1, and a flip raises it by 2; a strict local maximum works the other way. The degree of a state in the flip graph is its number of strict local extrema, d = a + b.
  2. The dispersion inequality (frozen). Every integer 1-Lipschitz function φ on the grid has disp(φ) = minK Σ|φ − K| ≤ D(m, n), where D(m, n) is the dispersion of the ramp i + j. No origami realisability is assumed, and neighbouring differences may be 0. The ramp itself attains equality (argued in the dossier).
  3. The proof’s rearrangement (private lemmas of the frozen proof). Sort every row and then every column. The multiset of values is unchanged, so the dispersion is unchanged; the entrywise comparison between neighbouring rows survives sorting, and no unit step is stretched, giving an F increasing along rows and columns. Both F and A − F (A = i + j) are increasing, so the FKG inequality gives Cov(sgn(F − EF), A − F) ≥ 0, and hence the mean absolute deviation of F is at most that of A. Finally an integer centre is no worse than a rational one, and the best centre of A is (m + n + 2)/2.
  4. The diameter (argued in the dossier + page numerics). The source’s Theorem 4.1 gives diameter ≥ D(m, n) and its Proposition 4.2 gives diameter ≤ the largest dispersion; with inequality (4) the diameter is exactly D(m, n). The page checks this by breadth-first search on grids with at most 3000 states: the diameter equals D(m, n), and any two states are at flip distance disp((h₁ − h₂)/2). A longest path runs from −A to A, and the page draws it step by step, each step flipping an extremum that lowers the distance by 1.
  5. The counting polynomials of degrees (frozen). Fix a strict minima and b strict maxima (d = a + b ≥ 5, a ≤ b) and count only the height functions whose minima are not all on one side; for m, n ≥ 2d − 1 the count is a polynomial of total degree ≤ d − 3. The key steps: contract the function along whole “frozen” columns and rows to a unique core, split the count into a sum over cores where each core contributes a product of counts of positive compositions, and lower the degree to d − 3 with the joint bound r + s ≤ d − 1 on the frozen runs in both directions.
  6. Limits. The source’s origami correspondence, its Theorem 4.1 and Proposition 4.2 and the flip graph itself are not formalized here; the page’s flip graph, diameters, flip distances and enumerations are computed numbers. The source’s Conjecture 4.6 and the non-rectangular questions remain open.
